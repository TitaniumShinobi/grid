[Los Santos]
The city of Davis is located within Los Santos.

[Downtown Los Santos]
Downtown is the financial center of Los Santos and its most urbanized area, with the presence of the tallest high-rises in the city, including the huge Maze Bank Tower, the FIB and IAA buildings, the Union Depository and the Mile High Club, still under construction. The district is divided into the neighborhoods of Pillbox Hill, Mission Row, Textile City and Legion Square.

[Rockford Hills]
Rockford Hills contains the smaller neighbourhood of Burton in the southeast.

[Vespucci]
Vespucci is split into the neighborhoods of Vespucci Beach and Vespucci Canals.
