[[LEAD]]
'''Sustancia Rd''' is a street in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] and both [[Palomino Highlands]] and the [[Tataviam Mountains]] in [[Los Santos County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road is a continuation of [[Innocence Blvd]], starting at the junction with [[El Rancho Blvd]] and has connections to [[Fudge Ln]], [[El Burro Blvd]] and an unnamed road leading to the [[NOOSE]] [[NOOSE Headquarters|headquarters]] before ending with connections to the [[Palomino Fwy]].
