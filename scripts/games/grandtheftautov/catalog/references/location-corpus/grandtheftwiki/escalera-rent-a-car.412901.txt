[[LEAD]]
'''Escalera Rent-A-Car''' is a car rental company located on [[Greenwich Pkwy]] in [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company rents out cars from the following car manufacturers: [[Albany]], [[Benefactor]], [[BF]], [[Brute]], [[Declasse]], [[Dundreary]], [[Emperor (manufacturer)|Emperor]] (misspelled as ''Emporer''), [[Enus]], [[Fathom]], [[Imponte]], [[Obey]], [[Ubermacht]] and [[Vapid]]. A [[Rental Shuttle Bus]] displaying the company logo can be occasionally be seen driving around the airport.
