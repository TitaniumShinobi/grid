'''Tequi-La-La''' is a bar/nightclub in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The bar can be purchased by any protagonist for $2,000,000 with a weekly return of $16,500. The bar is based on [[wp:Whisky A Go Go|Whisky A Go Go]]; however, the Tequi-La-La building is painted yellow on the second story and black at street level, while the real-life bar is painted almost entirely red.

While collecting Vinewood "souvenirs" for [[Nigel]] and [[Mrs. Thornhill]], [[Trevor Philips]] encounters Willie, the bassist for the hair metal band [[Love Fist]], at the bar, [[Vinewood Souvenirs - Willie|beating him up for his tooth]].
