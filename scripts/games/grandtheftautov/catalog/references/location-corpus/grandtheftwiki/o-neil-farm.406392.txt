[[LEAD]]
The '''O'Neil Farm''' is a farm located in [[Grapeseed]] in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]]. It is the home of the [[O'Neil Brothers]] as well as their base of operations for their methamphetamine lab. The farm was destroyed by [[Trevor Philips]] as revenge for stealing a potential contract with the Triads.
