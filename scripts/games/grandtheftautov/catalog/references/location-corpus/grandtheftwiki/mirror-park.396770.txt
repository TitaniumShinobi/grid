'''Mirror Park''' is a middle class residential neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The neighbourhood is bordered by [[East Vinewood]] in the north, the [[Tataviam Mountains]] in the east and [[La Mesa]] in the west. The neighbourhood has a number of houses surrounding a park and has a limited number of businesses. Some of the houses in the neighbourhood are up for rent by [[Wolfs International Realty]] with more houses being.
