'''Jefferson''' is a district slightly to the northeast of the city center of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]], modeled after the residential district of [[:wp:Watts, Los Angeles, California|Watts]] in [[wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]]. It is a stronghold for the [[Ballas]], [[Grove Street Families]] and [[Los Santos Vagos]] during part of 1992, with the exception of the [[County General Hospital]], as it is used by all Los Santos-based gangs. The area is more of a Poor district, with numerous one-story houses and a few businesses. There are no known residents in Jefferson, although Carl Johnson can purchase a [[Jefferson Safehouse|home]] in Jefferson. North of Jefferson is [[Las Colinas]], while to the south are [[Idlewood]] and [[Ganton]]. West of Jefferson is [[Glen Park]], while to the east is [[East Los Santos in GTA III Era|East Los Santos]].
