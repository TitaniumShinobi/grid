The '''Senora Freeway''' ([[Route 1]] and [[Route 13]]) is a freeway that runs through the [[Los Santos County]] in [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]]. Its southern end is at the meeting of the two freeways, the [[Los Santos Freeway (GTA V)|Los Santos Freeway]] and the [[Palomino Freeway]] in the northeastern part of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. It travels north until reaching [[Paleto Cove]], where it becomes the [[Great Ocean Highway]].
