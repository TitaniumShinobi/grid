The '''Great Chaparral''' is a rural and hilly area of [[Los Santos County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Great Chaparral is bordered by the [[Grand Senora Desert]] in the north, [[Tongva Hills]] in the west and [[Vinewood Hills]] in the south.
