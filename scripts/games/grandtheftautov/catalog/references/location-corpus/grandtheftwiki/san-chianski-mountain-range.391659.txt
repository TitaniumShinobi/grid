The '''San Chianski Mountain Range''' is a mountain range stretching from [[Mount Gordo]] to the [[RON Alternates Wind Farm]] in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The range is bordered by the [[Grand Senora Desert]] in the west and the [[Pacific Ocean]] in the east. The [[Humane Labs and Research]] facility and the [[Davis Quartz]] quarry are located within the range.
