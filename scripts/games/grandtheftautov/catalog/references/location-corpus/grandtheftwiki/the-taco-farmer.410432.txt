'''The Taco Farmer''' is a fast food restaurant located at the corner of [[Carson Ave]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. When switching to [[Franklin Clinton]], the player may find Franklin walking away from the business.
