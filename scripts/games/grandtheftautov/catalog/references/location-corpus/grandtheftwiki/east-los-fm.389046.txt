'''East Los FM''' is a radio station in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] featuring various kinds of Latin American music, including pop, rock, rap, traditional and electronic music. The featured artists also come from various countries in Central and South America, though a majority originate from Mexico.

'''DJs''': [[Don Cheto]] & [[Camilo Lara]]
