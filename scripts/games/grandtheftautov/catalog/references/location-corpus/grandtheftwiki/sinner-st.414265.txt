[[LEAD]]
'''Sinner St''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running from [[Mission Row]] in the south to [[Textile City]] in the north. The street has connections to [[Adam's Apple Blvd]], [[Atlee St]], [[Integrity Way]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[Sinners Passage]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]].
