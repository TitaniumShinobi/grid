'''Chantelle's Beauty Salon''' is a beauty salon located on [[Carson Ave]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. During the mission [[Where Credit's Due]] in GTA Online, the [[Protagonist (GTA Online)|protagonist(s)]] repossess a pink [[Zion]] for [[Simeon Yetarian]] from the beauty salon.
