[[LEAD]]
'''Vinylism''', also known as '''Vinylism Record Store''', was a store located on [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store presumably shut down shortly before the events of GTA V and GTA Online as the store front remains although a ''Now Leasing'' sign is next to the company sign.
