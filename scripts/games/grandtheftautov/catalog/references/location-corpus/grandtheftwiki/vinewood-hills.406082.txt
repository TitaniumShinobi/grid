'''Vinewood Hills''' is a neighbourhood and mountain range in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The area contains many large houses owned by the rich and celebrities who pay [[Money|$]]1,000 per square foot of land. The [[Galileo Observatory]] and the [[Vinewood Sign]] are both found within the neighbourhood. It is based of the affluent Los Angeles neighbourhood of Hollywood Hills and Hollywood Hills West.
