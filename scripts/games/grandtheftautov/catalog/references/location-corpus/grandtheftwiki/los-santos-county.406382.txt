'''Los Santos County''' is a county located within [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The county includes the cities of [[Davis]] and [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] along with [[Chumash]], a small beachside town. The north of the county is a rural area with places such as the [[Great Chaparral]] allowing for outdoor activities. The county is bordered by [[Blaine County]] to the north and the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the east, south and west. The county is served by the [[Los Santos County Sheriff]] police department. It is the most populous county in San Andreas.
