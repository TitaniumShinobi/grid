[[LEAD]]
'''Suds Law Laundromat''' is a coin-operated Laundromat located at the corner of [[Macdonald St]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company may be related to [[Suds]], a Laundromat located further north on Strawberry Ave. The company [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] is ''555-0142'', which results in the caller being asked to check the company website for hours and availability.
