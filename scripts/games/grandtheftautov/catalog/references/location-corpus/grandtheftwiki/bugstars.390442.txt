[[LEAD]]
'''Bugstars''' is a pest control business in [[Grand Theft Auto V]].

Bugstar has two slogans. The first, "''Putting the FU in fumigate''", makes reference to [[wp:Fumigation|fumigation]] and the term "''fuck you''". The second, "''Bug'R'Us''", makes reference to toystore [[wp:Toys "R" Us|Toys Я Us]] whilst also sounding like "''[[wp:bugger|bugger]] us''". Their business number is 1-999-BUGSTAR. The name of the company may be a reference to the GTA clone "Gangstars". The uniform of the company resembles those used by the [[wp:Ghostbusters|Ghostbusters]] and also those used by the fictional bug extermination company in [[wp:King of the Hill|King of the Hill]] called "Dale's Dead Bug".
