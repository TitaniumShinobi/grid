[[LEAD]]
'''Saint Mark's''' is a district in northeastern [[Portland]], [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City]] featured in [[Grand Theft Auto III]], [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]], [[Grand Theft Auto Advance]] and [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]]. It borders [[Harwood]] to the north, [[Portland View]] to the south, [[Portland Beach]] to the east, and [[Hepburn Heights]] and the [[Red Light District]] to the west.

[[SECTION:2:Places of interest]]
[[File:Salvatore'sGentlemen'sClub-GTAIII-exterior.jpg|thumb|Salvatore's Gentlemen Club in GTA III.]]
Don [[Salvatore Leone]] has his [[Salvatore's Gentlemen's Club|mansion]] - the main Leone base of operations - on the north-eastern tip of the Saint Mark's district.

Other Leone fronts in the Saint Marks district include [[Momma's Restaurante]]; some believe that [[Marco's Bistro]] is controlled by the Leones, seeing as how in GTA Liberty City Stories there is a [[Leone Sentinel]] in the parking lot.

Also, in GTA Liberty City Stories, there is an apartment in this district that serves as the first [[Portland Safehouse|safehouse]] for [[Toni Cipriani]], given by [[Vincenzo Cilli]].
