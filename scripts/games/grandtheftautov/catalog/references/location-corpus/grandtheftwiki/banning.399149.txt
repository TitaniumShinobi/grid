'''Banning''' is a small industrial neighbourhood in the south of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Banning is bordered by [[Davis]] to the north, northeast and northwest, [[Rancho]] to the east, [[Elysian Island]] to the south and the [[Port of Los Santos|Port of South Los Santos]] to the west.
