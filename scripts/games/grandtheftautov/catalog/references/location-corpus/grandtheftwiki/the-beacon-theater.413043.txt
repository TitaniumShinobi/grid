[[LEAD]]
'''The Beacon Theatre''' is a movie theatre located on [[Davis Ave]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. In late [[2013]], the theatre is showing two films, [[Bitch in the Trunk]] and [[Whose Placenta?]], which can not be viewed by the player.
