[[LEAD]]
'''Vespucchill''' is a business with stores on the boardwalk of [[Vespucci Beach]] and on [[Magellan Ave]] in [[Vespucci]], both in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store at the boardwalk has the appearance of a beach and souvenir shop while the Magellan Ave store is listed as a ''smoke shop''. The sign for both stores, however, suggests that is a store to purchase food and drink.
