[[LEAD]]
The '''Mile High Club''' is a skyscraper under construction in Downtown [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. Commercial and business premises are being offered and the building's slogan is ''High Rise - Low Rates''. It is being constructed by a company called [[STD Contractors]]. The building is one the most dominating features of the Los Santos skyline; only the [[Maze Bank Tower]] is taller than the Mile High Club.
