'''Humane Labs and Research''' is a chemical research and manufacturing facility located in the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The facility works with the [[Central Intelligence Agency#HD Universe|IAA]] to create biological weapons and entering the facility results in the player obtaining a [[Wanted Level in GTA V|four-star wanted level]]. [[Merryweather Security Consulting]] have also guarded shipments to the facility in the past. The researchers test their products on [[Animals in GTA V|animals]] with monkeys being seen in cages inside the facility. Chemical shipments are taken, by van, from the facility to [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]] twice a day. In the media, the facility is reported to be developing cheap perfumes.
