[[LEAD]]
'''Picture Perfect Drive''' is a road in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road starts at [[Milton Dr]] in [[Vinewood Hills]] before going through [[Rockford Hills]] and [[GWC and Golfing Society]] before ending at [[North Rockford Dr]] in [[Richman in GTA V|Richman]]. There are also connections to [[Americano Way]], [[Cockingend Dr]], [[Dunstable Dr]], [[Greenwich Pl]], [[Handy Way]], [[Hillcrest Ridge Access Rd]], [[Mad Wayne Thunder Dr]], [[Richman St]] and [[South Mo Milton Dr]].
