'''Jamaica''', also known as '''Xaymaca''' by the [[:wp:Taino people|Taino]] (Jamaica's indigenous population) is an island country located in the Caribbean Sea. Jamaica gained its independence from the [[United Kingdom]] on August 6, [[1962]] although the country's head of state remains the British monarch, [[:wp:Elizabeth II|Elizabeth II]]. The capital of Jamaica is Kingston.
