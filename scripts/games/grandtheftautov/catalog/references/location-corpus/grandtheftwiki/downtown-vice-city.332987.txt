[[LEAD]]
'''Downtown''' is the business sector of [[Vice City in GTA III Era|Vice City]] in [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City]] and [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories]]. It is bordered by [[Little Haiti]] to the south and a body of water elsewhere.

[[SECTION:3:Empire buildings controlled]]
*(6)

[[SECTION:2:Weapon locations]]
*[[Uzi]] - Found underneath the staircase leading to the Skumole Shack.
*[[Molotov Cocktail]] - Found underneath the [[Tacopalypse]] canopy.

[[SECTION:2:Rampage locations]]
;GTA Vice City
*M60 Rampage: Located in the northernmost corner of the [[Moist Palms Hotel]].
*Flamethrower Rampage: Located next to a staircase leading to a helipad on the north side of the [[Hyman Memorial Stadium]].
*Molotov Rampage:  Located at the top of a staircase of a building near the VCN building.
*Minigun Rampage: Located in front of a door on the ground floor of the [[VAJ Finance]] building.
