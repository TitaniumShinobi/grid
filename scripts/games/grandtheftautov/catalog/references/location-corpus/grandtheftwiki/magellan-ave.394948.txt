'''Magellan Ave''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running northwest in [[Del Perro]], as an exit from the [[Del Perro Freeway]], to southeast in [[La Puerta]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue has conncetions to [[Aguja St]], [[Bay City Ave]], [[Conquistador St]], [[Cortes St]], the [[Del Perro Freeway]], [[Goma St]], [[Melanoma St]], [[Palomino Ave]], [[Red Desert Ave]], [[Sandcastle Way]], [[Vespucci Blvd]] and [[Vitus St]]. The avenue runs through [[Vespucci]], [[Vespucci Beach]],
