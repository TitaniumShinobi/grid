[[LEAD]]
'''Vespucci Beach''' is a [[:Category:Beaches|beach]] in [[Vespucci]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The [[Marabunta Grande]] control the area but the [[Los Santos Vagos in GTA V|Los Santos Vagos]] also appear during the mission [[Repossession]] but can not be found before or after the mission.
