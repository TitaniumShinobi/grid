'''Mount Josiah''' is a mountain in central [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]], located directly north of [[Fort Zancudo]] and west of the [[Alamo Sea]]. Mount Josiah is the second tallest peak in the county after [[Mount Chiliad in GTA V|Mount Chiliad]] and is taller than [[Mount Gordo]].
