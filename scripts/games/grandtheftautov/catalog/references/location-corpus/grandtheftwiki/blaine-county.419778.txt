'''Blaine County''' also known as the '''Morning Glory County''' is a large desert and forest area in the northern half of [[San Andreas in GTA V|Southern San Andreas]] founded in [[1823]]. It is north of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] and [[Los Santos County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Blaine County contains a number of farms and methamphetamine labs, resulting in a large portion of the population being farmers, farm hands or drug addicts.
