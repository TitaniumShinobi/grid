[[LEAD]]
'''Merryweather Security Consulting''' is a civilian security firm and private military contractor in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. In GTA V, the company serves as a major antagonistic faction due to its ownership by [[Devin Weston]], one of the two main [[antagonist]]s.
