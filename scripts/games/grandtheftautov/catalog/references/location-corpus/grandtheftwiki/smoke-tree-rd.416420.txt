'''Smoke Tree Rd''' is a road in the [[Grand Senora Desert]] in [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are connections to [[Cat-Claw Ave]], [[Cholla Rd]], [[Nowhere Rd]] and [[Panorama Dr]].
