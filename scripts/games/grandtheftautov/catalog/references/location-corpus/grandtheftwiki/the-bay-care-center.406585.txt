[[LEAD]]
'''The Bay Care Center''' is a hospital, incorporating the Paleto Bay Funeral Home and Crematorium, located on [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The player will respawn at the care center when it is the closest hospital after being [[wasted]].
