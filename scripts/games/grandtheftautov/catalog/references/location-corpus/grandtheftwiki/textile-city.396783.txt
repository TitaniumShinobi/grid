[[LEAD]]
'''Textile City''' is an industrial and commercial neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Almost all the businesses and factories in the area are related to clothing. [[Simmet Alley]] is said to be very famous and contains a number of businesses with local residents and tourists shopping throughout the day.
