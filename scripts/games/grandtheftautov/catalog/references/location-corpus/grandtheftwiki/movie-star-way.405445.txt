'''Movie Star Way''' is a northwest to southeast street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street begins in [[Rockford Hills]] in the northwest before travelling southeast to [[Little Seoul]]. The street has connections to [[Heritage Way]], [[Marathon Ave]], [[San Andreas Ave]] and [[South Boulevard Del Perro]].
