'''South Boulevard Del Perro''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The boulevard is a continuation of [[Abe Milton Pkwy]] and runs parallel with [[Boulevard Del Perro]], which it eventually merges with. The bouelvard is almost entirely in [[Rockford Hills]] with only its eastern most tip being part of [[Burton]]. There are connections to [[Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Mad Wayne Thunder Dr]], [[Milton Rd]], [[Movie Star Way]], [[Portola Dr]] and [[Rockford Dr]].
