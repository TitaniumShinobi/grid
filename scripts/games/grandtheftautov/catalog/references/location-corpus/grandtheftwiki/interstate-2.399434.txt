[[LEAD]]
'''Interstate 2''', also known as '''I-2''' and most of the '''[[Del Perro Freeway]]''' is a freeway that runs through [[Downtown Los Santos in GTA V|Downtown Los Santos]] heading east. Its western terminus is at a merging off the [[Great Ocean Highway]] ([[Route 1]]) in Del Perro and goes east throughout the northern-central section of Los Santos before it later turns southwards and ends at the intersection of the [[Interstate 4]]/[[Route 15]], where it continues as [[Route 20]].

The highway never serves less than six lanes in total and is one of the largest highways in the GTA series by max lanes. The highway is eight lanes without lane merges at its greatest.
