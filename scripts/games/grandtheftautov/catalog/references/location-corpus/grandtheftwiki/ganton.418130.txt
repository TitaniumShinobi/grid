'''Ganton''' is a deprived district in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]] located in the eastern part of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]] and based on real life [[:wp:Compton, California|Compton]].

Ganton is a stronghold of the [[Grove Street Families]], as [[Grove Street]] is located here, and is populated by many Grove Street Family gang members. These include [[Carl Johnson]], [[Sweet]] and [[Ryder]]. Former residents at the beginning of GTA San Andreas include [[Beverly Johnson|Beverly]] and [[Brian Johnson]] (both deceased) and [[Big Smoke]] (who moved to Idlewood).

In [[Grand Theft Auto V]], Ganton is replaced with the incorporated City of [[Davis]], however [[Grove Street]] still remains. By [[2013]] the [[Ballas in GTA V|Ballas]] are in control of the Davis area.
