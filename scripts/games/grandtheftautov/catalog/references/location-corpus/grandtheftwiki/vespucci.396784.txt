'''Vespucci''' is a high-income beachfront residential neighborhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Many tourists go to the area to take pictures with 'bodybuilders, performance artists and hobos'.
