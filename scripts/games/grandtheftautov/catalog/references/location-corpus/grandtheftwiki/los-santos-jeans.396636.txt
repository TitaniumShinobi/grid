[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos Jeans''' is, or was, a [[:Category:Clothing Stores|clothing store]] located on [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store has its shutters down and part of the sign, featuring the [[Untied States of America|American]] flag with the company name, has partially fallen down and not been replaced.
