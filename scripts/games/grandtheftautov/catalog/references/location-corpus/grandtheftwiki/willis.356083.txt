'''Willis''' is a nine-block neighborhood in eastern [[Dukes]], [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]], bordered by Tudor Street to the north ([[Meadows Park]]) and to the east ([[Francis International Airport, Dukes|Francis International Airport]]); Seymour Avenue to the west ([[Meadow Hills]]); and Lynch Street to the south ([[Beechwood City]]).
