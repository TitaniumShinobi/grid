[[LEAD]]
'''Cent Carpet''' is a carpet and flooring retailer located on [[Carson Ave]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company finances both commercial and residential customers and can be hired to fit or repair carpets and flooring.
