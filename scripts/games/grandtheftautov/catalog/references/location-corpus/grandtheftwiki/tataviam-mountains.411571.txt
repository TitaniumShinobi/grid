The '''Tataviam Mountains''' are a mountain range located on the eastern edge of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Notable locations within the range are the [[Land Act Dam]] and its [[Land Act Reservoir|reservoir]], while the range is infamous for being the location where actress [[Leonora Johnson]] was murdered in [[1975]]. In [[2013]], [[Dom Beasley]] jumps from the dam into the small stream below and dies.
