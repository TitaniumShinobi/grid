'''West Coast Talk Radio (WCTR)''' is a talk radio station in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is a left-wing biased radio station, only listenable in Los Santos County. If the player leaves Los Santos County while tuned into WCTR, the station cuts out and [[Rebel Radio]] plays instead.
