[[LEAD]]
'''Cat-Claw Ave''' is a road in the [[Grand Senora Desert]] in [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road travels from [[Smoke Tree Rd]] in the north to [[Route 68]] in the south.
