[[LEAD]]
'''East Joshua Rd''' is a street running southwest to northeast running from [[Sandy Shores]] to the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] in [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The road also passes through the [[Alamo Sea]] area and [[Grapeseed]]. The street is a continuation of [[Joshua Rd]] and has connections to [[Marina Dr]], [[Panorama Dr]], [[Seaview Rd]] and [[Senora Fwy]].
