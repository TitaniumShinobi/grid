'''Pacific Bluffs''' is a beachfront neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The area consists a beach, a high-end residential area, an area with hotels and restaurants, and a rural area.
