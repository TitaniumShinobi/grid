[[LEAD]]
'''Candybox''' is a company that manufactures confectioneries in [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories]], [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The company is on the [[BAWSAQ]] stock exchange and has a store on [[Burlesque]] in [[Star Junction]], [[Algonquin]]. They have been "tantalising American tongues" since [[1927]]. Boxes of its products can be seen in any supermarket in GTA Vice City Stories, and its logo can be seen on [[Mule]] trucks around [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] and [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. Candybox is a parody of [[:wp:The Hershey Company|Hershey]], and the Candybox Store in [[Burlesque]] is based on the [[:wp:File:Hershey's en Times Square.jpg|Hershey's store in Times Square]].
