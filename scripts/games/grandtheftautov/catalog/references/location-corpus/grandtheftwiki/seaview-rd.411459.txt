'''Seaview Rd''' is a road in [[Grapeseed]], [[Blaine County]] that runs parallel to the [[Alamo Sea]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road begins from [[East Joshua Rd]] in the east with connections [[Grapeseed Ave]], [[Joad Ln]] and [[Grapeseed Main St]] before continuing as [[North Calafia Way]] in the west. [[The Lost Brotherhood]] have a hangout along the road, which appears in the mission [[Mr. Philips]].
