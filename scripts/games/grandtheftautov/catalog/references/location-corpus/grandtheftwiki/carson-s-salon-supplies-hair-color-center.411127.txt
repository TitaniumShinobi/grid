'''Carson's Salon Supplies & Hair Color Center''' is a beauty salon and hairdressers located on [[Carson Ave]] in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The business has a billboard for [[Jock Cranley]]'s election campaign on the side of the building.
