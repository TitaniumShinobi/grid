[[LEAD]]
'''The Richman Hotel''' is a [[Richman in GTA V|Richman]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] hotel in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is based on [[wp:The_Beverly_Hills_Hotel|The Beverly Hills Hotel]].

[[Jimmy De Santa]] frequents The Richman Hotel, as he is picked up there on countless occasions for hang-outs. It is assumed that this is where Jimmy stayed when the De Santa family temporarily left the [[De Santa House]].
