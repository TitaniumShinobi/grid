The '''IAA Building''' is a high-rise federal building and the west coast headquarters for the [[Central_Intelligence_Agency#Grand_Theft_Auto_V|International Affairs Agency]], located in the [[Pillbox Hill]] neighbourhood of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. Loosely based on [[wp:One California Plaza|One California Plaza]], it is one of the tallest buildings in the Los Santos skyline. Its design is similar to the [[FIB Building]], and the two buildings share a common plaza.
