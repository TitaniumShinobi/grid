[[LEAD]]
'''Southern Tile''' is a business located on [[Alta St]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The [[Los Santos Transit]] tram service runs directly outside the buildings entrance. A sign on the buildings exterior states that the company is having a special sale with tiles costing 99[[Money|c]] per square foot. The business has likely closed with two "For Rent" signs appearing on the building although the first has been disconnected and the second sees a person answer and then hang up without speaking.
