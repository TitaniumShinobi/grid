The '''La Puerta Freeway''' (Spanish for "The Gate") is a [[Highways|highway]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is based on the real-life [[wp:Interstate 5|Interstate 5]], and is also [[Interstate 5]] in the game. The La Puerta Freeway extends from the [[Del Perro Freeway]] in [[Downtown Los Santos in GTA V|Downtown]], southward to [[Elysian Island]] where it becomes the [[Elysian Fields Freeway]].
