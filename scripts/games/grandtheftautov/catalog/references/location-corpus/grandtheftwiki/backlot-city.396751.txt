'''Backlot City''' is a neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The neighbourhood is made up entirely of offices, sound stages and sets for the [[Richards Majestic]] film studio part owned by [[Solomon Richards]] and [[Devin Weston]]. Actors, including those in character suits, can be found walking around the neighbourhood preparing for filming. The neighbourhood, which is completely gated, has security guards who will begin chasing the player along with the [[LSPD in GTA V|police]] in GTA V until the completion of [[Mr. Richards]], when the player can walk around the neighbourhood freely.
