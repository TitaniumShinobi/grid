[[LEAD]]
'''The Blue Ark''' is a radio station in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] featuring dub and dancehall music, hosted by Lee "Scratch" Perry. The name of the station is a reference to the [[wp:Black Ark Studios|Black Ark]], a studio Lee Perry founded in 1973 to record reggae and dub songs through innovative techniques.

'''DJ''': [[Lee "Scratch" Perry]]

'''Genres''': [[wp:Dub (music)|dub]], [[wp:Dancehall|dancehall]]
