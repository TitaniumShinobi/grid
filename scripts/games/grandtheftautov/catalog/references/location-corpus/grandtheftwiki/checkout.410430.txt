[[LEAD]]
'''Checkout!''' is a chain of mini-supermarkets located in [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]], [[Alderney]] and [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It sells products from brands such as [[Dusche Gold]] and [[Redwood Cigarettes]]. The store's motto is "''Shopping for busy people''", and is open from 9am till 5pm.
