'''Aguila Burrito''' is a [[Mexico|Mexican]] food restaurant on [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] for the store is 555-0199, which results in the caller being asked to call again later.
