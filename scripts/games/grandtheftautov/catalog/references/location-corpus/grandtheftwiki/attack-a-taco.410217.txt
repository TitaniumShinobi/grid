[[LEAD]]
'''Attack-A-Taco''' is a fast food restaurant company based in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company has a store on [[Crusade Rd]] in [[Strawberry]] while vans can be found in [[South Los Santos]]. The company slogan is ''Kill that Hunger''.
