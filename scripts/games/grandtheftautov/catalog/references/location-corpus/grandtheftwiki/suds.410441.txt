[[LEAD]]
'''Suds''' is a laundry business located at the corner of [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The business may be linked to the nearby [[Suds Law Laundromat]] located further south on Strawberry Ave. The company is a specialist on knitted items and drapery.
