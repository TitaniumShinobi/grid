'''Vinewood Music''' is a [[:Category:Music Stores|music store]] located on the corner of [[Alta St]] and [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. The company was established in [[1991]], describes itself as the 'suppliers of (the) finest Rock N' Roll' and also sells comics.
