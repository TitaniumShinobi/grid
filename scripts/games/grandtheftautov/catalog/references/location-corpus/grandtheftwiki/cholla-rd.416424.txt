'''Cholla Rd''' is a road in the [[Grand Senora Desert]] in [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road connects [[Panorama Dr]] and [[Smoke Tree Rd]] despite there also being an intersection where the two meet.
