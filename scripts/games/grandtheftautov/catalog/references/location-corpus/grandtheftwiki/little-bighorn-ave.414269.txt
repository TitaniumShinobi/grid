[[LEAD]]
'''Little Bighorn Ave''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which runs from [[Rancho]] in the south to [[Textile City]] in the north and runs through [[La Mesa]], [[Mission Row]] and [[Strawberry]]. The road has connections to [[Adam's Apple Blvd]], [[Atlee St]], [[Capital Blvd]], [[Carson Ave]], [[Crusade Rd]], [[Integrity Way]], [[Jamestown St]], [[Macdonald St]], [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] and [[Sinners Passage]]. The southern end of Little Bighorn Ave is controlled by the [[Los Santos Vagos]] gang. A small homeless community can be found along Little Bighorn Ave in Mission Row and the player can also pick up a [[Prostitutes|prostitute]] in the same area.
