[[LEAD]]
The '''Maze Bank of Los Santos''' is a banking institution present in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. It is based on [[wp:U.S. Bancorp|U.S. Bank]]. Its logo can be seen on top of Los Santos' tallest skyscraper, the [[Maze Bank Tower]]. Maze Bank also owns the naming rights to the city's sports arena, the [[Maze Bank Arena]].

The bank accounts of [[Michael De Santa]] and the GTA Online protagonist are held at Maze Bank. In GTA Online, the player can withdraw and deposit money into his/her account either by using an [[ATM]] or accessing the Maze Bank website from their [[mobile phone|smartphone]].
