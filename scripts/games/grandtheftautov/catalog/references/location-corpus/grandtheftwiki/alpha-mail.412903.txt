[[LEAD]]
'''Alpha Mail''' is a courier company featured in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], [[The Lost and Damned]], [[The Ballad of Gay Tony]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars]], [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company has three known depots: [[Port Tudor]] in [[Alderney]], either side of [[Jackhammer St]] in [[Industrial, Bohan|Industrial]] in [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] and [[Exceptionalists Way]] in [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]]. The Alpha Mail logo appears on some [[Mule]] trucks in GTA IV, and in GTA Chinatown Wars, [[Alphamail|Alphamail delivery vans]] are found bearing Alpha Mail's livery. Alpha Mail is also listed on [[BAWSAQ]].
