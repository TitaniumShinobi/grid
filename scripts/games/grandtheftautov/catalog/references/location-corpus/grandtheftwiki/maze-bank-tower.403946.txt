The '''Maze Bank Tower''' is a skyscraper in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]], based on [[wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles']] [[wp:U.S._Bank_Tower_(Los_Angeles)|U.S. Bank Tower]]. Located in [[Downtown_Los_Santos_in_GTA_V|Downtown Los Santos]], it is the city's tallest skyscraper and presumably the tallest building in the [[San Andreas in GTA V|state of San Andreas]]. [[Maze Bank of Los Santos|Maze Bank]] houses its headquarters in the building as evidenced by its signage on the building's base and crown. The building's base has a elaborate plaza with fountains and gardens. It is one of several [[Parachute|basejumping]] locations within San Andreas. The Maze Bank Tower is featured in the 1985 film [[18 Wheeler Deathrace]], although the building's real-life counterpart did not start construction until 1987.
