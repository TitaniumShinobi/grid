[[LEAD]]
'''The Families''' are an African-American street gang based in [[Chamberlain Hills]] and [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] featured in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The gang is involved in a long time feud with rival African-American gang the [[Ballas in GTA V|Ballas]] as well the [[Los Santos Vagos in GTA V|Los Santos Vagos]]. [[Franklin Clinton]], one of the [[protagonist]]s of GTA V, is a member of the gang. The gang also holds a small amount of [[Davis]] but their hold on that neighbourhood was weakened when they lost [[Grove St]] to the Ballas prior to early [[2013]].

[[SECTION:2:Grove Street Families in the HD Universe]]
The existence of [[Grove Street Families]] is revealed in a conversation between [[Franklin Clinton]] and [[Lamar Davis]] in the mission [[Hood Safari]]: 

Lamar: Eh, you know Grove Street used to be Families hood. <br />
Franklin: Grove Street Families. Man, long time ago. <br />
Lamar: What happened to them dudes? <br />
Franklin: Shit, I don't know. Could be dead. Shit, or could be CEOs by now. <br />
Lamar: Probably living in some suburb somewhere, driving SUVs and shit, you know, soccer moms and shit, coaching little league football teams. <br />
Franklin: Yeah, the suburban flight, huh? Probably so. <br />
Lamar: That's gonna be me and you one day dog. <br />
Franklin: If we lucky, man. Shit, at the moment, it looks like we're gonna end up the other direction.
