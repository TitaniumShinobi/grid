[[LEAD]]
'''Newport''' is a district in the eastern area of [[Staunton Island]], [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City]], which has appeared in [[Grand Theft Auto III]], [[Grand Theft Auto Advance]] and [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]]. It borders [[Torrington]] to the south, [[Fort Staunton]] and [[Liberty Campus]] to the north, an unnamed body of water to the east, and [[Belleville Park]] to the west.

[[SECTION:2:Places of interest]]
Newport boasts multiple landmarks, interactive or otherwise. Among them, the Staunton [[Ammu-Nation]], a [[Pay 'n' Spray]] automobile paint shop and one of [[8-Ball|8-Ball's]] [[8-Ball Autos|bomb shop]] among a row of garage-based business, an unnamed Newport shopping mall, the [[Newport Multistory Car Park]], and an unnamed civic building with Federal architecture (dubbed "[[City Hall (Staunton Island)|City Hall]]" by the official website of GTA III); located on the southern edge of Newport, just south of Callahan Bridge).

[[Asuka Kasen's Condo]] in GTA III and Toni Cipriani's [[Staunton Island Safehouse|Staunton Island safehouse]] in GTA Liberty City Stories are also situated within Newport.
