[[LEAD]]
'''Labor Pl'''  is a street, running west to north east, from [[Cypress Flats]] (west) to [[El Burro Heights]] (north east) in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to the [[Amarillo Way]], [[El Rancho Blvd]] [[Elysian Fields Freeway]], [[Orchardville Ave]] and [[Tower Way]].
