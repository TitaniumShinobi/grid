[[LEAD]]
The '''Abandoned Motel''' is a former motel on [[Algonquin Blvd]] in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The motel may have been run by [[Jeb Evans]] and his wife who owned a guesthouse in the town during the 1970s. A [[Stunt Jumps in GTA V|stunt jump]] involves jumping over the motel.
