The '''De Santa House''' is a mansion owned by [[Amanda De Santa|Amanda]] and [[Michael De Santa]] on [[Portola Dr]] in [[Rockford Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Amanda and Michael's children, [[Jimmy De Santa|Jimmy]] and [[Tracey De Santa|Tracey]], also live at the house, which also acts as a [[Safehouses in GTA V|safehouse]]. The house allows Michael to watch [[TV Shows in GTA V|television]], drink alcohol, smoke marijuana, practice [[yoga]] and play tennis against Amanda, with [[Trevor Philips]] also able to play tennis against Amanda at the house's tennis court. Michael's family members can be found inside the house when not on a mission along with housemaid [[Eva]] and groundsman [[Carlos (GTA V)|Carlos]]. The house also has a garage, which can hold two cars.
