[[LEAD]]
'''El Rancho Blvd''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] from [[Rancho]] to [[Murrieta Heights]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The boulevard also runs through [[Cypress Flats]] and [[El Burro Heights]]. The boulevard starts as a  continuation of [[Dutch London St]] connecting with [[Amarillo Vista]], [[Capital Blvd]], [[Carson Ave]], [[Elysian Fields Fwy]], [[Fudge Ln]], [[Innocence Blvd]], [[Labor Pl]], [[Orchardville Ave]], [[Popular St]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[South Shambles St]], [[Sustancia Rd]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]] before continuing further north as [[Mirror Park Blvd]]. It is based on Los Feliz Boulevard in Los Angeles.
