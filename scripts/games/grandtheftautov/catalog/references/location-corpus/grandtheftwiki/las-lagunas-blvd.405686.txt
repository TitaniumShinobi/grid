[[LEAD]]
'''Las Lagunas Blvd''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running from [[Pillbox Hill]] in the south through [[Downtown Los Santos in GTA V|Downtown]], [[Vinewood in GTA V|Vinewood]] and [[Burton]] to [[West Vinewood]] in the north, which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Dorset Dr]], [[Hawick Ave]], [[Occupation Ave]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[Spanish Ave]] and [[Vinewood Blvd]]. Its based on La Cienaga Boulevard in Los Angeles.

[[SECTION:2:Homes]]
In GTA Online, the player can purchase one of thirteen apartments along Las Lagunas Blvd through [[Dynasty 8]].
