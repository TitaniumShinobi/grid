'''Del Perro''' is a residential beachfront neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Del Perro is popular with tourists, in particular the [[Del Perro Beach|beach]], and has two piers: the [[Del Perro Pier]] and [[Pleasure Pier]]. It is based on Santa Monica, California.
