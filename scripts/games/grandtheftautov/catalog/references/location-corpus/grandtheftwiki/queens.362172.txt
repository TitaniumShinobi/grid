'''Queens''' is a district of [[San Fierro]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. It is implied to house most of San Fierro's gay, lesbian, bisexual, and transgendered population. The neighborhood situated just east of [[San Fierro City Hall|City Hall]]. To the north lies [[Santa Flora]], to the south is [[Hashbury]], and to the east is [[King's]]. The name ''Queens'' is a tongue-in-cheek reference to its residential make-up, and to the fact that it is meant to emulate San Francisco's famous Castro District, predominated by gay men.
