'''Puerto Del Sol''' is an unincorporated town officially located within the [[La Puerta]] neighbourhood of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The area is a popular destination for wealthier Los Santos residents comprising primarily of a number of jetties for docking yachts and boats as well as tourists with the [[Puerto Del Sol Yacht Club]] serving as a hotel on [[Palomino Ave]].
