[[LEAD]]
'''O'Sheas Barbers Shop''' is a barbers shop on [[Alhambra Dr]] in [[Sandy Shores]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The player, as [[Franklin Clinton]], [[Michael De Santa]] or [[Trevor Philips]], can visit the barbers shop to change hairstyles. The interior and the barber, [[Donna]], are shared with the other barbers across the [[San Andreas in GTA V|state]].
