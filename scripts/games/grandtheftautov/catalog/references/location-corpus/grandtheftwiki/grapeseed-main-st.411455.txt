'''Grapeseed Main St''' is a street in [[Grapeseed]], [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road begins at the junction of [[Joad Ln]] and [[Union Rd]] in the north with and ending at [[Seaview Rd]] and [[North Calafia Way]] in the south. The street is primarily residential and commercial before entering the more rural farmland of northern Grapeseed.
