[[LEAD]]
'''Harwood''', is a district on [[Portland]], [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City]], in [[Grand Theft Auto III]], [[Grand Theft Auto Advance]] and [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]]. It is a northernmost district in the island, bordering [[Saint Mark's]] and [[Hepburn Heights]] to the south.
