[[LEAD]]
'''Marlowe Dr''' is a road in northern [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs from [[Richman Glen]] in the west through [[Vinewood Hills]] and [[Banham Canyon]] before ending in Vinewood Hills in the east. The road has connections with [[Baytree Canyon Rd]], [[Lake Vinewood Dr]] (two connections), [[Lake Vinewood Est]], [[Milton Dr]], [[Mt. Haan Dr]], [[Mt. Haan Rd]], [[Senora Rd]] and [[Tongva Dr]]. There are also dirt roads that connect to the [[Vinewood Sign]] and [[East Galileo Ave]].
