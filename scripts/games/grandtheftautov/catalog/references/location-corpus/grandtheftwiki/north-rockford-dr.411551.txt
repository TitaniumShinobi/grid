'''North Rockford Dr''' is a street in northern [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street starts in the north as a southward bound continuation of [[Tongva Dr]] in [[Richman Glen]] and travels south through [[Vinewood Hills]] and [[Richman in GTA V|Richman]] before ending at [[West Eclipse Blvd]] in [[Morningwood]]. The street continues further into Morningwood as [[South Rockford Dr]]. There are also connections to [[Ace Jones Dr]], [[Banham Canyon Dr]], [[Kortz Dr]], [[Marlowe Dr]], [[Picture Perfect Drive]] and [[Richman St]].

The northern part of the road is in the very rural north of the city while the sections in Vinewood Hills and Richman is a high-end residential area with large houses.
