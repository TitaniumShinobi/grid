The '''Banner Hotel & Spa''' is a hotel that appears in the [[HD Universe]]. The chain has a hotel in [[Star Junction]], [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] and in [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. Based on [http://www.emporis.com/en/wm/bu/?id=embassysuitestimessquare-newyorkcity-ny-usa 1568 Broadway] in [[wp:Times Square|Times Square]], the building also houses the [[Seagull Theater]].

The telephone number for the hotel is: 1-669-555-5464.
