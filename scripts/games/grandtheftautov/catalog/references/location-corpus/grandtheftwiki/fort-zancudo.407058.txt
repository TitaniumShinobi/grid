[[LEAD]]
'''Fort Zancudo''' is a [[United States of America|US]] [[military|Air Force]] base appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The base is bordered by [[Mount Josiah]] to the north, the [[Zancudo River]] to the east, the [[Great Chaparral]] to the south east, [[Lago Zancudo]] to the south, southwest and west, and [[North Chumash]] to the northwest.
