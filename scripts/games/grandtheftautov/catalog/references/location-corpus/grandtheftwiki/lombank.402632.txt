[[LEAD]]
'''Lombank''' is a bank in the [[HD Universe]] that appears in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The company has known branches in [[Alderney City]], [[Algonquin]], [[Hove Beach]] and [[Ludendorff]] and it also holds shares with [[BAWSAQ]]. The company's logo is seen on a replica of [http://www.100wilshireblvd.info 100 Wilshire Blvd] in the GTA V trailer. The bank's logo resembles that of [[:wp:US Bank|US Bank]], while its name may be taken from [[:wp:Lombard Street, London|Lombard Street]], a street in [[London]] that was the former home of most British banks.
