[[LEAD]]
'''O'Neil Way''' is a road in [[Grapeseed]], [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road has connections to [[Union Rd]] in the northeast and [[Joad Ln]] in the southwest as well as dirt roads leading to the [[O'Neil Farm]].
