[[LEAD]]
'''Blaine County Talk Radio''' is a talk radio station in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is a parody of American conservative political talk radio. The station is only heard if the player is in [[Blaine County]]; if the player is tuned to BCTR and leaves Blaine County, the station cuts out and [[West Coast Classics]] plays instead.
