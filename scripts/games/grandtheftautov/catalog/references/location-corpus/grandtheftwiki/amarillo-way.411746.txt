'''Amarillo Way''' is a street, running southeast to northwest, in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to the [[Elysian Fields Fwy]] (at both its north and southern ends), [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Labor Pl]]. The street runs parallel to the freeway and to [[Tower Way]] on the opposite side of the freeway.
