[[LEAD]]
'''Marina Dr''' is a street running west to south east starting alongside the [[Alamo Sea]] and ending in [[Sandy Shores]], going through the [[Grand Senora Desert]]. The street has connections to [[Algonquin Blvd]], [[Alhambra Dr]], [[Armadillo Ave]], [[East Joshua Rd]], [[Lesbos Ln]], [[Meringue Ln]], [[Mountain View Dr]], [[Niland Ave]], [[Panorama Dr]] and [[Zancudo Ave]]. [[Clinton]], an African-American war veteran, can be found on the corner of Armadillo Ave when playing as [[Franklin Clinton]]. A [[Races in GTA V|race]] can also be found on the street.
