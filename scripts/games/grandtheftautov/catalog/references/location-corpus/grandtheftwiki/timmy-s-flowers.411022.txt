[[LEAD]]
'''Timmy's Flowers''' is a florist located at the corner of [[Macdonald St]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Davis]] and [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company is located at the border of the Davis and Strawberry neighbourhoods of Los Santos and has a 'Red Rose Special' for [[Money|$]]11.99.
