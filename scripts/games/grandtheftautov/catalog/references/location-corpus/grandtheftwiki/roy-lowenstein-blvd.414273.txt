[[LEAD]]
'''Roy Lowenstein Blvd''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] that runs from [[Davis]] in the southwest to [[Rancho]] in the northeast. The street has connections to [[Carson Ave]], [[Covenant Ave]], [[Dutch London St]], [[Innocence Blvd]], [[Little Bighorn Ave]] and [[Macdonald St]]. The [[Ballas]] control the part of the street in Davis while the [[Los Santos Vagos]] control the area in Rancho.
