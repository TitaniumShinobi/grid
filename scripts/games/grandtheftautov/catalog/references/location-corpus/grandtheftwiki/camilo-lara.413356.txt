'''Camilo Lara''', DJ of [[East Los FM]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] along with [[Don Cheto]], is a Mexico-City based DJ and producer behind the group [[wp:Mexican Institute of Sound|Mexican Institute of Sound]] (or ''Instituto Mexicano del Sonido'' in Spanish), who had their song "''Es-Toy''" featured on East Los FM.
