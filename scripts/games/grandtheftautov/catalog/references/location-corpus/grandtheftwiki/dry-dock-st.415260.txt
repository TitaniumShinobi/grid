'''Dry Dock St''' is a street, running west to east, in [[Cypress Flats]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Orchardville Ave]] in the east and [[Popular St]] in the west.
