[[LEAD]]
'''Greenwich Pkwy''' is a street running from [[La Puerta]] to [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The parkway briefly enters [[Vespucci Beach]] and has connections to [[Autopia Pkwy]], [[Dutch London St]] and [[Exceptionalists Way]].
