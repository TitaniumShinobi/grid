[[LEAD]]
'''Save-a-¢ent''' is a discount chain store appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], [[The Lost and Damned]], [[The Ballad of Gay Tony]], [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are two stores in [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]], one at the corner of [[Albany Ave]] and [[Feldspar St]] in [[Fishmarket North]] and another at the corner of [[Galveston Ave]] and [[Jade St]] in [[Westminster]]. There are four stores in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]: at the corner of [[Brouge Ave]] and [[Carson Ave]] in [[Davis]], on [[Vespucci Blvd]] in [[Little Seoul]] at the [[Korean Plaza]] on [[Calais Ave]] in Little Seoul, and at the corner of [[North Rockford Dr]] and [[Boulevard Del Perro]] in [[Morningwood]].
