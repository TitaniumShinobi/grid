[[LEAD]]
'''Stoner Cement Works''' are two industrial cement works plants in the [[Grand Senora Desert]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The cement works were established in [[1952]] with one located on [[Senora Rd]] and another on [[Joshua Rd]]. The Joshua Rd plant contains a number of shipping containers, including from [[Bilgeco]]. A [[Letter Scraps|letter scrap]] can be found on the top platform of the Joshua Rd plant.
