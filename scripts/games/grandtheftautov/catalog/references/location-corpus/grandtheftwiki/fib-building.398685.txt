The '''FIB Building''' is a high rise federal building and the west coast headquarters for the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation#HD_Universe|Federal Investigation Bureau]] in the [[Pillbox Hill]] neighbourhood of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. Based on [[wp:Two California Plaza|Two California Plaza]], it is one of the tallest skyscrapers in the Los Santos skyline. Its design is similar to the [[IAA Building]], and the two buildings share a common plaza. However, the FIB Building is distinguished from the IAA Building by its skylight.
