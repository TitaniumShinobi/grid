'''North Point''' is the northern most point of mainland southern [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The area is located to the north of [[Paleto Bay]], to the west of [[Procopio Beach]] and to the south of the [[Pacific Ocean]].

The North Point Trim Track, a fitness obstacle course, is located in North Point, which features nine obstacles. Pedestrians can be seen running the course during the day but will make no attempt to use the obstacles. The course is maintained by the Paleto Bay Department of Health and is sponsored by [[Crevis]], [[EgoChaser]] energy bars, [[Flow Water]], [[Junk Energy]] and [[Muscle Gymnasium]]. A changing area and access to first aid are located near the end of the course. The area is used by the [[United States of America|US]] [[military]] with soldiers from [[Fort Zancudo]].
