'''Sonar Collections Dock''' is a purchasable dock in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is located in [[Paleto Cove]], [[Blaine County]], and is one way to obtain a [[Submersible]]. The dock costs [[Money|$]]250,000 to buy, and finds nuclear waste in waters around the map. Each nuclear waste found generates $23,000 income for the player. There are thirty of them scattered throughout the ocean around the map. If you wish to collect them all, it is suggested to do it all at the same time, and it takes between 1 hour 30 minutes and 2 hours to complete. Once all thirty barrels of nuclear waste are collected, you will receive a $250,000 bonus, making the dock a relatively quick way of scoring $940,000.

There is a [[Strangers and Freaks|stranger]], [[Abigail Mathers|Abigail]], who will appear soon after you purchase the dock and will give you a mission to collect 30 pieces of submarine wreck. This time you will be using the raft and scuba diving equipment. The reward for this mission is $10, but among the [[Wrecks|wrecks]], you will find many treasures. The raft is much quicker than the submarine.
