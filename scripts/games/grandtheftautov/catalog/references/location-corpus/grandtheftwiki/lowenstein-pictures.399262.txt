[[LEAD]]
'''Lowenstein Pictures''' is a film production company mentioned in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The company was established in the 1920s by [[Roy Lowenstein]] and made a number of family-friendly films despite Lowenstein's vicious mistreatment of his wives and children. The company went out of business in [[2013]] after the events of [[Franklin and Lamar]].
