[[LEAD]]
The '''Vinewood Casino''' is a casino located on [[Mirror Park Blvd|Mirror Park Boulevard]] in [[East Vinewood]], [[Los Santos]], next to the [[Vinewood Racetrack]] in [[GTA V]]. It is based on the Hollywood Park Casino in Inglewood, California. The building appears to have been under construction prior to the events of Grand Theft Auto V, because there is a large banner over its entrance that reads "Opening Soon". It plays no role in the storyline whatsoever and is inaccessible to the player.
