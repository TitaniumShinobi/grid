[[LEAD]]
'''Cypress Flats''' is an industrial neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The majority of the neighbourhood lies in the east of Los Santos and is bordered by [[La Mesa]] in the north and north east, [[El Burro Heights]] in the east and south east, [[Terminal]] to the south, [[Elysian Island]] to the south west, [[Banning]] to the west and [[Rancho]] to the north west.
