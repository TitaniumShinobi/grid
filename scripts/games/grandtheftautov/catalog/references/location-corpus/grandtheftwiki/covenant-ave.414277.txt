'''Covenant Ave''' is a street in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Covenant Ave is controlled by the [[Ballas]] and has connections to [[Brouge Ave]] and [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]]. There are no businesses located on the street.
