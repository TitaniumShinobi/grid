The '''Rockford Hills Metro Station''' is a metro station for the [[Los Santos Transit Metro]] service running in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The station is located underground and is located between [[Boulevard Del Perro]] and [[South Boulevard Del Perro]] in [[Rockford Hills]]. It can be accessed from [[Mad Wayne Thunder Dr]] or [[Portola Dr]]. An armed [[LSPD in GTA V|police officer]] can sometimes be seen in the station. The metro stops at [[Burton Metro Station|Burton]] travelling clockwise and [[Del Perro Metro Station|Del Perro]] travelling counter-clockwise.
