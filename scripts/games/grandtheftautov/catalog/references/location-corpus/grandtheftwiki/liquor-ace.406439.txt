[[LEAD]]
'''Liquor Ace''' is a liquor store and methamphetamine laboratory on [[Algonquin Blvd]] on the outskirts of [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store is owned by [[Trevor Philips]] who bases his [[Trevor Philips Industries (gang)|gangs]] interests from the store using the downstairs as a liquor store and the upstairs as a methamphetamine laboratory with [[Chef]] keeping an eye on things upstairs. The area is used by many of the local residents to purchase drugs and [[Prostitutes|prostitutes]] can be seen soliciting for business.
