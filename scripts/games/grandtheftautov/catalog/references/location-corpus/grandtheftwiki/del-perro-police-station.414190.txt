The '''Del Perro Police Station''' is a [[LSPD in GTA V|Los Santos Police Department]] police station located at the end of [[Red Desert Ave]] on the [[Del Perro Pier]] in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The station is one of two located on the beaches of Los Santos, the other being [[Vespucci Beach Police Station]]. No police officers can be found at the station and the player will not respawn there if [[Busted|arrested]]. Despite this, a [[Police Cruiser]] can occasionally be found at the station.
