[[LEAD]]
'''Bay City Ave''' is an avenue in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue begins in [[Vespucci Beach]] before continuing northeast through [[La Puerta]], [[Vespucci Canals]] and [[Del Perro]], ending in [[Pacific Bluffs]]. The avenue has connections to [[Aguja St]], [[Bay City Incline]], [[Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Conquistador St]], [[Cortes St]], [[Cougar Ave]], [[Equality Way]], [[Goma St]], [[Imagination Ct]], [[Invention Ct]], [[Magellan Ave]], [[Marathon Ave]], [[Melanoma St]], [[Palomino Ave]], [[Playa Vista]], [[Prosperity St]], [[Red Desert Ave]], [[Rub St]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[Sandcastle Way]], [[Tug St]], [[Vitus St]] and [[West Eclipse Blvd]]. The avenue is just over one mile long.
