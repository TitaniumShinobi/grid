'''Brouge Ave''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue runs from [[Davis]] in the southwest to [[Rancho]] in the northeast. The [[Ballas]] control the avenue, which has connections to [[Carson Ave]], [[Covenant Ave]], [[Grove St]] and [[Macdonald St]].
