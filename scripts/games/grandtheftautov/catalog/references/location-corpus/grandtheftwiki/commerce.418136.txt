'''Commerce''' is a district in [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. It surrounds the area of [[Pershing Square]]. North of Commerce is [[Downtown Los Santos in GTA III Era|Downtown Los Santos]], while south of Commerce is [[Verdant Bluffs]]. West of Commerce are [[Verona Beach]], [[Market, Los Santos|Market]] and [[Los Santos Conference Center|Conference Center]], while to the east are the districts of [[Little Mexico]] and [[Idlewood]]. As the name suggests, the district is a commercial district; a lot of businesses and office buildings are found in this area, and there are almost no residences found in the area. Commerce is based on the [[wp:Civic Center, Los Angeles|Civic Center]] and [[wp:Commerce, California|Commerce]] districts of Los Angeles.
