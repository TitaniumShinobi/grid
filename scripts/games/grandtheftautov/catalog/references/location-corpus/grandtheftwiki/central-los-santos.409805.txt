[[LEAD]]
'''Central Los Santos''' is the central region of the city of [[Los Santos]], [[San Andreas]]. The region includes [[Downtown Los Santos]], [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Legion Square]], [[Textile City]], [[Mission Row]], [[Burton]], [[Vinewood]], [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Alta]], and [[Hawick]]. It is based off the region of [[wp:Central Los Angeles]].
