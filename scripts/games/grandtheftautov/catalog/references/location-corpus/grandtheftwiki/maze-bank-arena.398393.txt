The '''Maze Bank Arena''' is a sports arena in the [[La Puerta]] district of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is the home arena of the [[Los Santos Panic]] basketball club. [[Maze Bank]] owns the arena's [[wp:naming rights|naming rights]].
