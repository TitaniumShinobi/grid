[[LEAD]]
'''North Yankton''' is a [[United States of America|American]] state in the [[HD Universe]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. North Yankton is on the American/[[Canada|Canadian]] border and contains the town of [[Ludendorff]]. North Yankton is based on the real-life state [[:wp:North Dakota|North Dakota]]. The state is rural and the climate is primarily cold with the state covered in snow in both its appearances in GTA V. The states police force is the [[North Yankton State Patrol]].
