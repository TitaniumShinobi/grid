[[LEAD]]
The '''Blaine County Savings Bank''' is a small [[:Category:Banks|bank]] chain located in southern [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The bank has two branches located at the corner of [[Cascabel Ave]] and [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]] and another on [[Route 1]] in [[Chumash]], [[Los Santos County]]. Security company [[Gruppe Sechs]] are used to transport money between the two branches.
The Paleto Bay branch is used by the [[Los Santos County Sheriff]] to hold funds obtained from bribery, extortion, and general corruption, which stands at over [[Money|$]]8 million when it is robbed by [[Franklin Clinton]], [[Michael De Santa]], [[Trevor Philips]], and their chosen accomplice. When exiting the bank, the group is confronted by local police, [[NOOSE]] agents and [[Military|soldiers]] from [[Fort Zancudo]].
