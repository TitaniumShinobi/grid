The '''El Burro Heights Fire Station''', or the '''Los Santos County Fire Department Fire Station 7''', is an [[Los Santos Fire Department]] fire station located on [[Capital Blvd]] in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The station shares the same grounds as the [[St. Fiacre Hospital]]. The stations main building is a large two storey building, where fire-fighters wait and the [[Firetruck]] spawns, which can accommodate up to six Firetrucks with single-storey areas at the rear for office space. There is also a four storey tower for practice, a small single-storey cabin, a water tank and two [[Xero]] pumps.
