[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos Customs''' is an automobile customization shop in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] that allows the player to customize various standard road vehicles in the game. Upgrades can be both aesthetic or mechanical. The garage is based on [[wp:West Coast Customs|West Coast Customs]].

Los Santos Customs replaces the [[Pay 'n' Spray]] garages from previous games.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
[[File:LosSantosCustoms-GTAV-LosSantosInternational.jpg|thumb|left|The Los Santos Customs at Los Santos International Airport.]]
Los Santos Customs garages are located throughout the game. There are four within the game, located at [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]], [[La Mesa]], [[Burton]], and the [[Grand Senora Desert]] (the latter of which [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] can purchase, and once he does, all modifications performed there are free for him).
