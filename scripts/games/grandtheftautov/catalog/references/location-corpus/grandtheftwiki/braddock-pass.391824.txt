'''Braddock Pass''' is an area in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The [[Senora Freeway]] goes through Braddock pass, which is located south of [[Paleto Bay]]. The player can also raid a marijuana farm in the area.
