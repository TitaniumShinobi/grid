[[LEAD]]
The '''LSPD Auto Impound''' are two automobile impounds operated and controlled by the [[LSPD in GTA V|Los Santos Police Department]], which appear in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The LSPD outsource the Rancho lot to certain individuals. The first impound lot is at the [[Mission Row Police Station]] at the corner of [[Sinner St]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]] in [[Mission Row]]. The second is located at the [[Davis Sheriff's Station]] on the corner of [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] in [[Rancho]].
