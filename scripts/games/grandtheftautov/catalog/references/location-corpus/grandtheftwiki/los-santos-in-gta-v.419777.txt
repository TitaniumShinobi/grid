[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos''' is a fictional city in the state of [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The city is bordered by [[Blaine County]] to the north and the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the east, south and west. The city has various cultures visible throughout from the film-making [[Vinewood in GTA V|Vinewood]] to the gang-controlled areas like [[Strawberry]]. The city of Davis is located within Los Santos. Los Santos is based on the real-life city of [[:wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles, California]]. A different version of Los Santos previously [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|appeared]] in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]].
