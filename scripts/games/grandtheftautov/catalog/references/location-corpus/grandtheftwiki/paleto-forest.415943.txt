'''Paleto Forest''' is a forest south of [[Paleto Bay]] in [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The [[Great Ocean Hwy]] runs through the forest and the [[Aerial Tramway]], giving access to the peak of [[Mount Chiliad in GTA V|Mount Chiliad]], can be found in the forest. The forest provides good hunting for locals, including [[Cletus Ewing]], while the trees provide jobs at [[Paleto Forest Sawmill]]. The area is controlled by the [[Ballas]], a gang from [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], who grow cannabis in the forest.
