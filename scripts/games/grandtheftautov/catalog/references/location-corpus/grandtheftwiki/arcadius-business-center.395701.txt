The '''Arcadius Business Center''' is a skyscraper in [[Downtown Los Santos in GTA V|Downtown]] [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. Its design is based on the [[wp:Westin Bonaventure Hotel|Westin Bonaventure Hotel]]; however, the Arcadius Business Center is an office building whereas its real-life counterpart is a luxury hotel. Whereas the Westin Bonaventure has 4 outer towers, the Arcadius Business Center only has 3 outer towers, and the outer towers on the Arcadius Center are the same diameter as the central core while the real-life building's outer towers are smaller than the central core. Unlike its real-life counterpart, the Arcadius Center is one of Los Santos' tallest high rises.
