[[LEAD]]
'''LTD''', also known as '''Limited Gasoline''', is a [[Filling Stations|gasoline retailer]] based in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. The garages do not become open to the player to enter until after the introductory missions for both [[Franklin Clinton]] and [[Michael De Santa]] have been completed. Each station has a [[Jerry Can|jerry can]] between gas pumps that can be collected for use as a weapon later. The convenience store can be used to purchase health snacks and sodas to replenish a characters health, use an [[Automated Teller Machine|ATM]] to check the characters bank balance or to access the internet via computer in a back office, although this may increase the players [[Wanted Level in GTA V|wanted level]]. The store can also be robbed, usually netting between [[Money|$]]120 and $950.
