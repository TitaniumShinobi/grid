'''China''', officially the '''People's Republic of China''', is a country in East Asia bordering [[Afghanistan]], [[Bhutan]], [[India]], [[Kazakhstan]], [[Kyrgyzstan]], [[Laos]], [[Mongolia]], [[Myanmar]] (Burma), [[Nepal]], [[North Korea]], [[Pakistan]], [[Russia]], [[Tajikistan]] and [[Vietnam]], as well as maritime borders with [[Japan]], the [[Philippines]], [[South Korea]] and Vietnam. The capital of China is [[:wp:Beijing|Beijing]]. There are a number of references to China, primarily through various Triad gangs, throughout the [[Grand Theft Auto]] series.
