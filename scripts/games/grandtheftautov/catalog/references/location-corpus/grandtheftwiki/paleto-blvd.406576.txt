'''Paleto Blvd''' is a the main street in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are connections to [[Cascabel Ave]], [[Duluoz Ave]], the [[Great Ocean Highway]], [[Procopio Dr]] (twice) and [[Pyrite Ave]].
