'''San Vitus Blvd''' is a north-south street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The boulevard runs from [[West Vinewood]] in the north to [[Burton]] in the south, going through Burton and [[Rockford Hills]]. There are connections to [[Abe Milton Pkwy]], [[Carcer Way]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Eclipse Blvd]], [[Hawick Ave]], [[Las Lagunas Blvd]], and [[Spanish Ave]].
