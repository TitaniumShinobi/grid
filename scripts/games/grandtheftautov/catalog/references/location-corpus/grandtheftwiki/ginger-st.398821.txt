'''Ginger St''' is a street in [[Little Seoul]] and [[Vespucci Canals]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Almost the entire street is located in Little Seoul. The street has connections to [[Lindsay Circus]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[South Rockford Dr]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]].
