[[LEAD]]
'''Glory Way''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs from [[East Vinewood]] in the northeast, through [[Mirror Park]] and ends in East Vinewood in the southwest. There are connections to [[Bridge St]], [[Mirror Park Blvd]] and [[Popular St]].
