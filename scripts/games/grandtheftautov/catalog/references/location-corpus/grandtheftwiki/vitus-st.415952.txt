[[LEAD]]
'''Vitus St''' is a street running northeast in [[Vespucci Canals]] to southwest in [[Vespucci Beach]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], briefly enter [[Vespucci]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Bay City Ave]] and [[Magellan Ave]].
