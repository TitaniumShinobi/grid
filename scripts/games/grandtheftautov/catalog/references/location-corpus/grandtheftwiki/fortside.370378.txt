'''Fortside''' is a middle class residential neighborhood geographically located in central [[Bohan]], [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]]. Its boundaries, starting from the north and moving clockwise are: the Grand Boulevard ([[Boulevard]]) to the north, Attica Avenue ([[Bohan Industrial]]) to the east, Switch Street ([[South Bohan]]) to the south, and the Humboldt River to the West. However, despite Fortside's middle class intentions.
