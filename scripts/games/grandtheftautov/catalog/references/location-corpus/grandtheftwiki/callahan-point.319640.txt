'''Callahan Point''' is a district in southwestern [[Portland]], [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City]] in [[Grand Theft Auto III]], [[Grand Theft Auto Advance]] and [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]]. It borders [[Atlantic Quays]] to the east, [[Chinatown, Portland|Chinatown]] and [[Trenton]] to the north, and an unspecified body of water to the south and west.
