[[LEAD]]
'''Prosperity St''' is a northwest to southeast street in [[Vespucci Canals]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Imagination Ct]], [[Invention Ct]], [[Palomino Ave]], [[San Andreas Ave]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]].
