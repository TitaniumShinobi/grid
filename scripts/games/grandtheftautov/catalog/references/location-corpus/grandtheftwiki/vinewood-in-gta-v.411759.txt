'''Vinewood''' is a district in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The area is famous for being the centre of the [[United States of America|American]] film industry with a number of large scale premieres, such as the film [[Meltdown (film)|Meltdown]], and contains the [[Vinewood Walk of Fame]] and the [[Vinewood Sign]]. [[West Vinewood]], [[East Vinewood]], [[Vinewood Hills]], and Downtown Vinewood are the neighbourhoods that comprise of  the larger Vinewood district. So Vinewood is one large neighbourhood with a few small neighbourhoods in it.
