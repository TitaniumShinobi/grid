[[LEAD]]
'''The Emissary''' is a luxurious hotel in [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]].

Located at Nickle Street and Columbus Avenue on the northeastern corner of [[Star Junction]], [[Algonquin]], the 15-floor hotel is clearly based on the real-life [[wp:Plaza Hotel|Plaza Hotel]] in New York City by design and location. However, The Emissary is noticeably scaled down to adapt to Algonquin's smaller size. Its position on the corner of [[Middle Park]] makes it a visible landmark across much of the park and its immediate surroundings.

Based on its rooftop sign, the Emissary is implied to be a 5-star hotel. The building is otherwise not interactive nor accessible.
