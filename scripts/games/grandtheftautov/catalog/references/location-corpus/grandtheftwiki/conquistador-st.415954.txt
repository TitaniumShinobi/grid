[[LEAD]]
'''Conquistador St''' is a street running from the northeast in [[Vespucci Canals]] to the southwest in [[Vespucci Beach]], briefly entering [[Vespucci]], in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Bay City Ave]] and [[Magellan Ave]].
