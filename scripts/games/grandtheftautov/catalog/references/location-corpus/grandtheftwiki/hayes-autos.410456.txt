[[LEAD]]
'''Hayes Autos''' is an auto repair garage on [[Little Bighorn Ave]] in [[La Mesa]] and [[Macdonald St]] in [[Rancho, [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The garage is used, and possibly owned, by [[Devin Weston]] who has [[Franklin Clinton]], [[Michael De Santa]], [[Trevor Philips]] and [[Lamar Davis]] deliver stolen cars to the garage before they are transported into [[Blaine County]].
