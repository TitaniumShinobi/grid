'''Milton Rd''' is a street in northern [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs from [[West Vinewood]] in the south to [[Vinewood Hills]] in the north. The street has connections to [[Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Cox Way]], [[Didion Dr]], [[Eclipse Blvd]], [[Kimble Hill Dr]], [[Marlowe Dr]], [[Normandy Dr]], [[North Sheldon Ave]], [[Picture Perfect Drive]] and [[Spanish Ave]].
