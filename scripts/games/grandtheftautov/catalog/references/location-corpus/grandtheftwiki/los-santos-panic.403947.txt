[[LEAD]]
The '''Los Santos Panic''' is a professional men's basketball club present in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The team's home court is the [[Maze Bank Arena]]. The team is based on the [[wp:Los Angeles Lakers|Los Angeles Lakers]] of the [[wp:National Basketball Association|NBA]], as the Panic share the team colors of the Lakers, purple and gold. [[Ballas]] gangbangers often sport Panic jerseys because the two share the color purple.
