[[LEAD]]
'''Gyro Day''' is a food store located on [[Melanoma St]] in [[Vespucci Beach]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store sells ice cream, milk shakes and [[Raine]] water as well as [[eCola]], [[Sprunk]] and [[Junk]] sodas. The player can not purchase food from the store.
