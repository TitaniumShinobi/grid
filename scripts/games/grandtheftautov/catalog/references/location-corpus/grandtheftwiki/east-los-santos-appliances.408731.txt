'''East Los Santos Appliances''' is an appliance and appliance repair store located on [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The phone number of the store is ''555-0149'', which has the following recorded message: 'This number is no longer in service.'
