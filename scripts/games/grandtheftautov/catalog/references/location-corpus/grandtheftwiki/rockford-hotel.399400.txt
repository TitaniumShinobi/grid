The '''Rockford Dorset Hotel''' is a luxury hotel that appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is located on [[Dorset Dr]]ive in [[Rockford Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. It is based on the [[wp:Beverly Wilshire Hotel|Beverly Wilshire Hotel]] in [[wp:Beverly Hills, California|Beverly Hills, California]].
