'''Chianski Passage''' is a road in the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] of [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road has only one connection, [[Union Rd]], as it ends at the [[Humane Labs and Research]] facility.
