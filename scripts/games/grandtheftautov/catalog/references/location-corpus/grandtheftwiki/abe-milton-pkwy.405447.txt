'''Abe Milton Pkwy''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The parkway starts in [[Rockford Hills]] before travelling southeast through [[Burton]], into Rockford Hills and ending in Burton. There are connections to [[Carcer Way]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Dorset Pl]], [[Eastbourne Way]], [[Las Lagunas Blvd]], [[Milton Rd]] and [[San Vitus Blvd]]. The parkway continues as [[South Boulevard Del Perro]] at its eastern end.
