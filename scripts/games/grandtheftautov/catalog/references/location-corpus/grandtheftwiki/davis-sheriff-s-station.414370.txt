The '''Davis Sheriff's Station''' is a [[LSPD in GTA V|Los Santos Police Department]] police station located on [[Innocence Blvd]] in [[Davis]], an incorporated city within [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The station has an [[LSPD Auto Impound|automobile impound lot]] connected to it. The buildings exterior displays information for the [[Los Santos County Sheriff]] although the gated parking lot shows information for the LSPD.
