[[LEAD]]
'''Vinewood Park Dr''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street begins in [[Vinewood Hills]] running through [[Vinewood Racetrack]] and Vinewood Hills before ending in [[East Vinewood]]. The street has connections to [[Elgin Ave]], [[Fenwell Pl]], the [[Los Santos Freeway]], [[Mirror Park Blvd]] and [[Senora Rd]].
