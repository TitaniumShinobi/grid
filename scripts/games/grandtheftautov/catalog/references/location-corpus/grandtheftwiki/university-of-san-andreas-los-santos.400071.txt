The '''University of San Andreas, Los Santos''' or '''ULSA''' is a university located in [[Richman in GTA V|Richman]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|Southern San Andreas]] that was established in [[1919]].
