[[LEAD]]
'''Panorama Dr''' is a street running northwest to southeast running from [[Sandy Shores]] into the [[Grand Senora Desert]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Algonquin Blvd]], [[Alhambra Dr]], [[Cholla Rd]], [[East Joshua Rd]], [[Joshua Rd]], [[Marina Dr]], [[Route 68]], [[Smoke Tree Rd]] and [[Zancudo Ave]].
