[[LEAD]]
'''Del Perro Heights''' is an apartment building featured in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. It is located on [[Prosperity St]]reet in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], near the [[Marathon Aven]]ue intersection.

Aside from being mostly a residential building, it is also where the ''Simpson, Cochrane & Bailey'' and ''Feist & Welch LLP'' offices are located, as seen in the entrance sign.
