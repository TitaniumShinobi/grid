'''Bishop's Chicken''' is a fast food restaurant chain appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company has locations at the corner of [[Davis Ave]] and [[Macdonald St]] in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] and another in the [[Tataviam Mountains]].
