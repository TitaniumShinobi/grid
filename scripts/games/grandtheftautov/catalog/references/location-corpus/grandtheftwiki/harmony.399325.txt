'''Harmony''' is a small town located in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Many of the residents of Harmony are related with "91%" of residents spending some time in [[Bolingbroke Penitentiary]]. Harmony is located close to [[Sandy Shores]], which is to the west.
