'''South Shambles St''' is a street, running north to south, from [[El Burro Heights]] to [[Cypress Flats]] in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[El Rancho Blvd]] and [[Hanger Way]].
