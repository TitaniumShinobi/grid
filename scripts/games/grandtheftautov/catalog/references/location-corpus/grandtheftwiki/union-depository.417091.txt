The '''Union Depository''' is a large bank in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The Union Depository holds over $200 million in gold bars for the federal government, and the bank is protected by guards within the building and a dedicated tactical unit of the [[LSPD in GTA V|LSPD]].

The Union Depoistory tower is not based on any one skyscraper in [[wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]]. The building's height and setbacks are based on the [[wp:Gas Company Tower|Gas Company Tower]] while the tower's hexagonal footprint is based on the [http://www.emporis.com/building/400-south-hope-los-angeles-ca-usa Mellon Bank Center]. The building's height and hexagonal footprint are also loosely based on the west tower of the [[wp:Suncor Energy Centre|Suncor Energy Centre]] in [[wp:Calgary|Calgary]], [[wp:Alberta|Alberta]], [[Canada]].
