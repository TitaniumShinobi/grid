The '''Badger Building''' is a mid-rise building in the [[Vinewood in GTA V|Vinewood]] district of Los Santos based on the [[wp:Capitol Records Building|Capitol Records Building]], located at the corner of Elgin Avenue and Hawick Avenue. Presumably, it is the corporate offices of [[Badger]] telecommunications for the [[San Andreas in GTA V|state of San Andreas]].
