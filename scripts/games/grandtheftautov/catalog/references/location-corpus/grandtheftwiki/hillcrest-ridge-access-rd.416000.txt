[[LEAD]]
'''Hillcrest Ridge Access Rd''' is an unpaved road connecting [[North Sheldon Ave]] and [[Picture Perfect Drive]] in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road briefly runs parallel with [[Hillcrest Ave]] where a number of rich people live including [[Natalia Zverovna]], the mistress of [[Martin Madrazo]]. [[Michael De Santa]] and [[Franklin Clinton]] [[Marriage Counseling|pull down]] her [[Natalia Zverovna's House|house]], believing it to be owned tennis coach [[Kyle Chavis]], using Hillcrest Ridge Access Rd, which forces Michael to come out of retirement to repay Martin.
