[[LEAD]]
'''Mad Wayne Thunder Dr''' is a road in [[Rockford Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road has connections to [[Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Eastbourne Way]], [[Greenwich Way]], [[Picture Perfect Drive]], [[South Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Spanish Ave]] and [[West Eclipse Blvd]].
