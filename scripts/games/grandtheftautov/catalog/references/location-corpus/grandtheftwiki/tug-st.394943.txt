'''Tug St''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running northeast to southwest in [[La Puerta]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]].  The street has a connection to [[Bay City Ave]] and has both a [[Koi Retreat and Spa|retreat/spa]] and an apartment building located on the street, with a number of small jetties located around the apartment building.
