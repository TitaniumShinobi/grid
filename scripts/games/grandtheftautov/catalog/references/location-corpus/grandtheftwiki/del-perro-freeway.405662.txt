[[LEAD]]
The Del Perro Freeway is a highway which Interstate 2 is designated as. It is the busiest and largest( by width) freeway in the Los Santos area and the State of San Andreas. Its western terminus is at the Great Ocean Highway (Route 1) in Del Perro. Its Eastern terminus is at the Olympic/Palomino Freeway (Interstate 4/ Route 15) and Elysian Fields Freeway (Route 20) junction in East Los Santos. The real life counterpart is the Santa Monica Freeway.

Other major junctions include Palomino Avenue in Rockford Hills, Rockford Drive in Del Perro, and La Puerta Freeway (Interstate 5) in Little Seoul.
