[[LEAD]]
'''Trenton''' is a industrial district located in [[Portland]], [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City]]. It borders [[Portland View]] to the north, [[Atlantic Quays]] to the south, [[Portland Harbor]] to the east and [[Chinatown, Portland|Chinatown]] to the west.

[[SECTION:2:Places of interest]]
The area is not controlled by any gang, although senior [[Leone Family]] gang member [[Joey Leone]] owns a [[Joey's Garage|garage]] in the district.

Other notable businesses based in Trenton include [[AmCo]], [[Bitch'n' Dog Food]] (in [[2001]]), [[Don Kiddick's Sausage Factory]] (in [[1998]]), [[Liberty Pharmaceuticals]], [[Mean Street Taxis]], [[Liberty City Sawmills]], an unnamed bus depot (with spawning [[Coach]]es) and the [[Now and Zen Dojo]].
