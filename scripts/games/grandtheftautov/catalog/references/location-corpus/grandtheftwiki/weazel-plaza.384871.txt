'''Weazel Plaza''' is a skyscraper in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]], based on the real-life [[wp:Fox_Plaza_(Los_Angeles)|Fox Plaza]] in [[wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]]. The building's name suggests that it is the headquarters for the [[Weazel]] family of networks.
