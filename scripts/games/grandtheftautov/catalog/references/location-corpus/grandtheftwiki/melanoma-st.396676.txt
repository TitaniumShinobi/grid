'''Melanoma St''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running from the northeast in [[La Puerta]] to the southwest in [[Vespucci Beach]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Bay City Ave]] and [[Magellan Ave]].
