'''Pleasure Pier''' is a pier in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. A poster can be seen on a guard station at the beginning of the trailer and in the background when two men jogging on the street. Pleasure Pier is more than likely based on Santa Monica Pier. The area also seems to have a roller coaster and other features of an amusement park, which appear to be based on [[wp:Pacific Park|Pacific Park]]. The '''Del Perro Pier''' was first revealed in promotional GTA V photo viewers released in late October 2012.
