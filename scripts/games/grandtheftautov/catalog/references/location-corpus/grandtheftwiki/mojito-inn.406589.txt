The '''Mojito Inn''' is a hotel located on [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The inn has a banner for the annual ''Paleto Bay Poultry Pagaent'' suggesting that it may be the venue for the event.
