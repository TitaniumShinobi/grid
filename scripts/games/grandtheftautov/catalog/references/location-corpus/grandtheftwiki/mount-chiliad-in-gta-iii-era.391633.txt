[[LEAD]]
'''Mount Chiliad''' is a mountain in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]], located in [[Whetstone]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. Immediately to the north is the bustling city of [[San Fierro]], while just to the south, at the mountain's base, is the small town of [[Angel Pine]].

The Mount Chiliad name is reused for a [[Mount Chiliad in GTA V|similar mountain]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]].
