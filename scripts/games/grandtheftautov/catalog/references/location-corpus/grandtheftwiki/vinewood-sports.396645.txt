[[LEAD]]
'''Vinewood Sports''' is a [[:Category:Clothing Stores|clothing store]] located on [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store sells clothing from [[Eris]], [[ProLaps]] and [[Heat]]. The store front features a modified [[:File:Toni-GTAVC-artwork.jpg|image]] of [[Toni]] from [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City]].
