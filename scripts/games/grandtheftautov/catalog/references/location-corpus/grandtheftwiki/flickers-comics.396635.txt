'''Flickers Comics''' is a comic book store located on [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store sells comics for [[The Brown Streak]], [[Republican Space Rangers]] and [[Princess Robot Bubblegum]].
