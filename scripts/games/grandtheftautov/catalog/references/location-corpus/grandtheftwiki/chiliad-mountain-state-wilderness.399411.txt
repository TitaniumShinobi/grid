'''Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness''' is a state park located in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The state park surrounds [[Mount Chiliad in GTA V|Mount Chiliad]], which makes up most of the state park along with a forest. The area is home to a number of [[Animals in GTA V|animals]] due to its remote location, which includes birds, boars, cougars, coyotes and deer.
