'''Baytree Canyon Rd''' is a street in [[Blaine County]] and [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road runs from [[Downtown Vinewood]] through [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Baytree Canyon]], Vinewood Hills, the [[Grand Senora Desert]], [[Great Chaparral]] and the Grand Senora Desert before ending in [[Redwood Lights Track]] in [[Blaine County]].
