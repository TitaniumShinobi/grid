'''El Burro Heights''' is a neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The neighbourhood is controlled by the [[Marabunta Grande]] although [[Los Santos Vagos in GTA V|Los Santos Vagos]] can appear on the border with [[Rancho]].
