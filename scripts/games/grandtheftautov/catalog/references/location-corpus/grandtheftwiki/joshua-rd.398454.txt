[[LEAD]]
'''Joshua Rd''' is a street in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road starts in [[Harmony]] before running north to the [[Grand Senora Desert]], briefly into the [[Zancudo River]] and the Grand Senora Desert before ending in [[Sandy Shores]]. The road has connections to [[Algonquin Blvd]], [[Calafia Rd]], [[East Joshua Rd]], [[Marina Dr]], [[Panorama Dr]], [[Route 68]], [[Route 68 Approach]] and [[Senora Rd]].
