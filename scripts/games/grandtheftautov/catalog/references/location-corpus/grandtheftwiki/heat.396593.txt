[[LEAD]]
'''Heat''' is a sportswear clothing company in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]], [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. Advertisements for the company are seen all over [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]] and [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City in GTA Liberty City Stories]] and [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|in GTA IV]]. It is featured in GTA IV's in-game [[Websites in GTA IV|website]] [[Peepthatshit.com|www.peepthatshit.com]]. Several [[pedestrians]] around GTA IV's Liberty City can be seen wearing clothes made by the company. Heat has a theme available to download on [[Vipluxuryringtones.com|www.vipluxuryringtones.com]]. In the GTA V trailer, two joggers can be seen wearing Heat shorts, one with the GTA San Andreas logo and one with the GTA IV logo. A Heat store can be found on [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]].

Heat may be a parody of [[wp:Adidas|Adidas]], as the Heat logo in GTA IV is somewhat similar to Adidas' 'three stripe' logo. In GTA San Andreas, Heat's slogan is "Feel the Burn".
