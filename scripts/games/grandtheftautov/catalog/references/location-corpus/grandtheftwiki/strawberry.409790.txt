'''Strawberry''' is a neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]]. The neighbourhood is one of Los Santos' poorest, has a limited number of houses and many businesses, and is controlled by [[The Families]] gang. There are entrances to the [[Olympic Freeway|Olympic]] and [[La Puerta Freeway]]s as well as a [[Los Santos Transit]] station located in Strawberry.
