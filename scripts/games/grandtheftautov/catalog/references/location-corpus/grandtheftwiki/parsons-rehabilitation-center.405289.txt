The '''Parsons Rehabilitation Center''' is a treatment facility for substance abusers located on [[Marlowe Dr]] in [[Richman Glen]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The center is located in a rural area with three known employees: Professor [[W. Burton]], Dr. [[S. Greene]] and Dr. [[P. Chen]]. A number of people, who are presumably patients, can be found in the centers grounds as well as a [[Health|first aid kit]], a [[Spaceship Parts|spaceship part]] and a [[Carbine Rifle]]. The center consists of a housing building, a doctors office, a tennis court, a swimming pool and an area for residents to perform [[yoga]].
