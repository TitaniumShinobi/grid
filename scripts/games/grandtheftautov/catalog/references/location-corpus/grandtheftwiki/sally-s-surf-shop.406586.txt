[[LEAD]]
'''Sally's Surf Shop''' is a surfing equipment store located on [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company was founded in [[1981]] with [[Johnny Staple]] being a customer at the store, with a 'Congrats Johnny Staple' being written on one of the stores windows.
