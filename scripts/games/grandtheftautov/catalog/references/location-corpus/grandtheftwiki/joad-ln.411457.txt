[[LEAD]]
'''Joad Ln''' is a road in [[Grapeseed]], [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road has connections to [[Grapeseed Main St]] and [[Union Rd]] in the north, [[O'Neil Way]], and [[Seaview Rd]] in the south.
