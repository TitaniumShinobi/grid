[[LEAD]]
'''Liquors''' is a convenience store located on [[Brouge Ave]] in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store sells liquor, sundries, lottery tickets and has a delicatessen. There is a small area for customers to park their vehicles behind the store.
