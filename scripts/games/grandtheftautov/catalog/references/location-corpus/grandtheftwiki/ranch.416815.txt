[[LEAD]]
'''Ranch''' is a street fashion clothing store that appears in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories]], [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It appears that the Ranch store chain supplies western clothing such as blue jeans, cowboy hats, chaps, flannel shirts, and so on.
