[[LEAD]]
The '''Los Varrios Aztecas''', also referred to as the '''Varrios Los Aztecas''', is a Hispanic street gang operating in around the [[Alamo Sea]] in [[Blaine County]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The gang also has territory in northern [[Rancho]] and in [[West Vinewood]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. The gang is involved in drug and weapons smuggling. They had previously been based in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] but were forced out after a gang war with the [[Los Santos Vagos in GTA V|Los Santos Vagos]], and also have rivalries with El Salvadorean street gang the [[Marabunta Grande]], [[The Lost Brotherhood]] biker gang and [[Trevor Philips Industries (gang)|Trevor Philips Industries]], led by [[Trevor Philips]].
