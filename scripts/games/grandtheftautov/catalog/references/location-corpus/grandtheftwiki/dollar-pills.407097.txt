[[LEAD]]
'''Dollar Pills''' is a pharmacy chain with stores on the corner of [[Alta St]] and [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Alta]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Davis]], both in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] and in [[Harmony]] off [[Route 68]] in [[Blaine County]] and appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. A [[Getaway Driver|random event]] occurs at the Davis store where [[Patrick McReary]] and [[H]] rob the store but cannot escape due to not having a getaway car. The player, as [[Franklin Clinton]], can choose to kill them and either keep or return the money, or help their escape, which unlocks Patrick as a heist crew member. 

If [[Trevor Philips]] is left alive at [[Endings in GTA V|the end of GTA V]], [[Mrs. Philips|his mother]] tells him to [[Mrs. Philips (mission)|steal]] a van of [[Deludamol]]. Trevor finds the van in front of one of the Dollar Pills stores, [[Damaged Goods|taking it back]] to [[Trevor Philips' Trailer|his trailer]]; however, once he arrives, he finds that his mother had left.
