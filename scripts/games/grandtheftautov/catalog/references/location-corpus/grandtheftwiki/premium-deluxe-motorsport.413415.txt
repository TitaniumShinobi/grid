[[LEAD]]
'''Premium Deluxe Motorsport''' (abbreviated PDM) is a vehicle dealership located at the corner of [[Adam's Apple Blvd]] and [[Power St]] in [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company is owned by [[Simeon Yetarian]].
