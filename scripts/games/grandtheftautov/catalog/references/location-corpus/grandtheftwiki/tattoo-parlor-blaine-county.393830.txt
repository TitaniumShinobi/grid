[[LEAD]]
The unnamed '''Tattoo Parlor''' is a [[:Category:Tattoo Parlors|tattoo parlor]] located in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The player, as [[Franklin Clinton]], [[Michael De Santa]] or [[Trevor Philips]], can visit the tattoo parlor to purchase a tattoo with [[Spider (GTA V)|Spider]] working as the tattoo artist.
