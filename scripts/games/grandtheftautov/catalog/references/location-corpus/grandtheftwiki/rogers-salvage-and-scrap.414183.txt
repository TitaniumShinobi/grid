'''Rogers Salvage and Scrap''' is a waste recycling and scrap yard appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The business is located at the corner of [[Alta St]] and [[South Arsenal St]] in [[La Puerta]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. There are a number of [[wrecks]] in the scrap yard, including a [[USSR|Soviet]] tank from World War Two. The company [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] is 555-0069.
