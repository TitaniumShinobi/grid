'''South Arsenal St''' is a street in [[La Puerta]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Alta St]], [[Dutch London St]], and [[Mutiny Rd]] while going under the [[La Puerta Freeway]].
