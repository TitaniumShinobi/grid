[[LEAD]]
'''Cortes St''' is a street running northeast in [[Vespucci Canals]] to the west in [[Vespucci Beach]], briefly enter [[Vespucci]], in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Bay City Ave]] and [[Magellan Ave]].
