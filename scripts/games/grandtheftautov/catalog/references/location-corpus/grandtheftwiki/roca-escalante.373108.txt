'''Roca Escalante''' is a district of [[Las Venturas]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. To the left of Roca Escalante is [[The Emerald Isle]], while to the south is the [[Old Venturas Strip]]. To the east is the [[Julius Thruway|Julius Thruway East]] and the district of [[Creek]] while to the north are [[Spinybed]], [[Prickle Pine]] and [[K.A.C.C.|K.A.C.C. Military Fuels]].

Roca Escalante is mainly a commercial district, featuring a number of businesses and hotels, as well as buildings for public use.
