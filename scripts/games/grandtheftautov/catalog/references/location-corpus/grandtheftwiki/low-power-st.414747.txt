[[LEAD]]
'''Low Power St''' is a street in [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Low Power St continues on at ground level while a ramp keeps the player on Power St, heading to [[Alta]] and [[Hawick]]. There are connections to [[Integrity Way]] and [[San Andreas Ave]].
