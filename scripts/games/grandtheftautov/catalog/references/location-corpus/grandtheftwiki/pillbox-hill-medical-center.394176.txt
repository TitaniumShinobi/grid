'''Pillbox Hill Medical Center''' is a [[Hospitals|hospital]] in [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The hospital is a spawn point for [[Franklin Clinton]], [[Michael De Santa]] and [[Trevor Philips]] should they be killed, outside of missions, in the area. The hospital has several departments, including: emergency room, endoscopy, medical physics, radiology, ultra sound and urology. A chapel is also present within the hospital. The hospital has two entrances: one on [[El Rancho Blvd]] in [[Textile City]] and another on [[Strawberry Ave]] in Pillbox Hill with the player respawning at the Strawberry Ave entrance.
