[[LEAD]]
'''Tire Nutz''' is an auto repair and parts shop located at the corner of [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Power St]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The phone number for the business is ''555-2318'', which will always be busy if called.
