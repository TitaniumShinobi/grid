'''Covington Supplies''' is a garage located on [[El Rancho Blvd]] in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The business is owned by the [[Federal Bureau of Investigation#HD Universe|FIB]] and is used by corrupt agents [[Steve Haines]] and [[Dave Norton]] as a base for their illegal activities. These include [[Three's Company|infiltrating]] the [[Central Intelligence Agency#HD Universe|IAA]] [[IAA Building|building]], [[By the Book|torturing]] [[Ferdinand Kerimov]] for information and [[Blitz Play|stealing]] IAA bonds.
