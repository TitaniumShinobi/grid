'''Dynasty 8''' is a real estate company in the [[HD Universe]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. It is interactive, as players can go to sale sites to purchase properties that are located throughout Southern [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]]. The company also runs the website [[dynasty8realestate.com|www.dynasty8realestate.com]]. The site is interactive on GTA Online, with the player being able to purchase properties either in person or via the in-game website.
