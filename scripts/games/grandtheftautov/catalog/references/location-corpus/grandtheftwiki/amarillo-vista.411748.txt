'''Amarillo Vista''' is a street in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[El Rancho Blvd]] and [[Innocence Blvd]]. The [[Marabunta Grande]] can be found on the street.
