'''Leroy's Electronics''' is an electronics retailer owned by [[Leroy (GTA O)|Leroy]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company has stores on [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]] and on [[West Mirror Dr]] in [[Mirror Park]], both in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]].
