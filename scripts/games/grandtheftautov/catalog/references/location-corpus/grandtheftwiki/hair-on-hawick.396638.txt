'''Hair on Hawick''' is a [[:Category:Barbers|barber salon]] located on [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The store shares its interior and only employee, [[Donna]], with the [[Herr Kutz]] barber chain.
