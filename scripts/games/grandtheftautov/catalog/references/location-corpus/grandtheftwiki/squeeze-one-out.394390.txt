'''Squeeze One Out''' is a smoothie and fruit juice store located on the corner of [[Mirror Park Blvd]] and [[Nikola Ave]] in [[Mirror Park]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company logo has two slices of orange in place of the letter 'O'.
