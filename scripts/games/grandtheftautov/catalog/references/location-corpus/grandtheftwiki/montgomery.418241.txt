'''Montgomery''' is a small country town located in northern [[Red County]], just three miles from [[Bone County]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. It is immediately adjacent to the [[Montgomery Intersection]] and sits along the edge of Los Santos, overlooking the body of water which clearly separates [[Las Venturas]] to the north and [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]] to the south, along the major [[Highways|highway]] connecting these two cities. It has an estimated population of around 3,623 inhabitants.
