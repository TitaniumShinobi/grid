'''Peter Dreyfuss' House''' is a house owned by [[Peter Dreyfuss]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The house is located on [[Ace Jones Dr]] in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. In [[2013]], [[Franklin Clinton]] confronts Dreyfuss at his house after [[Letter Scraps|discovering]] that Dreyfuss had murdered [[Leonora Johnson]] before mutilating the corpse before tormenting her parents [[Eileen Johnson|Eileen]] and [[Harold Johnson]] in subsequent years. Dreyfuss attempts to escape with the player having the choice to kill or spare him.
