'''Gloria's Fashion Boutique''' is a clothing store located on [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] is ''555-0117'', which results in a recorded message playing asking the caller to call again later.
