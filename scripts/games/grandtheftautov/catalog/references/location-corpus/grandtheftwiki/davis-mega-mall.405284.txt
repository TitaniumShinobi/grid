[[LEAD]]
The '''Davis Mega Mall''' is a shopping mall located at the corner of [[Davis Ave]] and [[Carson Ave]] in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. [[Carson Self Storage]] and [[Discount Jewels]] are the only businesses known to be at the mall. The malls car park is occasionally used by non-playable characters.
