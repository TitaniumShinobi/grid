'''Occupation Ave''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs from [[Alta]] in the west to [[Burton]] in the east with connections to [[Alta St]], [[Elgin Ave]], [[Las Lagunas Blvd]], [[Meteor St]] and [[Power St]]. The avenue continues as [[Carcer Way]].
