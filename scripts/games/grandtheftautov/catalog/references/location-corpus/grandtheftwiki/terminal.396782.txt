'''Terminal''' is an industrial neighbourhood, consisting primarily of warehouses and containers, in the [[Port of Los Santos]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. [[Floyd Hebert]] works at the Port of Los Santos. He is [[Scouting the Port|briefly]] joined by [[Trevor Philips]] and [[Wade Hebert]] who begin planning a heist.
