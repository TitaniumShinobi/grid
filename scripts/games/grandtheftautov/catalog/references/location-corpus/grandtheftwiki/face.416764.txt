[[LEAD]]
'''Face ''' is a company in [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City]], [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]], [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]] that makes jewelry and watches. The brand is also featured on [[Peepthatshit.com]], and has sponsors on [[Hotring Racer]]s. In GTA V, [[Lazlow Jones|Lazlow]] promotes the brand as one of the official sponsors for the [[Fame or Shame]] television show.
