'''Stab City''' is an unincorporated [[:wp:Recreational vehicle|RV]] park in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Stab City, close to the [[Grand Senora Desert]], is filled with trailers owned by a chapter of [[The Lost Brotherhood]], which moved to Blaine County following the [[The Lost Civil War|Lost Civil War]] in [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] in [[2008]]. Stab City is located off of [[Calafia Rd]].
