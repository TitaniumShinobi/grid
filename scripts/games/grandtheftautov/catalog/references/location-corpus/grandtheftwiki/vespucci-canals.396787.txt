'''Vespucci Canals''' is a neighbourhood consisting of waterways in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Vespucci Canals were added to the Vespucci neighbourhood to add a [[Europe|European]] style area to the city. The housing in the area is reminiscent of [[:wp:Venice|Venice]], [[Italy]].
