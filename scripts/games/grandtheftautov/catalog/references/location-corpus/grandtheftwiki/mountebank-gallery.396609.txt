'''Mountebank Gallery''' is an art gallery on the corner of [[Hawick Ave]] and [[Power St]] in [[Alta]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. A [[Random Events in GTA V|random event]] occurs in an alley at the side of the gallery where a man holds a woman at gun point.
