'''Spinybed''' is a district located in northeast [[Las Venturas]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. It is primarily industrial, as one can see freight containers and abattoirs in many places, and many of the vehicles being driven around are industrial vehicles. It also houses a few residences. To the west of Spinybed is the district of [[Prickle Pine]]. To the east is the [[K.A.C.C.|K.A.C.C. Military Fuels Depot]]. To the south is [[Julius Thruway#Julius Thruway North|Julius Thruway North]] and the district of [[Roca Escalante]]. Its name is a play on words pronouncing "Spiny Bed".
