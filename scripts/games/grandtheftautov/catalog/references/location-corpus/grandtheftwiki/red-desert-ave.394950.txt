[[LEAD]]
'''Red Desert Ave''' is an avenue in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running northeast in [[Backlot City]]/[[Richards Majestic]] to southwest in [[Del Perro Beach]], ending at the [[Del Perro Pier]], appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue has connections with [[Bay City Ave]], [[North Rockford Dr]] and [[South Rockford Dr]] and runs through [[Del Perro]].
