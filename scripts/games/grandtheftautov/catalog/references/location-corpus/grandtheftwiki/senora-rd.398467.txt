[[LEAD]]
'''Senora Rd''' is a street in [[Blaine County]] and [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road starts in [[Harmony]], [[Blaine County]] before running through the [[Grand Senora Desert]], [[Redwood Lights Track]] and the Grand Senora Desert before ending in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. The road has connections to [[Baytree Canyon Rd]], [[Elgin Ave]], [[Joshua Rd]], [[Marlowe Dr]], [[Route 68]] and [[Vinewood Park Dr]].
