[[LEAD]]
'''Hayes Auto Body Shop''' is an auto repair shop located at the corner of [[Carson Ave]] and [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The shop is open 7 days a week, does insurance repairs and gives free estimates.
