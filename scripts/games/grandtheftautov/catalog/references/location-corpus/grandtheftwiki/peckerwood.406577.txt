'''Peckerwood''' is a store located on [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company was founded in [[1908]] with the words "College Style" appearing directly below the company name in its logo.
