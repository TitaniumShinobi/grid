'''Little Mexico''' is a small district located slightly to the east of the city centre of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]]. Surrounding this area are the districts of [[El Corona]], [[Idlewood]] and [[Commerce]]. Also nearby is [[Unity Station]]. Little Mexico is presumably based off of [[:wp:Olvera Street|Olvera Street]] in [[:wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]]. The district is controlled by the [[Varrios Los Aztecas]] and there are no known residents in Little Mexico.
