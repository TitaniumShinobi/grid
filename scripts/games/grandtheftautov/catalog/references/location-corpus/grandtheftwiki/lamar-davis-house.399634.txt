'''Lamar Davis' House''' is a one-storey house owned by [[The Families|Families]] gang member [[Lamar Davis]] and his dog [[Chop]], and is located on [[Forum Dr]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. During the mission [[Chop (mission)|Chop]], Lamar and [[Franklin Clinton]] were returning to Lamar's house with [[D]], a [[Ballas in GTA V|Ballas]] gang member they had kidnapped, before having to let him go. This results in Lamar asking Franklin to take care of Chop who initially moves to Franklin's [[Strawberry Safehouse|home]] and later to [[3671 Whispymound Dr]] in [[Vinewood Hills]].
