[[LEAD]]
'''White Widow''' is a clothing company in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is featured on [[Peepthatshit.com]] in the "Street Fashion" section, and is described on [[Designerslave.com]] as being owned by [[Sub Urban|Sub Urban Clothing]] and being popular with "''aging hipsters''". The brand's name comes from [[wp:White widow (cannabis)|a strain of cannabis]]. A store can be found at the corner of [[Hawick Ave]] and [[Power St]] in [[Alta]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]].
