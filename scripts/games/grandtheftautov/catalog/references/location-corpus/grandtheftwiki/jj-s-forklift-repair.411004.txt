'''JJ's Forklift Repair''' is an [[Forklift]] repair shop located on [[Crusade Rd]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company phone number is ''1-999-FORKUPS'' (a reference to ''fuck up''), which will go straight to voice mail.
