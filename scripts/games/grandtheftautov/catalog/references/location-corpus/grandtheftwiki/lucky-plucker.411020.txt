'''Lucky Plucker''' is a fast food restaurant chain, specialising in fried chicken, which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are restaurants at the corner of [[Palomino Ave]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]] in [[Little Seoul]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]].
