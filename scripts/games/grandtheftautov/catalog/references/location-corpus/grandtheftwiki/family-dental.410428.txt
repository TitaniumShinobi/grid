'''Family Dental''' is a dental surgery located at the corner of [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] for the surgery is ''555-1107'', which will always be busy. The surgery is headed by Dr. [[P. McCavity]] D.D.S.
