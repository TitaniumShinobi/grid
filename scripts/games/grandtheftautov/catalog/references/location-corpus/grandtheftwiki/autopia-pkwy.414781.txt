[[LEAD]]
'''Autopia Pkwy''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The parkway runs from [[Davis Ave]] in [[Davis]] to [[Greenwich Pkwy]] in [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]], running through [[Banning]], [[Maze Bank Arena]], [[Elysian Island]] and [[La Puerta]]. There are two other connections with [[Dutch London St]] and [[Exceptionalists Way]].
