'''Los Flores''' is a small district in eastern [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]], modeled after [[:wp:Boyle Heights|Boyle Heights]]. It is controlled by the [[Los Santos Vagos]]. There are no specific residents of Los Flores, along with no weapons, stationary vehicles, icons or any other things of interest, besides a couple of [[Collectibles|collectibles]], which are needed for [[Completion in GTA San Andreas|completion]]. The main roads of Los Flores are elevated. Part of it is also higher than the rest of the district. Los Flores is a combination of small time businesses and low quality apartments and houses, garages and a small bridge. Los Flores means The Flowers in Spanish.
