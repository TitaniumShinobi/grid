The '''Vanilla Unicorn''' is a [[strip club]] located in the [[Strawberry]] neighbourhood of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is the only accessible strip club in the game. The name is a parody of [[wp:Spearmint Rhino|Spearmint Rhino]], a real-life strip club chain.

As with [[The Triangle Club]] and [[Honkers Gentlemen's Club]] in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], the player can solicit a private dance from any stripper; however, if the player acts out of line, he will be thrown out by the club's security. Additionally, the player can take certain strippers home for a private booty call if their "like" statistic is raised to a sufficient level; buying private dances and flirting with the strippers raises their like statistic.
