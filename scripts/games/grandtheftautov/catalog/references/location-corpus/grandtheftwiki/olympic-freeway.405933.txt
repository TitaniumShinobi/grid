[[LEAD]]
The Olympic Freeway is a major highway in Los Santos which [[Interstate 4]] takes the Olympic Freeway designation. It is the border between [[Downtown Los Santos]] and [[South Los Santos]]. It is a eight-lane highway. Its western terminus is at the [[La Puerta Freeway]] [[(Interstate 5)]] junction near Downtown Los Santos. Other major junctions are San Andreas State Route 17 in [[La Mesa]] and its eastern terminus with the [[Del Perro]], Palomino, and Elysian Fields Freeways in [[East Los Santos]]. It is based on The Century Freeway (Interstate 105) in Central Los Angeles.
