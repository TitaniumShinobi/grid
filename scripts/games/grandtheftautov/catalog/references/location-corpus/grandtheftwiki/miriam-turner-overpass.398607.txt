The '''Miriam Turner Overpass''' is a suspension bridge in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]], connecting [[La Puerta]] with [[Elysian Island]]. The bridge is based on the [[wp:Vincent Thomas Bridge|Vincent Thomas Bridge]]. The bridge's namesake, [[Miriam Turner]], was a [[Vinewood in GTA V|Vinewood]] actress whereas [[wp:Vincent Thomas|Vincent Thomas]], the namesake of the bridge's real-life counterpart, was a Los Angeles Area politician who pushed for the bridge's construction.
