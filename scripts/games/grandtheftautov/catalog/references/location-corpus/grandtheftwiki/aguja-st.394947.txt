[[LEAD]]
'''Aguja St''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running from the northeast in [[La Puerta]] to the southwest in [[Vespucci Beach]], briefly going through [[Vespucci Canals]] and [[Vespucci]], appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Bay City Ave]], [[Goma St]] and [[Magellan Ave]]. Part of the street, located in Vespucci Beach, is a pedestrianised area.
