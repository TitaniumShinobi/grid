'''Davis Electricals''' is an electronics store, selling new items and buying second hand electronics for sale later, located on [[Carson Ave]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company phone number is ''[[Phone Numbers|384-555-0144]]'' with the player receiving a "We're not here. Call back later!" voice mail message.
