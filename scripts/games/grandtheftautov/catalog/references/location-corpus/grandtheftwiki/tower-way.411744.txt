'''Tower Way''' is a street, running northwest to southeast, from [[La Mesa]] in the northwest through [[Cypress Flats]] and to [[Murrieta Heights]] in the southeast, which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to the [[Elysian Fields Fwy]] (at both its north and south ends), [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Labor Pl]]. The street runs parallel to both the freeway and [[Amarillo Way]] on the opposite side of the freeway.
