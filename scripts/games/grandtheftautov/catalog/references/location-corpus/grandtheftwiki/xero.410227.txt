[[LEAD]]
'''Xero''' is a company in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]] presumably specialized in petroleum. Unlike its competitors, Xero does not appear to have set up any [[Filling Stations|gas stations]] in [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]], although it does in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. The company operate a refinery at the southwestern corner of the [[Acter Industrial Park]] in [[Alderney]], a building at [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]], and a gas station at the corner of [[Capital Blvd]] and [[Strawberry Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], Los Santos. Xero storage tanks and faded advertising of the brand can also be found in selected parts of the city, and Xero sponsorship vinyls are visible on certain [[Bati Custom]]s designs. Xero is a [[BAWSAQ]]-listed company.

Its name and logo design appear to be similar to that of [[Xoomer]] in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]].
