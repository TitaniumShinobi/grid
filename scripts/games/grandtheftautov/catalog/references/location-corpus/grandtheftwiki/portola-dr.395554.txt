'''Portola Dr''' is a road running north to southeast in [[Rockford Hills]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Portola Dr is a primarily shopping area with a residential area to its north, where the De Santa family and presumably the Dubose family ([[Hayden Dubose|Hayden]], [[Kimberly Dubose|Kimberly]], [[Melinda Dubose|Melinda]] and other children). The road has connections to [[Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Casers Pl]], [[Carcer Way]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Eastbourne Way]], [[Edwood Way]], [[Palomino Ave]], [[South Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Spanish Ave]] and [[West Eclipse Blvd]]. An alleyway, where Portola Dr meets Eastbourne Way, has a number of shops also considered to be Portola Dr including [[Vangelico's Fine Jewelry]], which is robbed during the mission [[The Jewel Store Job]] in GTA V.
