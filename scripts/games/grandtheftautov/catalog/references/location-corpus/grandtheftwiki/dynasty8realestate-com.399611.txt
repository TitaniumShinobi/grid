'''www.dynasty8realestate.com''' is a real estate website in the [[HD Universe]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. It is interactive in Grand Theft Auto Online, players can go to this site to purchase any properties that are located throughout [[San Andreas in GTA V| Southern San Andreas]].
