'''Cholla Springs Ave''' is a street running southwest to northeast in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Armadillo Ave]], [[Mountain View Dr]], [[Niland Ave]] and [[Panorama Dr]].
