[[LEAD]]
'''Johnsons Realty''' is a real estate agents in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. For sale signs for the company can be found across Los Santos' poorer areas with one employee, [[Angela Recroe]], listed as a contact. The phone number ''310-555-0159''  is listed, which puts the caller through to a man asking people to 'Stop prank calling me!'.
