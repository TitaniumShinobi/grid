'''Mosley Auto Service''' is an automobile dealership and repair shop located at the corners of [[Carson Ave|Carson]], [[Davis Ave|Davis]] and [[Strawberry Ave|Strawberry]] avenues in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The phone number for the business is ''555-0186'', which results in a nervous man stating "Stop prank calling me!" before hanging up. The dealership is [[Blow Up|attacked]] in early [[2013]] by one or two unnamed [[Protagonist (GTA Online)|individual(s)]] with the cars up for sale being destroyed with the damage still visible later in the year.
