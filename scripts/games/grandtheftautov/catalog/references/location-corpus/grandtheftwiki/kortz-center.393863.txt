The '''Kortz Center''' is a complex of cultural buildings located in [[Pacific Bluffs]], northwest of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. It is based on the real life [[wp:Getty Center|Getty Centre]] in [[wp:Brentwood, Los Angeles|Brentwood, Los Angeles]].

The institution is the home of cultural foundations, research centers and museums. There are sculptures, terraces and gardens in its area, as well as a vehicle parking and a labyrinth.

[[SECTION:2:Structure]]
There are four main buildings in this facility:

* Low Rotunda
* Bell Building
* Biranda Building
* Moseley Building
