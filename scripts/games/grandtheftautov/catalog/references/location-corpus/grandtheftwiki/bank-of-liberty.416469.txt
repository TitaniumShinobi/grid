[[LEAD]]
The '''Bank of Liberty''' is a chain of banks in both the [[3D Universe]] and the [[HD Universe]]. The bank appears in [[Grand Theft Auto III]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]] and [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is a parody of [[wp:Bank of America|Bank of America]] as it has the same colors and also a parody of [[wp:Sovereign Bank|Sovereign Bank]] as the text is the same and also a logo to the left of the title. 

In GTA IV, there are three branches in [[Algonquin]] and several more throughout the rest of the [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]]. Its main branch is located on [[Columbus Ave]] and Calcium Street in [[The Exchange]]/[[Chinatown, Algonquin|Chinatown]] neighborhood of the borough of Algonquin. Presumably, [[Gay Tony]]'s personal and business accounts are held at the Bank of Liberty as Luis was in the bank when the [[Irish]] [[I Luv LC|robbed it]].

In GTA V, [[Trevor Philips]]' bank account is held at the Bank of Liberty.
