'''New Empire Way''' is a street in [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Davis Ave]] and [[Greenwich Pkwy]]. The street runs to the airports depatures and arrivals terminals.
