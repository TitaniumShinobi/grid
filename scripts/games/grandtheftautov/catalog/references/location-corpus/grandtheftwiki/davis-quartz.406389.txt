'''Davis Quartz''' is a quarry located in the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. [[Merryweather Security Consulting]] personnel patrol part of the quarry although they do not attempt to stop the player when entering the quarry. The quarry appears to be inactive as no work can be seen taking place.
