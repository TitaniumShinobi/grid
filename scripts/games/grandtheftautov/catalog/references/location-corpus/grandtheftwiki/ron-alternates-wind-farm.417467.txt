The '''RON Alternates Wind Farm''' is a wind farm located close to the [[Palmer-Taylor Power Station]] located in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The wind farm is owned by the [[RON]] power company and maintained by the [[Los Santos Department of Wind Power]].
