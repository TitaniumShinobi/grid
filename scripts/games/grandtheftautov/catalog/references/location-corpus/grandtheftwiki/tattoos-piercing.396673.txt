'''Tattoos Piercing''', also known as '''Vespucci Tattoos''', is a [[:Category:Tattoo Parlors|tattoo parlor]] and body piercing shop located on [[Melanoma St]] in [[Vespucci Beach]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The player can not use the tattoo parlor to acquire tattoos.
