[[LEAD]]
'''Palomino Ave''' is a north-southwest avenue in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue starts in [[Rockford Hills]] in the north running through [[Little Seoul]], [[Vespucci Canals]] and [[Vespucci]] before ending in [[Vespucci Beach]] in the south. The street has connections to [[Bay City Ave]], [[Cancer Way]], the [[Del Perro Freeway]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Ginger St]], [[Goma St]], [[Lindsay Circus]], [[Magellan Ave]], [[Portola Dr]], [[Prosperity St]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[South Rockford Dr]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]].
