'''Fudge Ln''' is a small residential street, running southwest to northeast, in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The lane has connections to [[Innocence Blvd]], [[El Rancho Blvd]] (which it crosses)  and [[Sustancia Rd]]. The [[Marabunta Grande]] control the street.
