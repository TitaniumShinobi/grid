[[LEAD]]
The Vinewood Racetrack is a horse racing track in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is located in [[East Vinewood]], on the northeast side of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. It is based on the now-demolished [[wp:Hollywood_Park_Racetrack|Hollywood Park Racetrack]] in Inglewood, California. The [[Vinewood Casino]] is adjacent to the racetrack.
