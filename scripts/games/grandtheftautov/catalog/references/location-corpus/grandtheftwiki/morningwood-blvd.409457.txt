'''Morningwood Blvd''' is a boulevard in [[Morningwood]], [[Rockford Hills]] and [[Backlot City|Richards Majestic]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are connections to [[Boulevard Del Perro]], [[Cougar Ave]], [[Marathon Ave]], [[Perth St]] and [[West Eclipse Blvd]].
