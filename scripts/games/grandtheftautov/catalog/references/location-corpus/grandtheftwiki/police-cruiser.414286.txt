[[LEAD]]
The [[Vapid]] '''Police Cruiser''' is one of the two main [[Police Car|police cars]] used by the [[LCPD in GTA IV Era|Liberty City Police Department]] in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], the other being the [[Police Patrol]]. The Police Cruiser also refers to a [[Buffalo]]-based LCPD police car featured in [[The Ballad of Gay Tony]] (see [[#The Ballad of Gay Tony]]). The Police Cruiser reappears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]].
