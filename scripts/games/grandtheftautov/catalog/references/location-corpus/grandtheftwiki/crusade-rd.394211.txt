'''Crusade Rd''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] running from [[Davis]] in the southwest to [[Strawberry]] in the northeast. The road has connections to [[Capital Blvd]], [[Innocence Blvd]] and [[Little Bighorn Ave]].
