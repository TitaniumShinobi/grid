'''Tits N Bobs''' is an adult themed restaurant located on [[Capital Blvd]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company promises 'dinner and light relief' with the company [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] being ''337-555-0169'', which results in the caller being asked to phone back.
