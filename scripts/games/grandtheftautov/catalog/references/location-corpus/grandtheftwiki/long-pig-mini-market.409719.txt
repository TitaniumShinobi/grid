'''Long Pig Mini Market''' is a small [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] based convenience store chain, which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are stores on [[Alta St]] in [[Strawberry]] and at the corner of [[Jamestown St]] and [[Little Bighorn Ave]] in [[Rancho]].
