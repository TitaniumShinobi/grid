'''Paleto Bay''' is a small town in northern [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The town is located close to the nearby tourist attraction [[Mount Chiliad in GTA V|Mount Chiliad]] while the beach, served by [[Procopio Promenade]], and a [[Paleto Forest|forest]] are also nearby. The town is located four hours away from the nearest city, [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]].
