[[LEAD]]
'''Rancho''' is a primarily Hispanic neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Rancho has borders to [[Strawberry]] to the north, [[La Mesa]] to the east, [[Cypress Flats]] to the south east, [[Banning]] to the south and [[Davis]] to the west.
