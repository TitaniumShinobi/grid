'''Downtown''' is the central business district of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas]] that appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The district is based on real-life [[:wp:Downtown Los Angeles|Downtown Los Angeles]]. Like [[Downtown Los Santos in GTA III Era|its counterpart]] in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]], Downtown is likely to include the city's tallest buildings, numerous businesses, and government offices. Major [[highways]] are also likely to pass through Downtown.
