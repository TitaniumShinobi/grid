[[LEAD]]
'''Discount Beauty Store''' is a discount store for beauty products with branches in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are stores on the corner of [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] and [[Macdonald St]] in [[Rancho]], [[Alta St]] and [[Innocence Blvd]] in [[La Puerta]], & [[Clinton Ave]] and [[Fenwell Pl]] in [[Downtown Vinewood]]. The stores in Downtown Vinewood and La Puerta both have a [[Sprunk]] vending machine outside the store.
