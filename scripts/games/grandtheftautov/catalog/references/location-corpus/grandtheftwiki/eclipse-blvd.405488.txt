'''Eclipse Blvd''' is a street in [[West Vinewood]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Eclipse Blvd is a continuation of [[West Eclipse Blvd]] (from the corner with [[South Mo Milton Dr]]) and then continues as [[Vinewood Blvd]] (from the corner with [[Las Lagunas Blvd]]. There are also connections to [[Gentry Lane]], [[Milton Rd]] and [[San Vitus Blvd]].
