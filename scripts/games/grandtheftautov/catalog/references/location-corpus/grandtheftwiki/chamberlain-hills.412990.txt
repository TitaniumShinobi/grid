[[LEAD]]
'''Chamberlain Hills''' is a low-income and gang controlled residential neighbourhood in southern [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The neighbourhood is bordered by [[Strawberry]] in the north, [[Davis]] to the east, [[Maze Bank Arena]] in the south and [[La Puerta]] in the west.
