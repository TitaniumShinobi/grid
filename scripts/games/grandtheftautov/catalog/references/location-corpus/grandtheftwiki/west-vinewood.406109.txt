'''West Vinewood''' is a residential and commercial neighbourhood in the city of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Los Santos County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. A number of night clubs and bars can be found in the area and is also a location to pick up [[prostitutes]]. West Vinewood is a tourist hotspot because the Eclipse Strip is located in the neighbourhood on Eclipse Boulevard. It is also the most populated area in Vinewood because of the numerous apartment buildings and complexes.
