'''Tackle St''' is a street in [[La Puerta]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] leading to [[Puerto Del Sol]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has only one connection, [[South Rockford Dr]]. The street consists of a number of small marina jetties for docking small boats, a yacht club and two apartment buildings.
