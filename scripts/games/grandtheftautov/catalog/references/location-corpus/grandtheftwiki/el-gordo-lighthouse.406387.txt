'''El Gordo Lighthouse''' is a lighthouse located in northern [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The lighthouse is located close to [[Cape Catfish]] and was featured in a video for the [[Epsilon Program]]. [[Ursula]], a [[Random Encounter/Ursula|random character]], lives in a house located near the lighthouse, which she inherited after her mothers death. Ursula also tells the [[protagonist]] involved in her random encounter that a gardener visited her a number of times a week before falling off a cliff, which may have been due to her actions.
