'''Rodeo''' is a district to the west of the city center of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]], and is not controlled by any of the four gangs based in Los Santos. North of Rodeo are the districts of [[Vinewood in GTA III Era|Vinewood]] and [[Richman, Los Santos|Richman]], while to the south is [[Santa Maria Beach]]. West of Rodeo is [[Flint County]], while to the east are [[Marina, Los Santos|Marina]] and [[Verona Beach]]. It is based on the real-life Rodeo Drive in Beverly Hills. Rodeo is the most expensive shopping district in San Andreas.
