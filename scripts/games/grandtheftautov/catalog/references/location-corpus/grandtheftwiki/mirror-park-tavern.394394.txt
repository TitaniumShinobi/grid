'''Mirror Park Tavern''' is a [[:Category:Bars|bar]] located on [[Mirror Park Blvd]] in [[Mirror Park]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The tavern has a 'Happy Hour' on Monday to Friday between 5 and 8.
