[[LEAD]]
'''West Eclipse Blvd''' is a boulevard in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The boulevard begins in [[Pacific Bluffs]] before travelling northwest through [[Morningwood]] and [[Richman in GTA V|Richman]] before ending in [[Rockford Hills]]. The boulevard has connections to [[Americano Way]], [[Bay City Ave]], [[Del Perro Freeway]], [[Dorset Dr]], [[Dunstable Dr]], [[Eclipse Blvd]], [[Great Ocean Highway]], [[Greenwich Pl]], [[Handy Way]], [[Mad Wayne Thunder Dr]], [[Morningwood Blvd]], [[North Rockford Dr]], [[Playa Vista]], [[Portola Dr]], [[Prosperity St]], [[Rockford Dr]], [[Sam Austin Dr]], [[South Mo Milton Dr]], [[South Rockford Dr]] and [[Steele Way]].
