'''El Corona''' is a district slightly to the south-east of the city centre of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]]. El Corona is the main strong hold for the [[Varrios Los Aztecas]] gang, and there are five known residents, [[Cesar Vialpando]], [[Gal]], [[Hazer]], [[Sunny]] and [[Kendl Johnson]] (who moves to El Corona to live with Cesar Vialpando).

El Corona can be described as a working-class, poor and gang-ridden district, as evidenced by the gang presence, some stores and the numerous houses in the district. The majority of the areas social problems primarily stems from the gang activity and gang violence. El Corona is modeled after [[wp:El Sereno, Los Angeles|El Sereno]]. Surrounding El Corona are the districts of [[Little Mexico]], [[Verdant Bluffs]], [[Idlewood]] and [[Willowfield]]. Also nearby is the [[Los Santos International Airport]].
