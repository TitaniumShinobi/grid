[[LEAD]]
'''Soulwax FM''' is a radio station in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] featuring house and dance music, mainly dance-punk and fidget house. The station focuses on playing various tracks from Europe, most notably Belgium and neighboring countries (France, Netherlands, and Germany).

'''DJ''': [[Soulwax]]

'''Genres''': [[wp:Dance-punk|dance-punk]], [[wp:Deep house|deep house]], [[wp:Electro house|fidget house]]
