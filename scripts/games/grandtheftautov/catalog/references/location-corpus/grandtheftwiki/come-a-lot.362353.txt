'''Come-A-Lot''' is a large medieval themed [[casino]] along [[The Strip]] in [[Las Venturas]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]], based on the [[:wp:Excalibur Hotel and Casino|Excalibur Hotel and Casino]] in Las Vegas. The casino is inaccessible to the player. Come-A-Lot's name is a pun on Camelot, the realm associated with King Arthur, along with Excalibur, thus the origin of the name for both casinos. The name may also be a [[:wp:double entendre|double entendre]] of "cum a lot", a sexual phrase referring to excessive ejaculation.
