[[LEAD]]
'''Go Postal''' is a package delivery company operating in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The company is the sister company of the rail operator [[Go Loco]]. The company's name and slogan ("''We aim not to lose it''") are a reference to the term ''[[:wp:going postal|going postal]]'', and their [[Boxville|vans]] share a colour scheme with the [[:wp:USPS|United States Postal Service]] and [[:wp:Fedex|Fedex]].
