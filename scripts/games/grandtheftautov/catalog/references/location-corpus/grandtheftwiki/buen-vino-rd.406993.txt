[[LEAD]]
'''Buen Vino Rd''' is a road in [[Los Santos County]], starting in [[Banham Canyon]] although the majority of the road is in [[Tongva Hills]], appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are connections to [[Banham Canyon Dr]] and [[Route 68]]. The only business located on the road is [[Marlowe Vineyard]] and the only known resident is [[Devin Weston]] at his [[Devin Weston's House|large hillside house]].
