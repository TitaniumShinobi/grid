'''East Vinewood''' is a middle class neighbourhood in the larger [[Vinewood in GTA V|Vinewood]] neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. New houses are being built in the neighbourhood, which has a number of family houses.
