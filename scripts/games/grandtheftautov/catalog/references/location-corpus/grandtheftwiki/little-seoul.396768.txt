'''Little Seoul''' is a neighborhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The neighbourhood is influenced by [[South Korea|Korean]] culture and many Asian businesses can be found in the area. The Korean influence extends to [[United States of America|American]] businesses having their signage written in Korean. The [[Wei Cheng Triad]] have influence the neighbourhood.
