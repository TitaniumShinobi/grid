'''Rockford Hills''' is an affluent residential and commercial neighborhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Rockford Hills contains a large residential area consisting of large houses and a commercial area mainly centered around [[Portola Dr]] with a number of high-end businesses. Here is where the headquarters of the [[Epsilon Program]] and [[Lifeinvader]] as well as the [[Mount Zonah Medical Center]] are situated in. The neighborhood appears in a number of missions featuring [[Michael De Santa]], who (with his family) [[De Santa House|lives]] in the area and their neighbors the Dubose family.
