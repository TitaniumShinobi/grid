'''Laguna Pl''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] that runs from [[Hawick]] in the south to [[West Vinewood]] in the north, which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Hawick Ave]] and [[Spanish Ave]].
