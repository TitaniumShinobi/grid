[[LEAD]]
'''Mexican and American''' is a [[:Category:Restaurants|restaurant]] in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company has locations at the corner of [[Dutch London St]] and [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] in [[Rancho]], the corner of [[El Rancho Blvd]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]] in [[Murrieta Heights]], on [[Davis Ave]] in [[Rancho]] (attached to a [[Hearty Taco]]) and on [[West Mirror Dr]] in [[Mirror Park]]. The stores are open twenty-four hours a day and serve breakfast from 5-11am.
