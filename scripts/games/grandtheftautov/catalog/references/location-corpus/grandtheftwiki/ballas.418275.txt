[[LEAD]]
The '''Ballas''' are a Los Santos based street gang that appears in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]].

In GTA San Andreas, they act as the primary and first antagonistic group during the early game. During the beginning, the Ballas are the most powerful and influential street gang in Los Santos.

In GTA V, they dress the same as they did in GTA San Andreas, but only attack the player if they have been taunted or if one shoots a gun in their territory. They draw significant police presence, and driving through their territory is a good way to distract police following you and escape a [[Wanted Level in GTA V|wanted level]]. The Ballas territory in GTA V is the entire neighborhood of [[Davis]].
