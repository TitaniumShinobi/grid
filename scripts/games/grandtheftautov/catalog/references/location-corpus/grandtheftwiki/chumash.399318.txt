'''Chumash''' is a town north of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] in [[Los Santos County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The town, on the coast, appears to be largely tourist based and is a wealthy area.
