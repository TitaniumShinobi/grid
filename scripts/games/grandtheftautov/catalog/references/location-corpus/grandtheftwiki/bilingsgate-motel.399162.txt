[[LEAD]]
The '''Bilingsgate Motel''' is a [[:Category:Motels|motel]] located on [[Innocence Blvd]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. [[Trevor Philips]] meets former real estate agent [[Josh Bernstein]] at the motel and has sex with Bernstein's wife after destroying signs from Bernstein's former partner [[Lenny Avery]] and returns for the same purpose after [[Closing the Deal|beating up]] Avery. An [[eCola]] vending machine, which the player can purchase a soda from, can be found at the motel. The motel also offers a [[Badger]] public payphone, a [[Candy Box]] vending machine and colour televisions.
