'''Exceptionalists Way''' is a street, running northeast to southwest, in [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[Autopia Pkwy]], [[Greenwich Pkwy]] and [[New Empire Way]].
