[[LEAD]]
'''Ponsonbys''' is a high-class clothing brand featured in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]], with stores located on [[Portola Dr]]ive, [[Burton]] and [[Morningwood]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. Its name is a play on "ponce", a term for an effeminate man.

The shops are accessible to the player, and several expensive clothing can be bought from the stores. The brand was first seen in the GTA V Gameplay Trailer.

[[SECTION:2:Locations in Los Santos]]
Ponsonbys has three locations in the [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] metropolitan area;
*[[Rockford Hills]]
*[[Burton]]
*[[Morningwood]]
