'''Fenwell Pl''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs from [[Vinewood Hills]] to [[Downtown Vinewood]]. The street has connections to [[Clinton Ave]], [[North Conker Ave]] and [[Vinewood Park Dr]].
