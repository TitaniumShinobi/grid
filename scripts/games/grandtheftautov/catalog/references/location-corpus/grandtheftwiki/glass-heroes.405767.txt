'''Glass Heroes''' is an auto repair shop in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There is a Glass Heroes on [[Innocence Blvd]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. [[Franklin Clinton]] tows a car, owned by [[Phil (GTA V)|Phil]], to the business during [[Pulling Favors Again]] after recovering the broken down vehicle from [[Plaice Pl]]. Franklin later meets [[Lamar Davis]] and [[Trevor Philips]] at the repair shop during [[Pack Man]] before heading towards [[Paleto Bay]] to deliver some cars for [[Devin Weston]].
