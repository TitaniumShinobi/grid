'''Rockford Plaza''' is a large shopping mall in located in [[Rockford Hills]] in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. It is based on [[wp:Beverly Center|Beverly Center]]. The mall features several stores; however, the [[Ponsonbys]] on the [[Las Lagunas Blvd]] side of the mall is the only store that is accessible to the player. A portion of [[Occupation Ave]] runs directly under the mall's footprint.
