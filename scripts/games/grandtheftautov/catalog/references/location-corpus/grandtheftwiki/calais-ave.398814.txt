[[LEAD]]
'''Calais Ave''' is an avenue running north-south from [[Little Seoul]], in the north, to [[La Puerta]], in the south, in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue has connections to [[Adam's Apple Blvd]], [[Innocence Blvd]], [[Dutch London St]], [[Lindsay Circus]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[South Rockford Dr]] and [[Vespucci Blvd]].
