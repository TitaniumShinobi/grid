[[LEAD]]
'''Kortz Dr''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The only connection is to [[North Rockford Dr]] as the street ends at the [[Kortz Center]]. The street begins in [[Richman in GTA V|Richman]] and travels through [[Pacific Bluffs]] and [[Banham Canyon]] before ending in Pacific Bluffs.
