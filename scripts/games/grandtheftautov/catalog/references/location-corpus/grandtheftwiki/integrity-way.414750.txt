[[LEAD]]
'''Integrity Way''' is a street in [[Textile City]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. There are connections to [[Little Bighorn Ave]], [[Low Power St]], [[Simmet Alley]], [[Sinner St]] and [[Strawberry Ave]]. The street runs underneath [[Swiss St]].
