'''Mount Zonah Medical Center''' is a [[hospital]] in [[GTA V]], located somewhere in Western [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. It is clearly one of the main hospitals in Los Santos, and is implied to have high-quality care. The hospital also prescribes medicinal marijuana, as well. The hospital is clearly based on [[wp:Cedars-Sinai Medical Center|Cedars-Sinai Medical Center]] located in the Beverly Hills in Los Angeles. [[Isiah Friedlander]] works for the hospital.
