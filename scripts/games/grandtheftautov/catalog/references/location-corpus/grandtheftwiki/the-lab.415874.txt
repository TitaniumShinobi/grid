[[LEAD]]
'''The Lab''' is a radio station in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is hosted by Alchemist and Oh No of [[wp:Gangrene_(group)|Gangrene]] fame.

All songs on the station will be released on the album "''Alchemist & Oh No Present: Welcome to Los Santos''" published by Mass Appeal Records. Initially exclusive to the PC version, it was added to all versions with part 2 of the  [[Ill-Gotten Gains Update]].

'''DJs''': Alchemist & Oh No

'''Genres''': Alternative Hip-Hop, Dancehall, Electronica, Indie Rock, R&B
