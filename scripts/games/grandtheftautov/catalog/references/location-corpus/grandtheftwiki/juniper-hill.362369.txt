'''Juniper Hill''' is a district of [[San Fierro]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. It is a small neighborhood sandwiched between [[Palisades]] to the west, [[Chinatown, San Fierro|Chinatown]] and [[Calton Heights]] to the east, [[Santa Flora]] to the south, and [[Paradiso]] and [[Juniper Hollow]] to the north.  It is highly affluent, and its residents are friendly, but aggressive. Juniper Hill is modeled after [[:wp:San Francisco|San Francisco's]] [[:wp:Russian Hill, San Francisco, California|Russian Hill]], which is a wealthy neighborhood just east of Pacific Heights, the city's most affluent district.  Russian Hill is the area in which Lombard Street (known as the "crookedest street") zig-zags for one block, providing one of the city's most famous landmarks.  Interestingly, the San Fierro version of this landmark, [[Windy Windy Windy Windy Street]], has been re-created in neighboring Calton Heights instead.

Occasionally, members of [[Wu Zi Mu|Wu Zi Mu's]] Mountain Cloud Boys [[San Fierro Triads|Triad]] can be seen in this neighborhood, as Chinatown and Calton Heights sits nearby, which are both Triad turf. These polite gang members will not hesitate to shoot [[AK-47|AK-47s]] at anyone engaging in criminal activity on their turf, even to their own affiliate, [[Carl Johnson]].
