'''Union Rd''' is a road in [[Grapeseed]] and the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] in [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road is a continuation of [[East Joshua Rd]], on the other side of the [[Senora Fwy]] in San Chianski Mountain Range, with other connections to [[Chianski Passage]], [[Grapeseed Ave]], [[Catfish View]] and [[O'Neil Way]], before ending at [[Joad Ln]] and [[Grapeseed Main St]] in Grapeseed.
