'''Barbareno Rd''' is a road in the town of [[Chumash]] in [[Los Santos County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road connects to the [[Great Ocean Hwy]] at both ends of the road. Barbareno Rd is an almost entirely residential road with the houses located on the beach front as well as a [[24/7]] and a [[Chumash Historic Family Pier|pier]].
