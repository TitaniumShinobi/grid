[[LEAD]]
'''South Mo Milton Dr''' is a road in northern [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road begins in [[Vinewood Hills]] before heading southeast into [[Rockford Hills]] and ending in [[West Vinewood]]. The road has connections to [[Dunstable Ln]], [[North Sheldon Ave]], [[Picture Perfect Drive]] and [[West Eclipse Blvd]]. An apartment on the road can be purchased by the [[Protagonist (GTA Online)|player character]] while playing GTA Online through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]].
