The '''Los Santos Benders''' are a professional soccer club in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. They are apparently based on the [[wp:Los Angeles Galaxy|Los Angeles Galaxy]] of [[wp:Major League Soccer|Major League Soccer]]. In radio advertisements, season tickets for Benders home games start at $50; also, Benders fans are infamous for English-style [[wp:Football_hooliganism|soccer hooliganism]]. Like the [[Los Santos Corkers]], [[The Feud]], and [[The Boars]], there is no explicit stadium in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] or [[San Andreas in GTA V|Southern San Andreas]] to house the team.
