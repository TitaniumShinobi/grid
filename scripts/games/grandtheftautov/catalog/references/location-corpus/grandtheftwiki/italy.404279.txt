'''Italy''', officially the '''Republic of Italy''', is a southern [[Europe]]an country and member of the European Union. Italy is bordered by [[France]], [[Switzerland]], [[Austria]] and Slovenia, while San Marino and the Vatican are enclaves within Italy. Italy consists of the Italian peninsula, Sicily, Sardinia, a number of smaller islands and the Campione d'Italia exclave.
