[[LEAD]]
'''Auto Re-Perez''' is an auto repair shop located at the corner of [[Crusade Rd]] and [[Little Bighorn Ave]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]. The company [[Phone Numbers|phone number]] is ''284-555-0139'', which will result in music being played as if on hold. The player cannot repair or modify their vehicles at the garage.
