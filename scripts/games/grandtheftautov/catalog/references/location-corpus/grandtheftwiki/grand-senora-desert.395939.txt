The '''Grand Senora Desert''' is a desert located in [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The town of [[Sandy Shores]] is located inside the desert, which is bordered by the [[Alamo Sea]] in the north and the [[Great Chaparral]] in the south. The deserts climate is primarily arid with occassional but powerful rain and thunderstorms, which will always occur during the mission [[Friends Reunited]].
