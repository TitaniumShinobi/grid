'''South LS Liquor''' is a liquor store located on the corner of [[Carson Ave]] and [[Dutch London St]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. A [[Soapboxxers|soapboxxer]] from [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] can be found asking passers to repent for their life of depravity and claiming to be their salvation can be found outside the store.
