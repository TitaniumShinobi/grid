'''Procopio Beach''' is a beach located in northern [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The beach begins at [[North Point]], to the north of [[Paleto Bay]], in the east and runs to the western edge of [[Mount Gordo]]. It is also bordered by the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the north, and [[Paleto Bay]] and [[Mount Chiliad in GTA V|Mount Chiliad]] to the south. The [[Procopio Truck Stop]] provides easy access to the beach from the [[Great Ocean Hwy]]. The beach is a popular destination for tourists and locals, with a number of people found along the beach during the day and evening.
