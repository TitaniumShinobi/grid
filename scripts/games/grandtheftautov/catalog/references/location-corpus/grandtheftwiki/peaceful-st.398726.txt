'''Peaceful St''' is a street in [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], running southwest to northeast, appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street has connections to [[San Andreas Ave]] in the northeast and [[Vespucci Blvd]] in the southwest.
