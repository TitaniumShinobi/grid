[[LEAD]]
'''Jetsam''' is a shipping company appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The company has a large office, entitled ''Jetsam Terminal'' in [[Terminal]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], a smaller office on [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]] and a warehouse at [[Los Santos International Airport in GTA V|Los Santos International Airport]]. The company likely exports to [[Canada]], [[France]], [[Ireland]], [[Mexico]], [[Russia]] and the [[United Kingdom]] as the flags of these countries are located at the Jetsam Terminal building. Shipping containers with the company logo can be found across the [[Port of Los Santos]].

The company likely transports dangerous chemicals with a sign at the Jetsam Terminal building reading that "This facility contains chemicals known to the State of Los Santos to cause cancer, birth defects or other reproductive harm". [[Chihuahua Hotdogs]] and [[Beefy Bills Burger Bar]] have vendors at the Jetsam Terminal building while there are also vending machines for [[Bean Machine]], [[Candybox]], [[eCola]] (x3), [[Raine]] and [[Sprunk]] with the player being able to purchase from the eCola and Sprunk machines.
