'''Marina''' is a district in western [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. The main feature of the neighborhood is a small canal, that as the area's name suggests is used as a marina. The canal eventually flows into the sea. Here, there are numerous businesses and residences, making it an ideal place to live in. Marina is not controlled by any Los Santos-based gang, although surrounding districts like [[Santa Maria Beach]] and [[Verona Beach]] have prominent gang activity going on.

Besides Santa Maria Beach and Verona Beach, the commercial neighbourhoods of [[Vinewood in GTA III Era|Vinewood]], [[Market, Los Santos|Market]] and [[Rodeo]] also envelop the area.
