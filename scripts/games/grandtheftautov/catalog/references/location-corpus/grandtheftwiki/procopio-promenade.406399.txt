[[LEAD]]
'''Procopio Promenade''' is a street running from [[Paleto Forest]] in the south to [[Paleto Bay]] in the north, which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. A wooden bridge is under construction/repair as of [[2013]] and acts as a stunt jump. The only connection is to the [[Great Ocean Highway]] at both ends of the road.
