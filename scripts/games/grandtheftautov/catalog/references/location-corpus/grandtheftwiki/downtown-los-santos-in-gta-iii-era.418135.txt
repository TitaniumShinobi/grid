'''Downtown Los Santos''' is a district of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]], and is modeled after Downtown [[wp:Los Angeles|Los Angeles]]. This is the location of the city's tallest structures, as well as numerous small businesses and public facilities.  Downtown Los Santos sits just north of [[Pershing Square]], the location of the city's municipal government. Also adjacent to the area are the districts of [[Temple]], [[Market, Los Santos|Market]], [[Commerce]], and [[Glen Park]].

The major [[Highways|highway]] that connects [[Los Santos International Airport]] (LSX) with [[Las Venturas]] bisects Downtown Los Santos into eastern and western portions, and establishes a connection with the highway servicing [[Ganton]], [[Jefferson, Los Santos|Jefferson]], and [[East Beach]] just south of Downtown. Just north of downtown is the [[Mulholland Intersection]], connecting the LSX-Las Venturas highway with the [[Mulholland Drive|boulevard]] servicing [[Rodeo]], [[Richman, Los Santos|Richman]], [[Vinewood in GTA III Era|Vinewood]], [[Mulholland]], [[Temple]], Glen Park, [[Jefferson, Los Santos|Jefferson]], [[Las Colinas]], [[Los Flores]], [[East Beach]] and [[East Los Santos in GTA III Era|East Los Santos]].
