[[LEAD]]
'''Alphabetz''' is a company that manufactures footwear in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The company has stores on [[Kunzite St]] in [[Star Junction]] and [[Nickel St]], [[Middle Park East]], both in [[Algonquin]], [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]], and on [[Magellan Ave]] in [[Vespucci]] and [[Hawick Ave]] in [[Hawick]], both in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]].
