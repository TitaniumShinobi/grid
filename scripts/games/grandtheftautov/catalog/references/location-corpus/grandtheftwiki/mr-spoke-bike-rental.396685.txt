[[LEAD]]
'''Mr. Spoke Bike Rental''' is a vehicle rental stand located on the boardwalk of [[Vespucci Beach]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The stand rents out bicycles, quad bikes and surf boards at an hourly or daily rate. [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] and [[Jimmy De Santa]] both rent bicycles from the stand during the mission [[Daddy's Little Girl]] before riding them to the end of [[Pleasure Pier]].
