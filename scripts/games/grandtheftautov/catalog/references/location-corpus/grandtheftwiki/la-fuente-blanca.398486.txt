'''La Fuente Blanca''' is a large ranch owned by [[Martin Madrazo]], head of the [[Madrazo Cartel]], located on [[Senora Rd]] in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. La Fuente Blanca has a large ranch house where Martin and his wife [[Patricia Madrazo|Patricia]] reside. Martin's men and nephew [[Javier Madrazo|Javier]] can be seen guarding the house, although Javier can no longer be seen after being killed during the mission [[Caida Libre]]. The ranch also features a number of stables, a horse riding and horse training area, a one-hole golf course, a water tower and a series of pig pens while a pool and jacuzzi can be found in the back garden. A [[Sprunk]] and an [[eCola]] vending machine can be found on the ranch and can be used to recover health. The is also the main territory held by the Madrazo Cartel in [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]].
