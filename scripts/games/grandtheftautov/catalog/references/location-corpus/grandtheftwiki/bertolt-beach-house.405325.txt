The '''Bertolt Beach House''' is a beach front house located in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Dr. [[Isiah Friedlander]] has his [[Isiah Friedlander's Office|office]] located in the building where [[Michael De Santa]] attends therapy sessions. After one therapy session, he [[Franklin and Lamar|points out]] the house to repossession agents [[Franklin Clinton]] and [[Lamar Davis]], both of whom would subsequently become a big part of Michael's life, where they repossess a [[9F]] and a [[Rapid GT]] for their employer [[Simeon Yetarian]]. Michael can later return to the for more therapy sessions with Dr. Friedlander and also returns during the mission [[Reuniting the Family]] with his wife [[Amanda De Santa|Amanda]], daughter [[Tracey De Santa|Tracey]] and son [[Jimmy De Santa|Jimmy]] for a group therapy session. Michael also has the option of visiting Dr. Friedlander at his office [[Random Event - Isiah Friedlander|one final time]] and has the option to kill the doctor.
