[[LEAD]]
'''Grapesed Ave''' is a road in [[Grapeseed]] and the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] in [[Blaine County]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The avenue continues north of [[Catfish View]] and has connections to [[Grapeseed Main St]], [[Seaview Rd]] or [[Union Rd]] as well as two dirt roads leading to the [[O'Neil Farm]]. The avenue runs under the [[Go Loco Railroad]] and the [[Senora Fwy]].
