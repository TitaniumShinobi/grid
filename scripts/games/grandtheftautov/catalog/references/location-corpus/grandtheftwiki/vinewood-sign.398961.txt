The '''Vinewood Sign''' is a landmark in [[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]] and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. It is located in [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]] and is based on the [[:wp:Hollywood Sign|Hollywood Sign]]. In GTA:SA [[Madd Dogg|Madd Dogg's]] [[Madd Dogg's Crib|Crib]] can be found beside this sign. Meanwhile, behind this sign are two [[Weapons in GTA San Andreas|weapons]], namely the [[Cane|cane]] and the [[Golf Club|golf club]].
[[File:Hollywood.jpg|thumb|left|The real Hollywood Sign]]

The GTA V version of the sign is accessible by following an unpaved road. Each letter has a climbable ladder.
