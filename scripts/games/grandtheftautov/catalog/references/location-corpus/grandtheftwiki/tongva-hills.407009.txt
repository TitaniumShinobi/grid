'''Tongva Hills''' is a rural area, primarily consisting of vineyards (including the [[Marlowe Vineyard]]), of [[Los Santos County]], [[San Andreas in GTA V|San Andreas]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. [[Devin Weston]] owns a [[Devin Weston's House|large house]] in Tongva Hills. Tongva Hills is bordered by [[Lago Zancudo]] in the north and [[Banham Canyon]] in the south.
