'''El Quebrados''' is a small town located at the northeast end of [[Tierra Robada]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. El Quebrados means "''The Broken''" in Spanish. It is situated immediately northwest of [[Aldea Malvada]]. It sits on the northern road connecting Tierra Robada to neighboring [[Bone County]] to the east, and is the last stop before continuing across [[Sherman Reservoir]]. Though the town looks depressive, some facilities are still present, including the [[El Quebrados Medical Center]] and [[El Quebrados Police Station]].
