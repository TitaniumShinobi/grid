'''East Beach''' is a district on the north-east coast of the city of [[Los Santos in GTA III Era|Los Santos]], and is controlled by the [[Los Santos Vagos]]. This district is based on the [[:wp:Long Beach|Long Beach]] suburb of Los Angeles. The only known resident of East Beach is [[Colonel Fuhrberger]].
