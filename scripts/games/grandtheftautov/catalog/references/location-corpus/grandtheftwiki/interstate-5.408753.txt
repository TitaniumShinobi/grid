'''[[La Puerta]] Freeway''' ('''Interstate 5''') is a freeway that runs through the southern part of [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. It starts/ends after a T-Intersection off the [[Del Perro Freeway]] and runs through West Los Santos as the [[Miriam Turner Overpass ]] before it ends at the [[Port of Los Santos|Port of LS]] and turns into [[Elysian Fields Freeway|Elysian Fields Fwy]]. 

The La Puerta Freeway is an Interstate highway in the in-game [[Interstate System in GTA V|Interstate system]]. It is one of the three Interstates in GTA V.
