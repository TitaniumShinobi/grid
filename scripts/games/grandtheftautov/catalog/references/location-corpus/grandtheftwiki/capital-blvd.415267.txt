[[LEAD]]
'''Capital Blvd''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which runs from [[Strawberry]] in the west to [[El Burro Heights]] in the east, running through [[Rancho]] and [[La Mesa]]. There are connections to [[Crusade Rd]], [[Davis Ave]], [[El Rancho Blvd]], [[Little Bighorn Ave]], [[Popular St]] and [[Strawberry Ave]], while Capital Blvd goes over the [[Elysian Fields Fwy]] at its eastern most part.
