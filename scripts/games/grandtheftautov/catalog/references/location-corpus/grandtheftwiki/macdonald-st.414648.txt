'''Macdonald St''' is a street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs from [[Strawberry]] in the west to [[Rancho]] in the east, running through [[Davis]]. The street has connections to [[Brouge Ave]], [[Davis Ave]], [[Jamestown St]], [[Little Bighorn Ave]], [[Roy Lowenstein Blvd]] and [[Strawberry Ave]]. The [[Los Santos Vagos]] have control of the eastern side of the street while [[The Families]] control the western most tip.
