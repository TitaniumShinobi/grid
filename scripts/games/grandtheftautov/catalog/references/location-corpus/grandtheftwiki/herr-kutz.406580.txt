[[LEAD]]
'''Herr Kutz''' is a chain of [[:Category:Barbers|barber shops]] with branches on [[Carson Ave]] in [[Davis]] and [[Mirror Park]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] and  on [[Paleto Blvd]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The only employee of all three shops is [[Donna]], who suffers from myopia and frequently injures clients. The player can "stalk" Herr Kutz on the real-world [[Lifeinvader]] [http://www.lifeinvader.com website] and receive a free haircut for [[Franklin Clinton]], [[Michael De Santa]] or [[Trevor Philips]] at Herr Kutz.
