[[LEAD]]
'''Grove St''' is a street in [[Davis]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] with connections to [[Davis Ave]] and [[Brouge Ave]]. It was once [[Grove Street Families]] turf, but by the events of [[Grand Theft Auto V]] the street is now under [[Ballas in GTA V|Ballas]] control with gang members easily visible wearing purple. The [[Los Santos River]] also runs parallel to the street. The street has twenty-two houses of which two are for sale and one for rent.
