[[LEAD]]
'''Alta St''' is a long north-south street in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street begins in [[Downtown Vinewood]] in the north and ends in [[Maze Bank Arena]] in the south also running through [[Hawick]], [[Alta]], [[Vinewood in GTA V|Vinewood]], [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Strawberry]] and [[La Puerta]]. The street, which continues as [[Baytree Canyon Rd]], has connections to [[Adam's Apple Blvd]], [[Clinton Ave]], [[Davis Ave]], [[Hawick Ave]], [[Innocence Blvd]], [[Integrity Way]], [[Occupation Ave]], [[Power St]], [[San Andreas Ave]], [[South Arsenal St]], [[Spanish Ave]], [[Vespucci Blvd]], and [[Vinewood Blvd]].
