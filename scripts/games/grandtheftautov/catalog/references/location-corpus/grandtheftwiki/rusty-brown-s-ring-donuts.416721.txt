[[LEAD]]
'''Rusty Brown's Ring Donuts''' is a donut and coffee shop in [[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]], [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], [[Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars]], and [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The shop is a parody of [[:wp:Dunkin' Donuts|Dunkin' Donuts]], as both companies used a baker as an advertising mascot.

[[SECTION:3:GTA Vice City]]
Rusty Brown's was first mentioned in an advertisement on [[Flash FM]] and [[Fever 105]]. True to [[Rockstar Games|Rockstar's]] humor, people in the ad have very sexually suggestive opinions of their donuts (i.e. ''"I like to lick lovingly around the outside and then thrust my tongue in the middle."; "I just love the batter. All over my face."'') [[Toni]], Flash's DJ, dedicates Wang Chung's "Dance Hall Days" to the shop.

[[SECTION:3:GTA Liberty City Stories]]
Rusty Brown's appears in GTA Liberty City Stories as a diner at [[Callahan Point]], [[Portland]], [[Liberty City in GTA III Era|Liberty City]] (circa 1998) where [[Greasy Joe's|Greasy Joe's Café]] should be in [[Grand Theft Auto III]] (circa 2001). A [[Police Car]] and a [[Mule]] can be found in the parking lot.

It is also likely to be [[Liberty City Bikers]] turf, as referenced in a [[Overdose of Trouble|mission]] where [[Toni Cipriani|Toni]] has to find a cure for the overdosed [[Maria Latore]], who thinks she left the cure, which was called "zap", near there. They find no zap there, and they also find the enraged biker gang, because Toni and Maria were behind [[Biker Heat|the killing]] of their leader, [[Cedric Fotheringay|Cedric "Wayne" Fotheringay]]. The bikers then give chase to the pair.
