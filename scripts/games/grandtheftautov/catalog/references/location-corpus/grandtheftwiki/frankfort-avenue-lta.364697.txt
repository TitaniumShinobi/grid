[[LEAD]]
'''Frankfort Avenue''' is an underground subway station in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]], in the [[Subway in GTA IV|Liberty City Subway]] system, serving the [[Algonquin Inner Line|W]] trains only. The express N and Q trains bypass this station at all times except late nights, when the N serves this station.

The station is not to be confused with [[Frankfort High LTA|Frankfort High station]] and [[Frankfort Low LTA|Frankfort Low station]], which are both located further north along [[Frankfort Avenue]].
