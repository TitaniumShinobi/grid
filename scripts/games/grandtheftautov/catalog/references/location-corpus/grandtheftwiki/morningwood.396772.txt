'''Morningwood''' is a wealthy neighbourhood in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. Morningwood is described as being one of the cleanest and safest neighbourhoods in the city. The neighbourhood has a lot of high-end businesses many of which go out of business in six months being unable to pay the rent.
