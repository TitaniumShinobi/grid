'''Invention Ct''' is a residential southwest to northeast street in [[Vespucci Canals]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The street runs parallel to [[Imagination Ct]] and has connections to [[Bay City Ave]] and [[Prosperity St]]. The majority of the street is treated as a pavement in the games as seen by the reactions of pedestrians while driving on the street.
