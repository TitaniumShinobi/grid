[[LEAD]]
'''Mom's Tacos''' is a [[:Category:Restaurants|fast food restaurant]] chain store in [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]] appearing in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The chain has restaurants at the corner of [[Carson Ave]] and [[Jamestown St]], on the corner of [[Davis Ave]] and [[Innocence Blvd]], and a third on [[El Rancho Blvd]]. The company's phone number is 310-555-1035.
