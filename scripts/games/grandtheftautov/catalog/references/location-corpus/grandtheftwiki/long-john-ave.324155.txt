[[LEAD]]
'''Long John Avenue''' is a street located in [[Alderney]] in [[Grand Theft Auto IV]]. The street is used in one mission, [[Flatline]], for [[Jimmy Pegorino]]. A [[Cavalcade]] can be found in a driveway of a home located across the street from the [[Westdyke Memorial Hospital]], which can be stolen for [[Stevie|Stevie's]] [[Stevie's Car Thefts|Car Thefts]].
