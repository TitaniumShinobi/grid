[[LEAD]]
'''Calton Heights''' is a district of [[San Fierro]], [[San Andreas in GTA III Era|San Andreas]]. It is situated toward the north end of the city, with a portion extending all the way to [[San Fierro Bay]]. To the west are [[Juniper Hollow]] and [[Paradiso]]; to the east are [[Financial]] and [[Downtown San Fierro|Downtown]]; and to the south is [[Chinatown, San Fierro|Chinatown]].
