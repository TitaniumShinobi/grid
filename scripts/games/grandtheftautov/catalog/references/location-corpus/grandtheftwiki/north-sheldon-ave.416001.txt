[[LEAD]]
'''North Sheldon Ave''' is a residential road in northern [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]], which appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]] and [[Grand Theft Auto Online]]. The road is almost entirely in [[Vinewood Hills]] with only the southern most tip of the road being located in [[Rockford Hills]]. There are connections to [[Ace Jones Dr]], [[Cockingend Dr]], two to [[Hangman Ave]], [[Hillcrest Ave]], [[Hillcrest Ridge Access Rd]], [[Milton Rd]], [[Normandy Dr]], [[Picture Perfect Drive]] and [[South Mo Milton Dr]].
