[[LEAD]]
'''Hawaiian Snow''' is a clothing store in [[Star Junction]], [[Liberty City in GTA IV Era|Liberty City]] and the corner of [[Hawick Ave]] and [[Power St]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. It and sells casual wear. The term "''hawaiian snow''" is slang for marijuana seeds. The brand is possibly a parody of [[wp:Volcom|Volcom]].
