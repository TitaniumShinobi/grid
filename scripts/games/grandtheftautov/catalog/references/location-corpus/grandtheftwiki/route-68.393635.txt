'''Route 68''' is an east-west rural [[Numbered Highways in San Andreas|Numbered Highway]] in the state of [[San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] and appears in [[Grand Theft Auto V]]. The highway passes through rural desert areas of the state and the small town of [[Harmony]] in [[Blaine County]]. There are stops along the highway such as a [[24/7]] and a [[Checkout!]].
