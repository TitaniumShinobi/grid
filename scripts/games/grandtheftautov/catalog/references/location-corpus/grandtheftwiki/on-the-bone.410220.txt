[[LEAD]]
'''On the Bone''' is a food and meat market, butchers and delicatessen located on [[Forum Dr]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos in GTA V|Los Santos]]. The business is open seven days a week and also sells some non-food items such as batteries and film. There are three newspaper dispensers located outside the business: two for the [[Daily Rag]] and one for [[Las Mentiras]] (or ''The Lies''). The company has two phone numbers ''1-898-THEBONE'' and ''562-555-3747'', which will always be busy. A [[Declasse]] [[Tornado]] can sometimes be found parked next to the store while [[prostitutes]] also loiter around the building during the night hours.
