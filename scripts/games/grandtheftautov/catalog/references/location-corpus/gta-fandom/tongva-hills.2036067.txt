'''Tongva Hills''' is an undeveloped part of the [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|State of San Andreas]] located in [[Los Santos County]] in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is bordered by [[Lago Zancudo]] to the north and [[Banham Canyon]] to the south.
