[[LEAD]]
'''East Vinewood''' is a suburb located in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. The neighbourhood contains the sub-district of [[Mirror Park]], the park of the same name and the [[Vinewood Racetrack]]. It is bordered by [[La Mesa]] and [[Murrieta Heights]] to the south, the [[Tataviam Mountains]] to the east, [[Vinewood Hills]] to the north and [[Downtown Vinewood]], [[Alta]] and [[Hawick]] to the west.
