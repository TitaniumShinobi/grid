[[LEAD]]
The '''Republic of Ecuador''' (Spanish: ''República del Ecuador''), simply known as '''Ecuador''', is a country in northwestern South America, bordered by [[Colombia]] to the north, [[Peru]] to the east and south, and the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the west. Ecuador also includes the Galápagos Islands in the Pacific, about 1,000 kilometers (621&nbsp;mi) west of the mainland.
