[[LEAD]]
'''DM Pets''' is a former exotic pet specialist located next to Vinewood Records on [[Eclipse Boulevard]] in the [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] neighborhood of [[West Vinewood]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is located a couple of blocks away from the nightclub [[Tequi-la-la]], and is going out of business, according to signs. Its telephone number is shown to be 1-999-DMXPETS.

After [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino Heist|The Diamond Casino Heist]] Update in [[2019]], it was replaced by the [[Arcades|8-Bit Arcade]] in the [[Grand Theft Auto V: Enhanced Edition|Enhanced Version]] of ''GTA Online''.
