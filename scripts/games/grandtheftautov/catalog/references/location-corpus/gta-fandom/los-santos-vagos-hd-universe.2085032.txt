[[LEAD]]
The '''Los Santos Vagos''', also known as '''LSV''' or the '''Vagos''', are a large [[Mexico|Mexican]] street gang appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}. They are said to be the largest Hispanic street gang in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] and are rumored to have connections with some major Mafia groups, according to [[The Underbelly Of Paradise]] [[Television in GTA V|TV]] program. Vagos are supporters of [[LS Pounders]] football team, as they wear different clothes with their logos and a yellow coloured T-shirt to match.

[[SECTION:2:Territory]]
The Vagos control almost the entire neighborhood of Rancho. However, the [[Varrios Los Aztecas (HD Universe)|Varrios Los Aztecas]] are sometimes seen in northern Rancho. Jamestown Street and the [[Rancho Projects]] in the southern part of Rancho are particular hotspots for Vagos activity, as well as various backalleys like Cypress Flats. In the storyline mission [[Repossession]], Vagos are found in [[Vespucci Beach]]. However, after the mission is completed, Vagos will be replaced by [[Marabunta Grande]]. In a phone call between a female member and her brother, it's revealed that LSV has also started expanding its influence into [[Las Venturas (HD Universe)|Las Venturas]].{{Ref|name=ped|type=audiodata|ref=''g_f_y_vagos_01_latino_mini_01.awc<br>PHONE_CONV5_CHAT2_01''}}

Los Santos Vagos members can be seen drinking, driving cars, hanging out in groups of two-five on the sidewalk, smoking cigarettes and [[Weed|marijuana]] and drinking [[Pißwasser]]. They are easily identified by their modern urban attire. Although LSV is characterized as a largely Mexican American street gang, they also include members of [[Guatemala]]n origin.
