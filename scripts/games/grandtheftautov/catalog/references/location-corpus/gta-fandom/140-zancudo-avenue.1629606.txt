'''140 Zancudo Avenue''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[Zancudo Avenue]] in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]], featured in the [[Independence Day Special]] for ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]121,000 and includes a 2-car garage.
