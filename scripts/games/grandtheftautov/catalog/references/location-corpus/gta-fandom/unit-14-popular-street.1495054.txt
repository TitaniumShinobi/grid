[[LEAD]]
'''Unit 14 Popular Street''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in a small warehouse complex accessed off of [[Popular Street]] in [[La Mesa]], along with [[Unit 2 Popular Street]]. It sits adjacent to the [[Maibatsu Factory]], and an alley between the two buildings, accessed off [[Supply Street]], may also be used to enter the complex.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]77,500 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
