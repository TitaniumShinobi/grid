'''197 Route 68''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located to the right of a derelict [[Globe Oil]] gas station and general store, accessed off of [[Route 68]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]29,000 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
