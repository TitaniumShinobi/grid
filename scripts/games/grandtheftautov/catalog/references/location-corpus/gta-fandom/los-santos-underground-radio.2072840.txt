[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos Underground Radio''' (LS-UR) is a [[Radio Stations in GTA Online|radio station]] in {{O}} added in the [[GTA Online: After Hours|After Hours]] update on 31 July 2018, and later anachronistically [[Radio Stations in GTA V|introduced]] to {{V}} as of the [[GTA Online: Arena War|Arena War]] update. It plays {{WP|House music|house}} and {{WP|techno}} music.
