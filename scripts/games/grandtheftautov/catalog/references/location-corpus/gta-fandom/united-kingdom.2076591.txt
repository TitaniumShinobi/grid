The '''{{WP|United Kingdom}}''' (officially named the '''United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland''', commonly known as the '''UK''', and erroneously known as '''{{WP|Great Britain|Britain}}'''), is a country located off the north-western coast of mainland Europe. The UK consists of four constituent nations: {{WP|England}}, [[Scotland]], [[Wales]], and {{WP|Northern Ireland}}. With the exception of Northern Ireland's land border with the [[Ireland|Republic of Ireland]], the UK is surrounded by the {{WP|Atlantic Ocean}}, the {{WP|North Sea}}, the {{WP|English Channel}} and the {{WP|Irish Sea}}.

The UK is the setting for {{L69}} and {{L61}}. The capital city, [[London]], is the centerpiece of those two video games. [[Manchester]] is available as a multiplayer map.

The ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' games are developed in the United Kingdom, more specifically in Scotland, where [[Rockstar North]] is based. The games are first shipped to the [[United States of America]] and [[Canada]], before they are made available in the UK and the remainder of continental [[Europe]], [[Australia]], [[New Zealand]], and finally the rest of the world.

The UK is currently home to five Rockstar studios:
*[[Rockstar North]]
*[[Rockstar Leeds]]
*[[Rockstar Lincoln]]
*[[Rockstar London]]
*[[Rockstar Dundee]]

The United Kingdom is also home to the ''[[BBFC]]'' rating system, which has given the most scrutiny towards the {{S}} without the need for any of the games to be censored or re-rated. [[Dan Houser]], [[Sam Houser]], [[Terry Donovan]] and [[Gary Dale]] are all British.

Owing to the series' British (especially Scottish) origins, many references are made to the country through the games.
