[[LEAD]]
The [[Maibatsu Corporation (HD Universe)|Maibatsu]] '''MonstroCiti''' is a two-door off-road competition car featured in {{O}} as part of the [[GTA Online: San Andreas Mercenaries|San Andreas Mercenaries]] update.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
*Available for purchase from [[Southern San Andreas Super Autos]] for [[Money|$]]1,485,000.
**Conversion at [[Hao's Special Works]] available for [[Money|$]]1,109,000.

[[SECTION:3:The Chop Shop]]
*A white-and-black MonstroCiti is featured as an optional vehicle in the [[Task: Getaway Vehicle|Getaway Vehicle]] task, as part of the [[Salvage Yard Robberies|Salvage Yard]]'s [[The Gangbanger Robbery|Gangbanger Robbery]].
