[[LEAD]]
The '''Vinewood Walk of Fame''' is an iconic [[Los Santos]] landmark featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas]]'', ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'', and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Location]]


[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas'']]
It is located in [[Vinewood (3D Universe)|Vinewood]] on [[Vinewood Boulevard (3D Universe)|Vinewood Boulevard]] in [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]], starting at the corner of [[Market Boulevard]] in the east and extending 3 city blocks to the [[Vinewood Cemetery]].

}}

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Grand Theft Auto Online'']]
The Walk of Fame is located on [[Vinewood Boulevard (HD Universe)|Vinewood Boulevard]], [[Downtown Vinewood]], extending over three city blocks from [[Meteor Street]] in the east to [[Las Lagunas Boulevard]] in the west, crossing over [[Power Street]] and [[Alta Street]]. A small section of stars also goes up the west side of Power Street, north of Vinewood Boulevard.
