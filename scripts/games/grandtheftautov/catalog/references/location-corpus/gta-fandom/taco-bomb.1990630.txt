[[LEAD]]
'''Taco Bomb''' is a fast food chain that appears in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. The player can buy stocks of the company in the [[Lcn-exchange.com]] website in ''Grand Theft Auto V''.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]
*The [[Prosperity Street Promenade]] location is based on the now-closed Gaucho Grill at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/vX79o5jXoTjHi85M6|1251 Third Street Promenade}} in Santa Monica, California{{Ref|name = gaucho-grill-closed|type = website|date = 11/20/2024|url = https://la.eater.com/2010/12/16/6705757/loteria-grill-coming-to-3rd-street-promenade|ref = ''Loteria Grill Coming to 3rd Street Promenade'' - Eater Los Angeles, December 16, 2010}}.
*The [[Little Seoul]] location is based on the {{WP|Denny's}} location at {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/FLxZ48wAJ76MGRPE7|3741 Wilshire Blvd}} in Los Angeles, California.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
In ''GTA V'' and ''Online'', there are three locations that can be seen around [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], though none are accessible by the player. There is also an unseen location inside Terminal one at [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]].
