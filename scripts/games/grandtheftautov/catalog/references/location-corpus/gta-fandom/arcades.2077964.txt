Next to the bar, there is the manager's office; this is where the owner manages the business. A [[Pixel Emporium]] laptop is provided to purchase arcade machines. Trophies for Arcade Games released after The Diamond Casino Heist update are placed on the desk along with the plushies obtained from [[Shiny Wasabi Kitty Claw]]. There is also a safe in the office that stores the Arcade's daily income, the amount of which varies according to the number of arcade games installed in the dedicated floor slots of the arcade. The maximum attainable income is $5,000 per in-game day. All floor slots must be equipped with a game to reach this amount, and the maximum capacity of the safe is $100,000 ($50,000 prior to [[GTA Online: The Contract|The Contract]] update). It must then be emptied for it to accumulate money again. Note that it is not necessary to acquire the most expansive arcade machines to attain the maximum passive daily income of $5,000.

Once an arcade machine is owned, the player can use Arcade Management in the Interaction Menu to place up to four copies of the machine around the main floor in any slot that is large enough to accommodate the machine or group of machines.
