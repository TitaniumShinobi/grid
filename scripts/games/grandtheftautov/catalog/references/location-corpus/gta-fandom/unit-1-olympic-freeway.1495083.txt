[[LEAD]]
'''Unit 1 Olympic Freeway''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located under the [[Interstate 4|Olympic Freeway]] overpass in [[La Mesa]], sitting at a lower elevation on the corner of [[Popular Street]] and the Olympic Freeway off-ramp. It can be accessed via an alleyway connected to Popular St, to the left of [[The Secure Unit]] building.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]70,000 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
