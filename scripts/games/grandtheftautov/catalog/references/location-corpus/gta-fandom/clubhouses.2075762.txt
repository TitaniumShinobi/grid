[[SECTION:3:Bar]]
The bar is staffed by the Clubhouse bartender [[Sindy]], whom the player can interact with to order free shots of [[The Mount Distilling Co.|The Mount]] whiskey and, post-[[GTA Online: The Criminal Enterprises|The Criminal Enterprises]] update, start a [[Bar Resupply]] mission. Tied to this mission is a cash bag located behind the office desk, where the player can collect their accumulated Bar Earnings, up to a maximum of $100,000.
