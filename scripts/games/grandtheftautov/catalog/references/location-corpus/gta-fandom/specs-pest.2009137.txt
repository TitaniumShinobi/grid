[[LEAD]]
'''Specs Pest''' is an optician in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located next to the [[Hardcore Comic Store]] on [[Eclipse Boulevard]], [[West Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]].

The store is not enterable and plays no role in the storyline. The name is a play on the phrase 'sex pest'.
