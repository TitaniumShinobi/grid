[[LEAD]]
The '''Gun Van''' is a mobile business in {{O}}, added as part of the continuation of the [[GTA Online: Los Santos Drug Wars|Los Santos Drug Wars]] update released during [[Gun Van Week]] on January 12, 2023.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
{{Quote|Just so you know, I don't like staying in one spot for too long.|Seller.}}
There are 30 possible spawn locations for the van. 

The van will change location every day around 06:00 UTC starting from January 12th, 2023, and will be at the new location on any new session started after its movement time. Players joining sessions that started prior to the move time will see it revert back to the previous location even if they have been in a newer session with the new location.
