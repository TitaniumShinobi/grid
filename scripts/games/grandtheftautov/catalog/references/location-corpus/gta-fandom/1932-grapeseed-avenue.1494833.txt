[[LEAD]]
'''1932 Grapeseed Avenue''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located on [[Grapeseed Avenue]] in [[Grapeseed]], being the smaller of the two structures on the property of [[Shady Tree Farm]]. The entrance to the [[O'Neil Ranch]] can be found across the street.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]27,500 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
