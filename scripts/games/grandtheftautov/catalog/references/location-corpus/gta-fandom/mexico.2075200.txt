The '''United Mexican States''', (Spanish: ''Estados Unidos Mexicanos'') simply known as '''Mexico''', is a country in North America, south of the [[United States of America|United States]], north of [[Guatemala]] and Belize and to the west of the island nation of [[Cuba]].

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*[[Mexican Arms Dealers]] {{nd}} A minor group who only make one appearance in the game.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*[[Cholos]] {{nd}} A Mexican street gang who rivaled the [[Cubans|Cabrones]]. Their name is derived from "cholo", a term for Mexican gang members.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*[[Pedro Garcia]] {{nd}} The Mexican Arms Dealers boss, is Mexican.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*[[Jerry Martinez]] {{nd}} The main antagonist of the game is Mexican.
