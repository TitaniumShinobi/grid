[[LEAD]]
'''Lesbos Lane''' is a two-way street in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]], in {{V}} and {{O}}. The street begins at [[Marina Drive]] and ends at [[Algonquin Boulevard]], running parallel to [[Meringue Lane]]. There is nothing of general interest on this street except from an occasional trailer.
