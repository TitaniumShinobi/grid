[[LEAD]]
'''Blaine County Depot''' is a [[Post OP]] location operating in [[Paleto Bay]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. Their phone number is 425-555-0170, but when called, there is no response. Blaine County Depot cannot be accessed by the player and plays no role in the storyline. 

Despite being a Post OP depot, a [[GoPostal]] [[Boxville (HD Universe)|Boxville]] often spawns at the location.
