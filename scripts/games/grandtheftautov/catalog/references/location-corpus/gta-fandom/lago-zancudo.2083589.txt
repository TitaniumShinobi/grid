'''Lago Zancudo''', which translates as '''Mosquito Lake''', is an undeveloped area located in [[Blaine County]] in the state of [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] within {{V}} and {{O}}. Its slogan is "Bringing a Taste of [[Mexico]] to the [[United States of America|U.S.A]]."
