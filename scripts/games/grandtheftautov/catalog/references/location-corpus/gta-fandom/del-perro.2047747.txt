[[LEAD]]
'''Del Perro''' is an incorporated affluent city featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located in [[Los Santos County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. It is bordered by [[Pacific Bluffs]] and [[Morningwood]] to the north, [[Rockford Hills]] to the east and [[Vespucci]] to the south.
