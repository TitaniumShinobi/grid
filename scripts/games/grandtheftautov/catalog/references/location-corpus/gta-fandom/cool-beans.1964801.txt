'''Cool Beans''' is a coffeehouse chain in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
*The [[Mirror Park]] location is based on the Intelligentsia Coffee location at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/QQHeJrPDR5PeUhCM9|3922 Sunset Boulevard}} in Los Angeles, California.
*The [[Vespucci Canals]] location is based on the former {{WP|The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf|Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf}} (now {{WP|Starbucks}}) location at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/8aUeQEcQD7B4BpgQ7|2901 Main Street}} in Santa Monica, California.
