'''Buen Vino Road''' is a [[List of Street Names|road]] in [[Banham Canyon]] and [[Tongva Hills]], [[Los Santos County]] that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. It runs from [[Banham Canyon Drive]] in the southwest to [[Route 68]] in the northeast.
