[[LEAD]]
'''Pillbox North Station''' is an underground [[Los Santos Transit]] station in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located in [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]]. The station is under construction during the events of the game and as such remains closed.
