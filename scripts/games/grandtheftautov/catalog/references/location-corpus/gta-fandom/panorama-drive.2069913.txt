[[LEAD]]
'''Panorama Drive''' is a [[List of Street Names|street]] in {{V}} and {{O}}, located in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]]. The street begins at [[Marina Drive]] and crosses [[Algonquin Boulevard]], passing through [[East Joshua Road]] and [[Joshua Road]] in Sandy Shores, [[Smoke Tree Road]] in [[Grand Senora Desert]] and ends at [[Route 68]]. The entrance to the [[Sandy Shores Airfield]] is located on this street.
