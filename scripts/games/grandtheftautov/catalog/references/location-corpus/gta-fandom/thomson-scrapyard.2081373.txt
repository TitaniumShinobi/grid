'''Thomson Scrapyard''' is a [[Scrap Yards|scrap yard]] in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is found south of the [[Senora National Park]] on [[Cat-Claw Avenue]] by the junction of the [[Senora Freeway]] and [[Route 68]] in the [[Grand Senora Desert]].
