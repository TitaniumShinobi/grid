'''12 Sustancia Road''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[Sustancia Road]] in [[El Burro Heights]], [[Los Santos County]] featured in the [[Independence Day Special]] for ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]143,000 and includes a 6-car garage.
