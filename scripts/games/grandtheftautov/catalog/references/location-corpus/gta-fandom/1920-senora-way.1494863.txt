[[LEAD]]
'''1920 Senora Way''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in a small electrical substation on the [[RON Alternates Wind Farm]] site off of [[Senora Way]]. The entrance to [[Palmer-Taylor Power Station]] can be found across the street. Signage indicates the substation site is protected by [[Merryweather Security]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]32,000 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
