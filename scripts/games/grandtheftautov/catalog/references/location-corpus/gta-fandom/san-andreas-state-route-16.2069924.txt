[[LEAD]]
'''San Andreas State Route 16''' is a north-south urban route on the [[Numbered Highways in GTA V]]. It is the shortest route in the system. The route was revealed in the GTA V map insert with its marker placed on [[Mad Wayne Thunder Drive]]. However, the map does not show the path of the route. One possibility is that it connects [[Richman (HD Universe)|Richman]] and [[Rockford Hills]] with the freeways of [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]], via [[Palomino Avenue]]. Its northern end is at [[San Andreas State Route 18]] and its southern end is at the intersection of [[Interstate 2|Interstates 2]] and [[Interstate 5|5]].
