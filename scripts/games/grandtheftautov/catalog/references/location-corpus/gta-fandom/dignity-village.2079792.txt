[[LEAD]]
'''Dignity Village''' is an unincorporated tent city established by [[Tramps|homeless people]] near [[Procopio Beach]], located in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. Dignity Village is bordered by the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the north, [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]] to the southwest, [[Mount Gordo]] to the east, and [[Paleto Bay]] to the west. It is featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', but plays no role in the storyline, except for a [[Gang Attacks|Gang Attack]] and having a [[Letter Scraps|letter scrap]]. It is accessible south of [[Route 1]], via an underpass. Dignity Village is the second-smallest settlement in ''Grand Theft Auto V ''and ''Grand Theft Auto Online'', while [[Cape Catfish]] is the smallest.

[[Curtis Weaver]], the final [[Bail Bond]] target, is located here.
