[[LEAD]]
'''Rimmers''' is a clothes and sunglasses manufacturer in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]
*The [[Suffolk]] location is based on the {{WP|Sunglass Hut}} branch at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/w8rUJ1RcD27Wp5Bf9|157 Spring Street}} in {{WP|SoHo, Manhattan}}. This is also the building that inspired the [[Yogarishima]] branch nearby and the [[Back Passage-Garnet Street Clothing Store]].
