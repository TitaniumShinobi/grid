[[LEAD]]
The '''Los Santos Department of Wind Power''' is the company in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' that makes and maintains the various wind turbines seen in places such as the [[RON Alternates Wind Farm]]. It is a subsidiary of the [[Los Santos Department of Water & Power]].
