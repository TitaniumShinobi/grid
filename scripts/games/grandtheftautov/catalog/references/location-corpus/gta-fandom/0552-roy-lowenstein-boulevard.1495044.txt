[[LEAD]]
'''0552 Roy Lowenstein Boulevard''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located behind the [[Panache Laundering]] laundromat on [[Capital Boulevard]]. It can be accessed either through the laundromat entrance on Capital Blvd or through the alleyway on the corner of [[Little Bighorn Avenue]] and [[Roy Lowenstein Boulevard]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]80,000 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
