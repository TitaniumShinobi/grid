'''12 Little Bighorn Avenue''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located next to a [[Los Santos Department of Water & Power]] substation overlooking the [[Los Santos River (HD Universe)|LS River]]. It is on the corner of [[Little Bighorn Avenue]] and [[Roy Lowenstein Boulevard]] in [[Rancho]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. The Roy Lowenstein Boulevard side of the building is occupied by stores including [[East Los Santos Appliances]], [[Trinculo Clothing]] and [[Bean's Market]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]32,000 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
