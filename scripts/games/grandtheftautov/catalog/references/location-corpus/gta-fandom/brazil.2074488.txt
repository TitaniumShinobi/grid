The '''Federative Republic of Brazil''', simply known as '''Brazil''', is the largest country in both {{WP|South America}} and the {{WP|Latin America|Latin American}} region. It is bordered by {{WP|Venezuela}}, {{WP|Guyana}}, {{WP|Suriname}} and the French overseas region of {{WP|French Guiana}} to the north, [[Colombia]] to the north-west, [[Bolivia]] and [[Peru]] to the west, [[Argentina]] and [[Paraguay]] to the south-west and [[Uruguay]] to the south.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*A Brazilian flag can be seen at a store near [[The Greasy Chopper]].
*Brazil is mentioned in the song ''Breaks'' by Kurtis Blow in the [[Wildstyle]] radio station.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*The [[Carioca Latin Dance]] is a Brazilian dancing school.
*There's a store named [[Modinha Music]] in [[Little Havana]]. "Modinha" is a term in Portuguese, used specially in Brazil, for a fading trend or a specific music genre named "Modinha", popular in Brazil.

**He is also the co-founder and was the lead singer and rhythm guitarist of {{WP|Sepultura}}, a Brazilian rock band featured on this station.
