'''Beachwalk Bistro''' is a small restaurant in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]''.

It is located next to [[Canal Treats]] and [[Papa Surf]] on the [[Vespucci Beach Sidewalk|walkway]] near [[Vespucci Beach]] in [[Vespucci]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. It accepts [[Fleeca]], [[Shark (company)|Shark]] and [[Limit]] credit cards and [[Money|cash]] as payment options. According to signs, the business prohibits the allowance of pets. It is inaccessible to the player and plays no role in the storyline.
