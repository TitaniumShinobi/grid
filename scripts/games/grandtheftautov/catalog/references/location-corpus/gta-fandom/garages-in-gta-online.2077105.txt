[[LEAD]]
'''Garages''' are purchasable [[Safehouses]] in {{O}}.

[[SECTION:2:Garage Tiers]]
The [[Dynasty 8/Dynasty 8 Listings|Dynasty 8 listings]] are divided into three tiers of purchasable properties; low-end, medium and high-end.

[[SECTION:3:Low-end]]
Low-end garages have a basic, dingy interior with poor lighting and various tools and machine parts scattered around. A laptop and radio are located on the rear workbench for the player to interact with. These garages can store a maximum of two [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].

[[SECTION:3:High-end]]
High-end garages feature premium quality lighting and an all-white interior, with a more pristine work area. Most of the [[Garage Mechanic]]'s vehicle maintenance and mechanical equipment are inaccessible and separated behind a glass wall at the rear of the garage. Like the other tier garages, the rear workbench features a laptop and radio, but a wall-mounted TV has also been added to the left of the workbench, allowing the player to watch TV and CCTV, and spectate other players. These garages can store a maximum of 10 Personal Vehicles and three Bicycles, which are stored on dedicated wall racks. Note that though there are six total bike racks, only three may be stored.

[[SECTION:4:Eclipse Boulevard Garage]]
{{Main|Eclipse Boulevard Garage}}
Added with the [[GTA Online: Los Santos Drug Wars|Los Santos Drug Wars]] update, the Eclipse Boulevard Garage is listed as a high-end garage, but features significant differences to other high-end garages. The property can hold up to a total of 50 Personal Vehicles, features customizable interiors similar to [[Office Garages]], access to a roof patio, and a kitchenette area on each floor that offer [[Snacks]] and other amenities.
