'''Davis Quartz''' is a terraced mining site in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is located on [[Senora Way]], to the east of [[Sandy Shores]] and southeast of the [[Grand Senora Desert]].
