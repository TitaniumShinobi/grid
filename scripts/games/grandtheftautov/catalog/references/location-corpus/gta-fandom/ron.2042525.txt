[[LEAD]]
'''RON OIL''' ([[BAWSAQ]]: RON), commonly shortened to '''RON''', is a multinational petroleum company featured in the {{HD}} of {{S}}. The player can buy stocks of the company on the [[Bawsaq.com]] website in {{V}}.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
