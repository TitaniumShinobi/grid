[[LEAD]]
'''Joshua Road''' is a two-way road in {{V}} and {{O}}. The street starts at intersection of [[Panorama Drive]] and [[East Joshua Road]] in [[Sandy Shores]] then travels westward throughout the [[Grand Senora Desert]] then ends at [[Route 68]] in [[Harmony]], where it continues as [[Senora Road]].
