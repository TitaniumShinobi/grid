[[LEAD]]
'''Suburban''', formerly known as '''Sub Urban''', ([[BAWSAQ]]: '''SUB''') is a clothing store chain and fashion brand mentioned in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{CW}}, and appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
Suburban is first mentioned on the [[Designerslave.com]] [[Websites in GTA IV|website]] as "Sub Urban", the owner of the [[White Widow]] fashion brand. It is sold in the [[Go Out]] store on [[East Island City]], retaining the logo of its previous [[Sub Urban (3D Universe)|3D Universe counterpart]].

An alternate logo also exists in ''Grand Theft Auto IV'', which features the name in block letters, although "Sub" and "Urban" remained separate. This logo would serve as a prototype for the logo that would debut in ''Grand Theft Auto V''.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
{{H4|game=VO}}
*The buildings housing the [[Alta]] and [[Chumash]] branches are modeled after the former Melrose Spa, an infamous gay bathhouse that operated at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/JMNVv6A3wniF3EwB6|7269 Melrose Avenue}} in the {{WP|Melrose Avenue#Melrose District|Melrose District}} of {{WP|Los Angeles}}, {{WP|California}}, until its closure in 2017; it has since been completely replaced by a differently designed structure.
