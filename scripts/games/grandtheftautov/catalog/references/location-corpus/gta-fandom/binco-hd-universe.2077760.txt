[[LEAD]]
'''Binco''' ([[BAWSAQ]]: '''BIN''') is a low-end clothing store mentioned in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}} and appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
Binco is first mentioned in billboards across [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]], depicting the facade of a Binco store with an address in [[Hove Beach]]. The screenshot used in the billboard is a leftover from the early stages of development, before the store was redesigned into the [[Russian Shop]]. Most files related to the Russian Shop, including the GXT entry for its map blip, still refers to the store as Binco except for the display name.{{Ref|name = gtaiv-americangxt-binco|ref = |type = textdata|date = 04/02/2025|filepath   = \GTAIV\common\text\american.gxt|filedata   = [BINCO] Russian Shop}} Despite this, the Binco logo can still be seen around the city, such as on vehicle liveries.
