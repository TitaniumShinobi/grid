[[LEAD]]
'''Bait Street'''  is a road in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in [[La Puerta]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] that begins at [[Autopia Parkway]] and ends at [[The Secure Unit]] storage facility. It serves as a security entrance for the facility.
