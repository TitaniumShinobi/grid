[[LEAD]]
The '''Grapeseed Train Depot''' is a railroad depot that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. The depot is used to store railroad cars and locomotives in [[Grapeseed]], [[Blaine County]], north of the [[HJ Silos Galileo Park]].
