[[LEAD]]
The [[Maibatsu Corporation (HD Universe)|Maibatsu]] '''Manchez Scout C''' is an off-road motorcycle featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of the [[GTA Online: Los Santos Drug Wars|Los Santos Drug Wars]] update.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
*Available at [[The Freakshop]] upon acquisition of the [[Brickade 6x6]], after completing all six [[First Dose]] missions.
**Can be requested from the [[Interaction Menu]], under the "Acid Lab" services. No cooldown is applied to the vehicle, meaning it can be requested right after being sent to storage or destroyed.
