[[LEAD]]
'''Paleto Boulevard''' is a four lane throughfare in {{V}} and {{O}}, that passes through the town of [[Paleto Bay]]. The street starts at  [[Great Ocean Highway]]. The street spans across Paleto Bay and ends at [[Procopio Drive]].
