[[LEAD]]
'''Xero Gas''' is a petroleum company in the {{S}}, featured in the {{HD}} titles.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto VI'']]
*The [[Ambrosia]] location shown in official screenshots fort {{VI}} is based on a former Chevron filling station at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/LsGuadCrhyP25LdE6|517 E Sugarland Hwy}} in {{WP|Clewiston, Florida}}, now occupied by Gator Tires Center.
*The [[Rockridge]] location seen in the [[Grand Theft Auto VI/Leaks#September_2022|September 2022 leaks]] appears to be based on the Valero gas station located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/NATzcjYbjurMeacR8|1705 NW 7th Street}} in {{WP|Miami}}, Florida.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]


[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
Xero Gas do not have any filling stations in {{IV}}; however, there is a Xero Gas refinery within the [[Acter Industrial Park]] in [[State of Alderney|Alderney State]], and an abandoned Xero Gas warehouse turned gang hideout in [[Bohan]], [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]].
