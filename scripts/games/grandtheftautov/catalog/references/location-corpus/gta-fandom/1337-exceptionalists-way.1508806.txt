'''1337 Exceptionalists Way''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located on a parallel side street on [[Exceptionalists Way]], facing the [[Opium Nights]] Hotel. It can be accessed via Exceptionalists Way by entering near the [[Alpha Mail]] warehouse. 

The address number is a reference to {{WP|Leet|Leetspeak}}.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]112,500 and can store 10 [[Personal Vehicles]] and three [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].

If the player has purchased the [[Criminal Enterprise Starter Pack]], this garage will be available for FREE.
