[[LEAD]]
'''Pill-Pharm''' is a pharmacy chain appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
<div class="mw-collapsible-content">
*The [[Koresh Square]] branch in [[Alderney City]] is based on the now-closed Valu-Plus store formerly located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/G6FfCeX4oiZRN4VdA|697 Broad Street}} in {{WP|Newark, New Jersey}}.
*The [[Frankfort Avenue]] and [[Jade Street]] branch in [[Star Junction]] is based on the now-closed {{WP|Duane Reade}} pharmacy formerly located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/5EMwsS7KNeCRmimA7|661 8th Avenue}} in {{WP|Hell's Kitchen, Manhattan}}.
*The [[Luddite Row]] branch in [[Lower Easton]] is based on the now-closed Fancy Pharmacy formerly located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/1EdZGMS6BZNCKtrWA|131 Essex Street}} in the {{WP|Lower East Side}}.
*The [[Deadwood Street]] branch in [[South Slopes]] is modeled after the now-closed {{WP|Rite Aid}} pharmacy formerly located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/2F6NsYgew47KM2kR6|725 Manhattan Avenue}} in {{WP|Greenpoint, Brooklyn}}.
*The [[Dukes Boulevard]] branch in [[East Island City]] is modeled after the Astoria Plaza located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/PR8kmo4ACB962Vpm9|28-56 Steinway Street}} in {{WP|Astoria, Queens}}.
</div>
