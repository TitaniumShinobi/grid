[[LEAD]]
'''1200 Route 68''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located behind the [[Sub Urban (HD Universe)|Suburban]] store in [[Harmony]], accessed off of [[Route 68]]. A [[The Open Road#Businesses|Counterfeit Cash Factory]] can be found within the same structure. The [[Dynasty 8]] photo preview shows the incorrect side of the building.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]28,000 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
