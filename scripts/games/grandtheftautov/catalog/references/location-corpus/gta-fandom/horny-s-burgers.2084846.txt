[[LEAD]]
'''Horny's Burgers''' is a fast food burger restaurant chain that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}, and is due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
{{H4|''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online''}}
*The [[Mirror Park]] branch is a modified rendition of the former Tom's Burgers restaurant, located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/6FpjhEb26W8dy2CE9|2861 Sunset Boulevard}} in the {{WP|Silver Lake, Los Angeles|Silver Lake}} neighborhood of {{WP|Los Angeles}}, {{WP|California}}, which closed in 2013. The space has since been modified and is now a Japanese restaurant.
