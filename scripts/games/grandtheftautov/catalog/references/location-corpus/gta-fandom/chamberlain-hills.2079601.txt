[[LEAD]]
'''Chamberlain Hills''' is a neighborhood in the [[South Los Santos|south region]] of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is bordered by [[Strawberry]] to the north, [[La Puerta]] to the south and west, and [[Davis]] to the east.
