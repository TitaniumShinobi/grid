[[LEAD]]
The '''Italian Republic''' (Italian: '''Repubblica Italiana'''), simply known as '''Italy''' (Italian: '''Italia'''), is a country in southwestern [[Europe]]. The capital and largest city is {{WP|Rome}}. The country is bordered by [[France]] to the west, [[Switzerland]] and [[Austria]] to the north and [[Slovenia]] to the east, as well as the enclaves of {{WP|San Marino}} and [[Vatican City]].

*[[Saint Mark's]], [[Portland]], [[Liberty City (3D Universe)|Liberty City]], has a large Italian community.

*There are Italian flags on the [[Orbit]] building in east [[Belleville Park]], [[Staunton Island]].
*Until its [[Bringing the House Down|destruction]], [[Fort Staunton]] on Staunton Island was home to many Italian businesses.
