[[LEAD]]
'''AntEater Antivrus''' is an antivirus software found in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'', developed by Viraware. It is installed on [[Fruit (HD Universe)|Fruit]] laptops in the [[Lifeinvader Office]]. It is also installed on some computers in the air traffic control tower in [[Fort Zancudo]] and also installed in the computer at [[Vanilla Unicorn]].
