[[LEAD]]
'''Zancudo Road''' is a two-way [[List of Street Names|road]] in {{V}} and {{O}}, located between [[Los Santos County]] and [[Blaine County]] near the [[Tongva Valley]]. The road begins at [[Route 68]] and ends at [[Zancudo Barranca]]. A vineyard is located on the road.
