The '''Murrieta Oil Field''' is an industrial area in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' in metropolitan [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], bordering [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]] to the west, [[El Burro Heights]] to the north and the [[Palomino Highlands]] to the east on [[El Burro Boulevard]].

This area is supposedly rich in petroleum or crude oil deposits. The land contains various [[Brute (HD Universe)|Brute]] pump jacks, which bob up and down to pump the crude oil from the ground.

There is a [[Car Scrapyard|car scrapyard]] in this area, which can be purchased by either [[Michael De Santa|Michael]], [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] or [[Trevor Philips|Trevor]] for [[Money|$]]275,000. After buying the [[Properties in GTA V|property]], the player will receive $150 for each destroyed car. It will also occasionally come under attack from [[The Lost MC]].
