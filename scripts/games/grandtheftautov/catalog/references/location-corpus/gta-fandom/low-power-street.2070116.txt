[[LEAD]]
'''Low Power Street''' is a two-way [[List of Street Names|street]] in [[Pillbox Hill]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:2:Places of Interest]]
*The construction site hole for access to [[Pillbox North Station]] is located in the car park where the [[Cutter]] is [[Driller|parked]] for the obvious approach to [[The Big Score (GTA V)|The Big Score]]).

[[SECTION:2:Businesses]]
*[[Ground & Pound Café]] <small>(corner of San Andreas Avenue)</small>
*Lower levels of the [[Union Depository]], notably where [[Franklin Clinton]] and [[Lamar Davis]] drive through to end their impromptu race in [[Franklin and Lamar]].
