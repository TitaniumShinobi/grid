'''Lamar's Depot''' is a depot appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}, owned by [[Lamar Davis]]. It is part of a large warehouse located on the border of [[Murrieta Heights]] and [[El Burro Heights]]. The depot is in the Murrieta Heights (west) side of the warehouse.
