[[LEAD]]
'''Raton Pass''' is a long rural [[List of Street Names|road]] in the [[Raton Canyon]] National Park, [[Blaine County]], that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It runs from [[Cassidy Trail]] in the west to [[Calafia Road]] in the east.
