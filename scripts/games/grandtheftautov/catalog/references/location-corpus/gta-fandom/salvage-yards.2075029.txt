[[LEAD]]
'''Salvage Yards''' are purchasable properties in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' added in [[GTA Online: The Chop Shop|The Chop Shop]] update.

[[SECTION:3:Salvage Yard Robberies]]
{{Main|Salvage Yard Robberies}}
Salvage Yard Robberies are a series of illegal jobs consisting of two preparations and the main job, which are executed in a similar manner to [[Heists in GTA Online|heists]]. These jobs consist of freemode preparations and the finale. Each robbery targets a specific car. The target vehicle and the type of the robbery changes every [[Events|event]] week.

After the completion of a robbery, the player decides whether they ship the car to Yusuf via the [[Terminal]], salvage the car parts, or keep the vehicle for themselves. The last option is only available if a specific vehicle is designated for this option during the event week.
