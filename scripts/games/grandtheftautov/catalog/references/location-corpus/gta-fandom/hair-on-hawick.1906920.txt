'''Hair on Hawick''' is a barber shop in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located on [[Hawick Avenue]] in [[Hawick]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. The shops' hair style program is known as traditional. The shop share the same interior and haircut options as the [[Herr Kutz Barber]], [[O'Sheas Barbers Shop]] and [[Beach Combover Barber]] shop.
