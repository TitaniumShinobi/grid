[[LEAD]]
'''Hayes Autos''' is an auto repair garage appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}, located on [[Little Bighorn Avenue]], [[South Los Santos]].

[[SECTION:3:The Diamond Casino & Resort]]
In [[2019]], the garage was used by a heist crew to plan a heist on [[The Diamond Casino & Resort]]. After [[Best Laid Plans|tracking down]] the heist crew members, the [[GTA Online Protagonist|protagonist]] finds the heist planning room and kills the heist crew members.
