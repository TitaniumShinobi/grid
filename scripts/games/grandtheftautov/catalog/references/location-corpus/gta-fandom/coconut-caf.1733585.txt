[[LEAD]]
The '''Coconut Café''' is a small restaurant and café in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located on [[Bay City Avenue]] and [[Goma Street]], [[La Puerta]] in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. The business sells sandwiches, bagels, coffee, milkshakes, and seafood. According to the logo, it was established in [[1967]].
