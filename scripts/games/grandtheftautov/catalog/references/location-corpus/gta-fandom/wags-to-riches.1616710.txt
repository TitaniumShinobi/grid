'''Wags to Riches''' is a pet accessories store in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located opposite the entrance to [[Los Santos Customs]] on [[Eastbourne Way]] in [[Burton]] and [[Rockford Hills]]. The store sells animal accessories, doggie bags and pet jewelry, some of which are depicted on its windows.
