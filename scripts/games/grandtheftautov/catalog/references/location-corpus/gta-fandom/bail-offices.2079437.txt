[[LEAD]]
A '''Bail Office''' is a purchasable property in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' added as part of the [[GTA Online: Bottom Dollar Bounties|Bottom Dollar Bounties]] update.

[[SECTION:3:Office]]
The office is the main area of the business. This is where Jenette, as well as two unnamed enforcement agents if hired, will be working. The holding cell is also located in this area, where bounty targets will be held for processing if brought in alive.

[[SECTION:3:Player's office]]
Behind Jenette's desk is the player's office. A computer there will allow the player to start bounty hunting missions. If purchased, the player's office will feature a small storage room used as the player's personal quarters. In the office, one of the desks will gain a plaque with a certificate of bail enforcement after the player has completed at least 10 bail target missions, a trophy of a golden sheriff star after completing at least 100 bail target missions, and a trophy of golden handcuffs after the player brings in all 24 unique bail jumpers alive. Also, completing four bounties will result in Jenette replacing the plant on the computer desk.

[[SECTION:3:Garage]]
Near the main entrance, there is a staircase leading into the garage, where the [[Burrito (Bail Enforcement)|Bail Enforcement Van]] is stored. Two [[Personal Vehicles]] may also be stored inside the garage.{{clr}}
