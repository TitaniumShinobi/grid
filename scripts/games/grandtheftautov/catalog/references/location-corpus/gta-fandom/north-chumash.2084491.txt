[[LEAD]]
'''North Chumash''' is a small unincorporated community located in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is situated north of [[Fort Zancudo]], southwest of the [[Chiliad Mountain State Wilderness]], west of [[Mount Josiah]] and sits on the coastline of the [[Pacific Ocean (HD Universe)|Pacific Ocean]].

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto V'']]
The Hookies bar and seafood restaurant is available for purchase by [[Michael De Santa]] or [[Franklin Clinton]] for [[Money|$]]600,000 after completing the mission [[Nervous Ron]] but not by Trevor Philips, due to his violent conflict with [[The Lost MC|The Lost]], who use Hookies as an area of congregation.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto Online'']]
The two-car garage [[2000 Great Ocean Highway]], located next to Hookies, is available for the [[GTA Online Protagonist|player]] to purchase for $31,500.
