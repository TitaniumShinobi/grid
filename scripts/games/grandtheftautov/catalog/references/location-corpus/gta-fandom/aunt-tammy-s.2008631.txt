'''Aunt Tammy's''' is a hair salon in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located on the corner of [[Mountain View Drive]] and [[Algonquin Boulevard]] in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], near the local [[Ammu-Nation (HD Universe)|Ammu-Nation]]. The store looks abandoned even though the outside light is still on at night.
