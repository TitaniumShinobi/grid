[[LEAD]]
'''Nowhere Road''' is a small dead-end road in {{V}} and {{O}}, located in the [[Grand Senora Desert]], just south of [[Sandy Shores]] and west of the [[Senora National Park]]. It begins at a T-junction at [[Smoke Tree Road]] and abruptly ends a few hundred meters up north in the middle of nowhere.
