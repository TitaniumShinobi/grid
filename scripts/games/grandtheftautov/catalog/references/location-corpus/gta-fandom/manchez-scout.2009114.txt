[[LEAD]]
The [[Maibatsu Corporation (HD Universe)|Maibatsu]] '''Manchez Scout''' is a military off-road motorcycle featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] update.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
*Can be found on [[Cayo Perico]], commonly parked at North Dock.
*Available for purchase from [[Warstock Cache & Carry]] for [[Money|$]]225,000.
**A trade price of $168,750 was added as part of the [[GTA Online: San Andreas Mercenaries|San Andreas Mercenaries]] update during [[Assault on Cayo Perico Week]], received as a reward after completing a bonus objective in the [[Assault on Cayo Perico]] [[Adversary Modes|Adversary Mode]].
