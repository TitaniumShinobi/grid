[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
*Before the mission [[Boulevard Baby]], when the player stands in front of Bahama Mamas, there will be music coming from inside the club, but the door won't open.
*In the downloadable version of {{TLAD}}, as well as its version that comes with {{EFLC}}, the club retains its {{IV}} appearance, still being inaccessible. However, [[The Beat 102.7]] can be heard playing from inside.
**On the other hand, in ''[[Grand Theft Auto IV: The Complete Edition|The Complete Edition]]'', the club exterior will exist as in {{TBOGT}}. It is still inaccessible, but now [[The Vibe 98.8]] can be heard playing from inside.
*Albums by [[OG Loc (HD Universe)|OG Loc]] can be found at the [[Paul Martin|DJ]]'s booth.
*There is a [[:File:Secret Bahama Mamas Map.png|map on a desk in the office]] room where [[Vic Manzano]] is killed. It is a copy of a real {{URL|https://www.oceangrafix.com/chart/zoom?chart{{=}}13276|shipping navigation chart for Salem, Marblehead and Beverly Harbors}}. The same chart was originally used in ''Grand Theft Auto IV'' on the bridge of the ''[[Platypus]]''.
