[[LEAD]]
'''Fruit of the Vine Wine Merchant''' is a chain of wine stores featured in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
*The [[Hawick Avenue]] location (alongside the [[Clinton Avenue]] location as they share the same architecture) is based on the now-closed McConnell's Picture Frames at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/hPPePQEJxp3SLxqD7|7969 Melrose Avenue}} in the {{WP|Melrose Avenue|Melrose District}} of {{WP|Los Angeles}}. This location features a large yellow neon sign where Fruit of the Vine's typical store signage originates.
