[[LEAD]]
The '''Fort Zancudo Approach Road''' is a two-way bridge/[[List of Street Names|road]] located in [[Blaine County]] in {{V}} and {{O}}, that begins at [[Route 68]] in [[Great Chaparral]] and ends at one of the entrances of [[Fort Zancudo]].

The Approach Road has an [[Ammu-Nation (HD Universe)|Ammu-Nation]] store next to it located on [[Route 68]], which is ironic, as the player might buy armor and ammo here in order to raid the military base.
