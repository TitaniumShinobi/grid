[[LEAD]]
'''Vespucci Sports''' is a sporting goods retail store on the corner of [[Prosperity Street]] and [[Palomino Avenue]] in [[Vespucci Canals]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It sells sports products made by many brands in the ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' series. In ''Grand Theft Auto Online'', the [[Capture]] mission 'Raid: Waterways' is outside its front door.
