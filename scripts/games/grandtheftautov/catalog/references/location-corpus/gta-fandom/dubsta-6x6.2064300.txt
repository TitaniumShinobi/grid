[[LEAD]]
The [[Benefactor]] '''Dubsta 6x6''' is an off-road pick-up truck featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[The "I'm Not a Hipster" Update]].

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]


[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto V'']]
'''Original version'''
*Available in every [[protagonist]]'s garage after installing The "I'm Not a Hipster" Update in single player.
'''Enhanced version'''
*Can be bought from [[Warstock Cache & Carry]] for [[Money|$]]249,000.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto Online'']]
*Can be purchased for $249,000 from Warstock Cache & Carry.
**Prior to the [[Cunning Stunts: Special Vehicle Circuit]] update, the vehicle could only be purchased after reaching [[rank]] 100.
