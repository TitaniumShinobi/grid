[[LEAD]]
The '''NOOSE Headquarters''' (also called the '''Los Santos Government Facility''') is the main operating base for the [[National Office of Security Enforcement]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is located deep in the [[Palomino Highlands]] within the eastern coast of [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. The headquarters is located east of the city of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] and southeast of the [[Land Act Reservoir]]. It is accessible by an exit on [[Route 15]] and its main entrance is located on [[Sustancia Road]]. The NOOSE headquarters are based on the {{WP|Twin Towers Correctional Facility}} in {{WP|Los Angeles}}.
