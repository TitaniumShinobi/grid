[[LEAD]]
The '''Vespucci Canals''' are a series of waterways located in the district of [[Vespucci]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. The Vespucci Canals are made up of several residential homes built on small islands surrounded by water. The neighbourhood is not very large but is affluent.
