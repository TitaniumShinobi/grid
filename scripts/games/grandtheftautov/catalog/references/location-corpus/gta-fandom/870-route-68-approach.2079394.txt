[[LEAD]]
'''870 Route 68 Approach''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in {{O}}.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in a small industrial site in [[Harmony]], situated between the [[Route 68 Approach]] and [[Joshua Road]]. The site is accessed via a dirt road off Joshua Road. It also sits across the street from [[Stoner Cement Works]]' Harmony location.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]62,500 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
