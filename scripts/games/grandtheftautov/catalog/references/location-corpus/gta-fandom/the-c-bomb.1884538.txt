'''The 'C'* Bomb''' is a store in {{V}} and {{O}}, located in [[Downtown Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]].

The store is not enterable and plays no role in the storyline. The name is a reference to the swear word 'c***'.
