[[LEAD]]
'''Galilee''' is a small unincorporated community located at the foot of [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]], [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] and west of [[Grapeseed]] that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is situated west of [[Grapeseed]] on the northern shores of the [[Alamo Sea]]. The community appears to have some buildings running off the grid while others use generators for power.
