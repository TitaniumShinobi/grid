'''Richman Glen''' is a semi-rural area in [[Vinewood Hills]], in the northwestern part of [[Los Santos County]], [[San Andreas]], featured in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is located between the two hill ranges of [[Vinewood Hills]] and [[Pacific Bluffs]].
