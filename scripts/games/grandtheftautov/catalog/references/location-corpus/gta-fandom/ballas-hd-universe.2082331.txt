[[LEAD]]
The '''Ballas''' are a large [[United States of America|African American]] street gang featured in {{V}} and {{O}}, where they appear as secondary antagonists in the [[GTA Online: Lowriders|Lowriders]] update missions, as well as deuteragonists in [[GTA Online: The Contract|The Contract]] update. They mainly operate in the cities of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] and [[Davis]].
