[[LEAD]]
'''One Armed Bandits''' is a [[Casino Work]] mission featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino & Resort|The Diamond Casino &amp; Resort]] update. It is a freeroam mission given to the [[GTA Online Protagonist|player]] by [[Agatha Baker]].
