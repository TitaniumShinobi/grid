[[LEAD]]
'''Cape Catfish''' is a small unincorporated community located in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is situated north of the [[Humane Labs and Research]] chemical research facility, southeast of [[El Gordo Lighthouse]] and [[Catfish View]], east of [[Grapeseed]] on the shores of the [[Pacific Ocean]]. A dirt road leads into the settlement, which is mainly a fishing dock with a small business with homes scattered around.
