[[LEAD]]
'''Vinewood Boulevard''' is an iconic east-west avenue featured in {{V}} and {{O}}. It runs through [[Downtown Vinewood]] in the city of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. It is a part of [[Route 18]].
