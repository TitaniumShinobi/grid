[[LEAD]]
The '''Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela''', simply known as '''Venezuela''', is a country on the northern coast of South America. It is bordered on the north by the Caribbean Sea and the Atlantic Ocean, on the south by [[Brazil]], on the west by [[Colombia]], Trinidad and Tobago to the north-east and on the east by Guyana.
