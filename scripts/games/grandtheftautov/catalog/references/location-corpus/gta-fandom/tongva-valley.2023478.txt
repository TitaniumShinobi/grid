[[LEAD]]
'''Tongva Valley''' is a rural area located in [[Los Santos County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], in {{V}} and {{O}}. The Los Santos and [[Blaine County]] line is located just south of [[Route 68]]. It is bordered by [[Lago Zancudo]] to the north, [[Tongva Hills]] to the west, [[Richman Glen]] to the south, and [[Great Chaparral]] and [[Vinewood Hills]] to the east.
