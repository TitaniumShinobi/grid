[[LEAD]]
'''White Widow''' is a clothing company featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto IV]]'', ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
{{H4|''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online''}}
*The [[Alta]] outlet is a rendition of the former Chateau Denim store located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/gSeUWXKy9Vb6pfM69|7500 Melrose Avenue}} in the {{WP|Melrose Avenue#Melrose District|Melrose District}} of {{WP|Los Angeles}}, {{WP|California}}.
*The [[Del Perro]] outlet is a rendition of the Anthropologie store located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/g6fq3wD8cBLv6P989|1402 Third Street Promenade}} in {{WP|Santa Monica, California}}.
