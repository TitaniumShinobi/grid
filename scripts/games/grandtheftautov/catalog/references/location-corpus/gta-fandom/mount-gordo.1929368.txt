[[LEAD]]
'''Mount Gordo''' is one of three named mountain peaks in [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', the others being [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]] and [[Mount Josiah]]. Mount Gordo is the smallest of the three. It is located in the northeast of [[Blaine County]]. A small [[Mount Gordo Lake|lake]] can be found here, as well as a small pond. The mountain is the site of the [[Jolene Cranley-Evans]] ghost [[Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA V|easter egg]].

The top of the mountain is one of the two locations where [[Michael De Santa|Michael]] can practice yoga. The other being [[Michael's mansion]].
