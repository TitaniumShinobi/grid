[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos Air''', or alternatively '''Los Santos Airways''', is an airline mentioned in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. Signage bearing the company's name can be found at the Departures entrance at [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]], and the company livery (bearing the alternative name) can be seen on [[Dodo (HD Universe)|Dodos]].
