[[LEAD]]
'''The Bean Machine''' is a coffeehouse chain corporation in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{CW}}, {{V}}, and {{O}}, and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
*The location on [[Prosperity Street Promenade]], [[Del Perro]] is based on the former Promenade Cafe at {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/CgrSRquJcpuuHhAK8|12 Third Street Promenade}} in Santa Monica, California.
*The location on [[Pleasure Pier]], [[Del Perro Pier]], [[Del Perro Beach]] is based on {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/QVeyqkpEK8xW53fx9|The Coffee Bean}} location on the Santa Monica Pier in Santa Monica, California.
