[[LEAD]]
The '''Republic of Poland''' (Polish: '''Rzeczpospolita Polska'''), more commonly known as '''Poland''' (Polish: '''Polska'''), is a country located in {{WP|Central Europe}}. A member of the {{WP|European Union}} since 2004, it is bordered by [[Germany]] to the west; the [[Czech Republic]] and [[Slovakia]] to the south; [[Ukraine]] and [[Belarus]] to the east; and the Baltic Sea, Kaliningrad Oblast (a [[Russia]]n exclave) and Lithuania to the north-east.

Poland is occasionally mentioned in the {{S}}, usually through characters' surnames.
