'''Sally's Surf Shop''' is a surfing store located on [[Paleto Boulevard]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It has been running since [[1964]]. It is apparently Hawaiian-themed as well, as indicated by the writing on the window that says "Aloha" and advertises Hawaiian snow.
