[[LEAD]]
'''3671 Whispymound Drive''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA V|safehouse]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'', given to [[Franklin Clinton]] upon completion of [[The Hotel Assassination]] by [[Lester Crest|Lester]], located on [[Whispymound Drive]] in [[Vinewood Hills]].

The house also makes an appearance in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', now known as '''The Clintons House''' since [[GTA Online: The Contract|The Contract]] update.

[[SECTION:2:Obtaining the Safehouse]]
After The Hotel Assassination, Franklin is told by Lester that his portfolio was getting a "little successful", so he figured tangible assets would be the best investment, but someone would need to live there for "tax purposes" so Franklin was chosen, to his relief as it meant he would no longer need to live with his aunt [[Denise Clinton|Denise]].
