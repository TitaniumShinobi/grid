[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*[[Four Iron]] {{nd}} Four Caddies are parked around the driving range of [[Leaf Links (3D Universe)|Leaf Links]] driving range where the mission's target is playing.
*[[Phnom Penh '86]] {{nd}} Two will always spawn parked directly under the path of the [[Maverick (3D Universe)|helicopter]] in Leaf Links, for some unknown reason.
*[[Stunt Boat Challenge]] {{nd}} The player must jump a ramp located in Leaf Links, passing over a Caddy and three golfers.
*[[Sunshine Autos Import Garage]] {{nd}} The Caddy is one of the 24 vehicles wanted by [[Sunshine Autos]] for their import garage. Placed on the fourth and final list.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*[[Home's on the Range]] {{nd}} Driven by [[Gonzalez]] at the [[Leaf Links (3D Universe)|golf course]].

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas'']]
*It is often found in the car park of the [[Avispa Country Club]] in [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] and [[Yellow Bell Golf Course]] in [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]].
*It is driven around the golf course at the Avispa Country Club in San Fierro.
*Three can be found parked inside the movie studio in [[Vinewood (3D Universe)|Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]].

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*One is parked inside the Leaf Links golf course by the tennis court. In the [[PlayStation 2]] version of the game, the Caddy may be used to trigger the [[Caddy Daddy]] side-mission. 
*Another one is parked close to the entrance of the golf course.
*One is parked at the [[InterGlobal Films|InterGlobal]] movie studio in [[Prawn Island]].

[[SECTION:3:''The Ballad of Gay Tony'']]
*During "[[Practice Swing]]", which is the only way to get one in single player. After the final objective (driving Tony back to his apartment in the Caddy), the vehicle should be right behind the player. One can simply take it and park it at the safehouse.
*One is parked near the mini golf course on [[Firefly Island]] in multiplayer.
*One is parked on the [[Golden Pier Swingers Golf Club|golf course]] in western [[Algonquin]] in multiplayer.
