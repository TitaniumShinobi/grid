[[LEAD]]
The '''Palomino Highlands''' are a hilly, undeveloped region in {{V}} and {{O}} located in southeast [[Los Santos County]]. It is bordered by the [[Pacific Ocean (HD Universe)|Pacific Ocean]] to the east, and by the [[Palomino Freeway]] and the city of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] to the west.
