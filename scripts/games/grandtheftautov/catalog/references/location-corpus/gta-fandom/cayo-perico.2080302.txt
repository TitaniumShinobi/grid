'''Cayo Perico''' (commonly referred to as just ''The Island'' or ''Paradise'') is a private island in the [[Caribbean]], off the coast of [[Colombia]],{{Ref|name=location}} that appears in {{O}} as part of [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] update.

[[SECTION:2:Geography]]
Although the name of the island is "Cayo Perico"—which translates to "Cocaine Cay" in Spanish (or lit. "Parakeet Cay")—Cayo Perico is not a {{WP|Cay#Examples|cay}} in the topographic sense, and is actually a {{WP|high island}} telling by its rocky topography and elevation. With the exception of a few beaches, Cayo Perico contains little sand, with most beaches on the island being covered by small rocks.

Cayo Perico's weather is always set to clear during heist intel or regular visits, therefore having no chance of rain or snow, although there is a very small chance that the weather can be set to a thunderstorm during the Cayo Perico Heist finale.

[[SECTION:2:Points of Interest]]
The island is littered with many historical artifacts and interesting objects, some of which are relevant to the heist and will be able to be sent to [[Pavel]] during [[Heist Prep: Gather Intel|intel gathering]] for [[The Cayo Perico Heist]]. Others are just noteworthy for existing.

The surrounding waters are home to many [[Wrecks]] as well as a large meteorite in the far northwest.
