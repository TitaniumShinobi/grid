[[LEAD]]
'''Didier Sachs''' is a clothing store chain appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
*The [[Star Junction]] location is based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/d3dLWM5THjRgmgWf8|174 Broadway}} in New York.
*The [[Suffolk]] branch building on [[Feldspar Street]] appears to be based on the Bendheim Building at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/hvoQYqUVPERcyhPz5|122 Hudson Street}} in {{WP|SoHo, Manhattan}}.
*The Suffolk branch building on [[Ersatz Row]] appears to be based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/p8T18zMK79F9TQg3A|465 Broome Street}} in {{WP|SoHo, Manhattan}}.
*The [[Big Willis Mall]] location is based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/JF1BhVrVq9fmxPLTA|1-58 Archer Avenue}} in {{WP|Jamaica, Queens}}.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online'']]
*The [[South Boulevard Del Perro]] location is based on the {{WP|Van Cleef & Arpels}} store at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/RmTPfoAp6XUnqgKq5|300 N Rodeo Drive}} in {{WP|Beverly Hills, California}}.
*The [[Prosperity Street Promenade]] location is based on the now-closed {{WP|Armani}} Exchange location on the {{WP|Third Street Promenade}} in {{WP|Santa Monica}}, {{WP|California}}{{Ref|name=armani|type=website|url=https://foursquare.com/v/armani-exchange/44df6360f964a520f0361fe3?openPhotoId{{=}}50dcbee6e4b01d38931257f8|ref=Armani Exchange}}.
*The [[Alta Street]] location is based on the {{URL|https://cornerbakerycafe.com/|Corner Bakery Cafe}} location at the {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/6FcoxJFmWorJxi7Y6|801 Tower}} in Los Angeles.
*The [[Heritage Way]] location is based on the former Francis-Orr store at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/SSh5YgVFzosACUs57|320 N Camden Drive}} in Beverly Hills.
