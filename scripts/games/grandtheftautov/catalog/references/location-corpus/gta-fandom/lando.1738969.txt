[[LEAD]]
'''Lando''' is a shipping company that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. Not much is known about the company, other than that they have operations in the [[Port of Los Santos]]. Lando is likely based on {{WP|American President Lines|APL}}, since both Lando and APL use birds in their logos.
