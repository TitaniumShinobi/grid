[[LEAD]]
'''Fort Zancudo''' is a [[Military (HD Universe)|U.S. military]] installation operated by the {{WP|United States Army|Army}}, the {{WP|United States Marine Corps|Marine Corps}}, and the {{WP|United States Air Force|Air Force}} featured in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:Location]]
[[File:FortZancudoSatellite.png|thumb|Satellite image of '''Fort Zancudo''' and surrounding areas.]]

Fort Zancudo is located on the outskirts of the [[Lago Zancudo]] swamps and at the base of [[Mount Josiah]] in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]].

It is bordered by the [[Zancudo River]] towards the south, as well as [[U.S. Route 1|Route 1]] on the west with the Great Ocean Highway passing underneath the runway of the base via a tunnel. There is also a [[Fort Zancudo Sculptures|memorial]] that can be found by the [[Fort Zancudo Approach Road]].
