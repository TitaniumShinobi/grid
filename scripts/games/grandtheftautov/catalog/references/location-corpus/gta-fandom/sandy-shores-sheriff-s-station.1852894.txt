[[LEAD]]
The '''Sandy Shores Sheriff's Station''' is a sheriff station in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in the same building as the [[Sandy Shores Medical Center]], on [[Alhambra Drive]] in [[Sandy Shores]]. It is one of the two branches in [[Blaine County]] operated by the [[Los Santos County Sheriff|Los Santos County Sheriff's Department]].
