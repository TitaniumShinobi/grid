[[LEAD]]
'''Unit 124 Popular Street''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in [[La Mesa]] under an [[Interstate 4|Olympic Freeway]] on-ramp, and is accessed through alley connected to [[Popular Street]]. It sits adjacent to a small skate park.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]25,000 (or for FREE if the player owns the [[Grand Theft Auto V/Editions|Collector's Edition]]) and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
