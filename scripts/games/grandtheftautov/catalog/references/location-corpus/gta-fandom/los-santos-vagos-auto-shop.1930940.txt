[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos Vagos Auto Shop''' is a collection of buildings located in [[Cypress Flats]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' that serves as a front and originally, the base of operations for the local Mexican gang, the [[Los Santos Vagos (HD Universe)|Los Santos Vagos]].
