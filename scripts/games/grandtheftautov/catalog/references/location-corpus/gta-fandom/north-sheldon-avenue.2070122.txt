'''North Sheldon Avenue''' is a residential [[List of Street Names|street]] in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:2:Places of Interest]]
*A [[Vinewood Hills Dog Exercise Park|dog exercise park]] can be found on the northeast side of the street, near the connection with Normandy Drive.
