[[LEAD]]
The '''Vinewood Casino''', also known as '''Be Lucky: Los Santos''', is a casino appearing in {{V}} and formerly in {{O}}, located on [[Vinewood Park Drive]] in [[East Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], overlooking the [[Vinewood Racetrack]].

On July 23, [[2019]], it was replaced by [[The Diamond Casino & Resort]], as part of [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino & Resort|The Diamond Casino & Resort]] update for ''Grand Theft Auto Online''.
