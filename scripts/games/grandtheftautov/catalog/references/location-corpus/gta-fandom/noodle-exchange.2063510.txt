[[LEAD]]
'''Noodle Exchange''' is a noodle restaurant appearing in {{SA}} and the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
The Noodle Exchange has a branch on [[Columbus Avenue]] in [[The Exchange (GTA IV)|The Exchange]], [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]]. The company also appears on a "Noodle Exchange" {{WP|cup noodle}} advertisement on a billboard in [[Star Junction]], with the slogan "''Use Your Noodle''".

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
*The Noodle Exchange branch in [[The Exchange (GTA IV)|The Exchange]], [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]] is based on One Battery Park Plaza located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/PUBsfJLqRVzeJYkV6|1 Battery Plaza}} in Manhattan, New York.
