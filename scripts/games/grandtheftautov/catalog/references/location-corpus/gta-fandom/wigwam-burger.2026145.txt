[[LEAD]]
'''Wigwam Burger''' is a fast food restaurant chain appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
*[[The Triangle]] branch of Wigwam Burger is located in a replica of the {{WP|Candler Building (New York, New York)|Candler Building}}, which once housed a large McDonald's restaurant.
*The [[Berchem]] branch is a rendition of the structure that once housed a {{WP|Sprint Corporation|Sprint}} branch on the ground floor at 114 Washington Street in {{WP|Hoboken, New Jersey}}. Whereas the real-life counterpart is a two-bay-wide attached building, the Wigwam Burger building forms a block corner and features four bays, including a corner polygonal bay.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'']]
In ''Grand Theft Auto V'', the [[Del Perro]] branch is a replica of a {{WP|The Coffee Bean & Tea Leaf|Coffee Bean Tea Leaf}} store, that used to be located on {{WP|Santa Monica Boulevard|11049 Santa Monica Boulevard, Los Angeles}}.
