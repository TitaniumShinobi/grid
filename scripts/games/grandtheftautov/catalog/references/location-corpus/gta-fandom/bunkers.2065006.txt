[[LEAD]]
'''Bunkers''' (also known as '''Los Santos Airbase Bunkers''', according to the sign) are purchasable properties in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' added in the [[GTA Online: Gunrunning|Gunrunning]] update. Judging by the signs seen on the entrances, it is implied to be former [[Military (HD Universe)|military]] facilities.
