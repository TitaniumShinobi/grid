[[LEAD]]
'''Mountain View Drive''' is a street in [[Sandy Shores]] in {{V}} and {{O}}. It passes through the northern part of [[Marina Drive]] and ends at [[Alhambra Drive]], crossing [[Algonquin Boulevard]] and [[Zancudo Avenue]]. It serves as a spawning point for a [[Utility Van]].
