[[LEAD]]
The '''Hands On Car Wash''' is a purchasable property in {{O}} added in the [[GTA Online: Money Fronts|Money Fronts]] update.

[[SECTION:3:Expanding the Business]]
Purchasing the Hands On Car Wash allows players to wash Personal Vehicles at the car wash for free, and unlocks the ability to expand the operation to [[Higgins Helitours (GTA Online)|Higgins Helitours]] and [[Smoke on the Water (GTA Online)|Smoke on the Water]], each purchased separately from Maze Bank Foreclosures and in any order.

Any passive income generated by both properties can be collected from the safe at Hands On Car Wash. This starts at $750 each and doubles to $1500 after completion of 15 Money Laundering missions for each of the three businesses, and the safe's income maxes out at $100,000, at which point it must be collected for more income to be added. Mr. Faber Work missions also contribute to the 15 missions required for increasing the business income, with each mission completed adding to the totals for all three businesses.
