[[LEAD]]
The '''Republic of Ireland''' (Irish: ''Éire''), simply known as '''Ireland''', is a country located in {{WP|Western Europe}}. The Republic of Ireland occupies approximately five-sixths of the island of Ireland. It shares its only land border with {{WP|Northern Ireland}} to the north-east, which remains part of the [[United Kingdom]].

Ireland uses the [[PEGI]] rating system, as with most [[Europe]]an countries, though many games in the series are still rated by the [[BBFC]].

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories'']]
*[[Joseph Daniel O'Toole]] is of Irish descent, and as a result is unable to be "made" by the [[Italy|Italian]] [[Sindacco Crime Family|Sindacco Family]].

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
*The [[McReary Family]] are a second-generation Irish-American crime family. Their crime affiliates, [[Michael Keane]] and [[Gordon Sargent]], are also of Irish descent.
*[[Phil Bell]] is described as "90% Irish".
*[[Steinway Beer Garden]] and [[Lucky Winkles]] are Irish-themed pubs found in [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]]. These pubs feature many pieces of Irish memorabilia, such as {{WP|Irish language|Gaelic}}/English road-signs for {{WP|Galway}}, {{WP|Cork}} and {{WP|Dublin}}, as well as a {{WP|Hurley (stick)|hurley}} that is hung up behind the bars, a reference to the Gaelic sport of {{WP|hurling}}.
*Most of the northwestern neighborhoods in [[Dukes]] have Irish influences.
*[[The Celebinator]] is from Ireland.
*The second part of ''[[A History of Liberty City]]'' focuses on Irish immigration to Liberty City.
*Several private jets feature the Irish flag on the tail; this might be a reference to the preponderance of Irish tourists in {{WP|Northeastern United States|Northeast US}} cities, such as {{WP|New York City|New York}}, {{WP|Philadelphia}}, {{WP|Washington, D.C.}} and {{WP|Boston}} during the {{WP|Celtic Tiger}} years in the 1990s and the 2000s.
*The in-game website [[American Travel Guide|americantravelguide.net]] mentions Ireland, claiming that if anyone touches the wallet of the person reading the website, they should beat the person up, mocking the stereotypes of drunken violence among Irish people.
*[[Blarneys Stout]] is a parody of {{WP|Guinness}}, a famous stout from Ireland. Its name is a reference to the famous {{WP|Blarney Stone}} in south-west Ireland.
*[[Harry Hall]] is of Irish and [[Italy|Italian]] descent.
