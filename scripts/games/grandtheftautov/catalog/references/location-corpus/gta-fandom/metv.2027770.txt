[[LEAD]]
'''Music Entertainment Television''' ('''MeTV''') is a music and entertainment television station which has been mentioned in {{VCS}}, {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{V}}, {{O}}, and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
Little is known of the ''GTA Vice City Stories'' rendition of MeTV, except that a news branch of the network, known as '''MeTV News''' is primarily featured on the official website of GTA Vice City Stories as a faux news channel reporting on events, features, and [[Characters in GTA Vice City Stories|characters]] in the games. MeTV News is hosted by [[Jenny Mitchell]], with [[Phil Herrick]] as a reporter.
