[[LEAD]]
'''Cholla Springs Avenue''' is a two-way street in [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]], that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. The avenue runs from [[Panorama Drive]] in the west to [[Niland Avenue]] in the east and has connections to [[Mountain View Drive]] and [[Armadillo Avenue]].
