[[LEAD]]
The '''San Andreas Super Sport Series''' also known as '''Hotring Circuit Races 2023''' are a series of [[Stunt Races]], originally introduced to ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' on March 20, 2018, as part of the [[GTA Online: Southern San Andreas Super Sport Series|Southern San Andreas Super Sport Series]] update. They were originally called '''Hotring Circuit''' Races until the [[GTA Online: After Hours]] update and returned to the original name in [[2023]] after [[Hotring Everon Week]].
