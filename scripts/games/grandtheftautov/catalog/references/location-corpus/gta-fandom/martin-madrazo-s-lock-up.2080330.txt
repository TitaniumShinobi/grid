[[LEAD]]
'''Martin Madrazo's lock-up''' is a collection of buildings located on an unnamed dirt road just to the north of [[La Fuente Blanca]], appearing in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. The lock-up only plays a role in ''GTA Online'', where it is often used as an alternative to his mansion for larger items the [[GTA Online Protagonist|protagonist]] must deliver for [[Martin Madrazo|Martin]].
