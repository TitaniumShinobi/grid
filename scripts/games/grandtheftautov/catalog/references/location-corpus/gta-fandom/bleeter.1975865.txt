'''Bleeter''' is a social networking and microblogging service that appears in ''[[The Ballad of Gay Tony]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]''. The service is an obvious parody of {{WP|Twitter}}. The player can buy stocks from them at the [[Bawsaq.com]] website in ''Grand Theft Auto V''.
