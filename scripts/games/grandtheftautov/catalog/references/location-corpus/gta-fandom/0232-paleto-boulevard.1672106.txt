[[LEAD]]
'''0232 Paleto Boulevard''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[Paleto Boulevard]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], featured in the [[Independence Day Special]] for ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. Discounting business/mission-related properties, it is currently the northernmost purchaseable safehouse in San Andreas, being slightly further north than the two nearby safehouses on [[Procopio Drive]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]121,000 and includes a 2-car garage.
