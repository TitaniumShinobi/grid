[[LEAD]]
'''Arena War''' is a business featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of the [[GTA Online: Arena War|Arena War]] update, which allows the player to buy an [[Arena Workshop]], as well as upgradeable and battle-ready vehicles from their website '''arenawar.tv'''.

[[SECTION:3:Arena-ready]]
There are a total of 15 vehicles already modified for use in the arena, categorized into the same three types as those available from the upgradeable vehicles.
