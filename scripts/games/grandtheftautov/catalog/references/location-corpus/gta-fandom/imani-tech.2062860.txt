[[LEAD]]
'''Imani Tech''' is a vehicle parts manufacturer featured in {{O}} as part of [[GTA Online: The Contract|The Contract]] update. Players can apply special Imani Tech customization options to select vehicles in their [[Agency]]'s [[Vehicle Workshop]].

[[SECTION:3:Slick Proximity Mine]]
Slick Proximity Mines can be outfitted to the underside of vehicles. In a similar fashion to [[Vehicle Mines]], these can be dropped{{vague|how often?}}, causing any vehicle driving over them to temporarily lose control of their steering wheel and brakes.

Slick Proximity Mines are available on select vehicles for a fixed price of $135,000.
