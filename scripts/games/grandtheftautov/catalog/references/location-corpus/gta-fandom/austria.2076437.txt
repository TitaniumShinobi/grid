[[LEAD]]
The '''Republic of Austria''', (German: ''Republik Österreich'') simply known as '''Austria''', is a country in {{WP|Central Europe}}. The country is landlocked and bordered by [[Italy]] and [[Slovenia]] to the south, the [[Czech Republic]] and [[Germany]] to the north, [[Hungary]] and [[Slovakia]] to the east, and [[Switzerland]] and [[Liechtenstein]] to the west.
