[[LEAD]]
'''Cheng Family Holdings''' is a [[China|Chinese]] holding company featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino & Resort|The Diamond Casino &amp; Resort]] and [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino Heist|The Diamond Casino Heist]] updates, owned by the [[Cheng Family]].

[[SECTION:3:The Diamond Casino & Resort]]
[[File:ChengFamilyHoldings-GTAO-PhoneMessage.png|thumb|right|150px]]
After [[2014|five years]] investing, the Cheng Family Holdings finally finished building the [[The Diamond Casino & Resort|Diamond Casino &amp; Resort]] business in Los Santos in which is then put under [[Tao Cheng]]'s management under his [[Mr. Cheng|uncle]]'s orders in Hong Kong. However, the family immdediately runs into trouble with the [[Duggan Crime Family]] who wants to destroy the casino and is employing underhanded tactics to sabotage it so they can buy it back at a cheap price. Tao Cheng then attend a meeting with [[Avery Duggan]] to make it clear that the Cheng Family Holdings has no interest in sellling, but the situation escalated, leading to [[Cheng Family-Duggan Family War|an all-out war]] between two families. Although Tao's uncle allowed the family to fight this war initially, he soon changed his mind and ordered Tao to sell the Casino after too much heat's been generated around it. Thanks to a deal with [[Thornton Duggan]], Tao was able to get the full price for the casino after the [[GTA Online Protagonist|online protagonists]] [[Casino - Cashing Out|assassinated]] Avery Duggan.

[[SECTION:3:The Diamond Casino Heist]]
In early December [[2019]], players with a [[Master Penthouse]] received a cryptic text messages from the Cheng Family Holdings saying ''"We don't blame you. Revenge will be sweet."''. A few days later, the online protagonist is contacted by [[Lester Crest|Lester]] for a meeting at [[Mirror Park]] where they meet [[Georgina Cheng]], Vice President of Cheng Family Holdings, and her assistants. Through Georgina, Lester and the online protagonist are tasked with robbing the Diamond Casino and Resort as revenge against the Duggan Crime Family for taking advantage of Georgina's brother Tao. Georgina and her staff later show up at the protagonist's arcade garage to hear the plan and execution Lester laid out. The heist is eventually pulled off, which collected the Chengs' revenge on the Duggans. Lester kept his promise with Georgina of robbing the casino on a weekly basis, allowing the online protagonist to replay the heist with different approaches freely.
