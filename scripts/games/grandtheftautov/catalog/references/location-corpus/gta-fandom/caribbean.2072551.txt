The '''{{WP|Caribbean}}''' is a region of the Americas that comprises the Caribbean Sea, its surrounding coasts, and its islands (some of which lie within the [[Caribbean Sea]] and some of which lie on the edge of the Caribbean Sea where it borders the North Atlantic Ocean). The region lies southeast of the {{WP|Gulf of Mexico}} and the North American mainland, east of {{WP|Central America}}, and north of {{WP|South America}}.

In the ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' series, the Caribbean is often referenced. The Caribbean region of [[Colombia]] makes an appearance in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] update.
