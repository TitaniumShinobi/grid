[[LEAD]]
'''Gruppe Sechs''' (German for '''group six,''' also written as '''gruppe 6''') is a private security company and riot control service appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
Gruppe Sechs security guards are not present in storyline missions or in traffic in ''Grand Theft Auto IV'' or {{EFLC}}, with Securicars driven by civilians and no deadly force being used if their vehicles are stolen.

The company's security guards make a minor appearance during the [[Multiplayer in GTA IV|multiplayer mode]] [[Bomb Da Base II]], driving Securicars in three possible spawn points around [[Algonquin]].
