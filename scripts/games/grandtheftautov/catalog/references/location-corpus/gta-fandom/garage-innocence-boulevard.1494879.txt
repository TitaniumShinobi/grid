[[LEAD]]
'''Garage Innocence Boulevard''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located at the [[Globe Oil]] gas station on the corner of [[Alta Street]] and [[Innocence Boulevard]] in [[La Puerta]]. It shares the building with a [[Discount Beauty Store]]. Unlike most other Garage and [[Apartments|Apartment]] properties, it is not listed under a full address on [[Dynasty 8]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]34,000 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
