'''Inspector Knickers''' is a lingerie retail store in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Location(s)]]
The store can be found on [[Hawick Avenue]], [[Alta]]. [[Trevor Philips]] will occasionally be found leaving the store dressed in women's lingerie when the player [[Switch Scenes|switches]] to him. The store is otherwise inaccessible to the player.
