[[LEAD]]
'''La Puerta Freeway''' or '''Interstate 5''' is a freeway that runs through the southern part of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. It starts/ends after a T-Intersection off the [[Del Perro Freeway]] and runs through West Los Santos as the [[Miriam Turner Overpass]] before it ends at the [[Port of Los Santos]] and turns into [[Elysian Fields Freeway]].

The La Puerta Freeway is an Interstate highway in the in-game [[Interstate System in GTA V|Interstate system]]. It is one of the four Interstates in {{V}} and {{O}}.
