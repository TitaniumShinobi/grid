[[LEAD]]
The '''San Andreas Highway Patrol (SAHP)''' is a state level agency that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
The San Andreas Highway Patrol does not operate any stations, but they are seen patrolling highways in ''GTA V'' and ''GTA Online''.

*One SAHP officer can be seen ending a traffic stop on the [[Palomino Freeway]] next to the train bridge and [[NOOSE Headquarters]] between 11 AM and 6 PM.
*One SAHP officer can be seen watching the [[Senora Freeway]] through binoculars next to the Senora Freeway and [[Route 68]] interchange between 11 AM and 3 PM.
*Two SAHP officers can be seen on the side of the Senora Freeway next to [[Davis Quartz]] between 3 PM and 6 PM. One will be sitting on their motorcycle while the other is standing and holding coffee.
*Two SAHP officers can be seen on the side of the Senora Freeway south of [[Grapeseed]] between 7 AM and 11 AM. One will be sitting on their motorcycle while the other is standing and holding a phone.
*Two SAHP officers can be seen on the side of [[Route 1]] south of the [[Paleto Forest]] between 3 PM and 10 PM. One will be sitting on their motorcycle while the other is standing and holding coffee.
*Three SAHP officers can be seen in [[Raton Canyon]] on a ledge south of the [[Altruist Camp]], as part of the ''{{WP|Thelma and Louise}}'' [[Secrets and Easter Eggs in GTA V|Easter egg]], between 7 PM and 8 PM. They will be standing next to two [[Sheriff Cruiser]]s and accompanied by an [[International Affairs Agency|IAA]] agent and two [[National Office of Security Enforcement|NOOSE]] members in a helicopter that can change between a [[Buzzard]] and a [[Police Maverick (HD Universe)|Police Maverick]].
