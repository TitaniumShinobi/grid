[[LEAD]]
'''142 Paleto Boulevard''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located on [[Paleto Boulevard]] in [[Paleto Bay]], to the left to the [[Xero]] Gas Station.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]26,500 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
