[[LEAD]]
'''El Burro Heights''' is a suburban neighborhood in [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]] in {{V}} and {{O}}. It borders [[Murrieta Heights]] to the north, [[Palomino Highlands]] to the east, [[Terminal]] to the south, and [[La Mesa]] and [[Cypress Flats]] to the west.
