[[LEAD]]
'''Atlee Street''' is a [[List of Street Names|street]] in [[Mission Row]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], in {{V}} and {{O}}. It runs from [[Little Bighorn Avenue]] in the east to [[Strawberry Avenue]] in the west. The street also connects to [[Sinner Street]]. The street is one-way between Strawberry Avenue and Sinner Street. The one-way section is home to a large number of small jewelry stores.
