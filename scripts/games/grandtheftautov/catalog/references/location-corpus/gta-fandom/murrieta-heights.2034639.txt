[[LEAD]]
'''Murrieta Heights''' is a neighborhood in the [[East Los Santos|east region]] of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], appearing in {{V}} and {{O}}. It borders [[El Burro Heights]] to the south, [[Mirror Park]] and [[East Vinewood]] to the north, [[Tataviam Mountains]] to the east, and [[La Mesa]] to the west.
