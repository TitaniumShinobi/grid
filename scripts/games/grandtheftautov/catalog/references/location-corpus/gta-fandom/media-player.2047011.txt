[[LEAD]]
The '''Media Player''' is a customizable radio station featured in {{O}} as part of the [[GTA Online: Los Santos Tuners|Los Santos Tuners]] update on July 20, 2021. The player can be unlock and populate the station with predefined music by finding [[Media Sticks]] around Los Santos, which comprise of genres such as pop, rock, R&B, disco, funk, house, and techno.
