[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos Customs''' is a vehicle customization and repair shop featured in {{V}} and {{O}}. The player can buy stocks from them at [[Bawsaq.com]].

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
There are four Los Santos Customs garages in [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]:

<gallery position="center" spacing="small" widths="250px">
LosSantosCustoms-GTAV-Burton.png|[[Carcer Way]], [[Burton]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]].
LosSantosCustoms-GTAV-LaMesa.png|[[Popular Street]], [[La Mesa]], Los Santos.
LosSantosCustoms-GTAV-LSIA.png|[[Greenwich Parkway]], [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]], Los Santos.
LosSantosCustoms-GTAV-Harmony.png|[[Route 68]], [[Harmony]], [[Blaine County]].
</gallery>

It is possible to enter all branches of Los Santos Customs (and [[Beeker's Garage]]) on foot. The Los Santos Customs branch in [[Harmony]] and Beeker's Garage can both be accessed through a small side door to the left of the vehicle entrance, while the branches in [[Burton]], [[La Mesa]], and [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]] must be accessed by parking a car far enough into the entrance that the door can't close, but not so far that the [[Cutscenes|cutscene]] of the car entering is triggered, before getting out of the car and walking in.

[[SECTION:2:Property]]
The Harmony branch of the company is one of the [[Properties in GTA V|properties]] available to be purchased by [[Franklin Clinton]] for [[Money|$]]349,000 and generates a weekly income of $1,600. It is available to purchase after the mission [[Nervous Ron]].

[[SECTION:3:E-Mail]]
Upon purchasing the Harmony Los Santos Customs shop as Franklin, [[Hao]] will email the player regarding the purchase:

{{Quote|WTF? Just heard you bought a franchise in LS Customs, man??? Crazy. Clearly your game's not as weak as I thought it was! Well I guess I can't charge you for work any more :) Just please promise me you won't make me call you boss... Say hi to that shit-talker Lamar.|Hao's e-mail}}

[[SECTION:3:Benefits]]
If Franklin purchases the Los Santos Customs garage on [[Route 68]], all vehicle modifications will be free, although only at that location and only for him. However, it is still possible to take advantage of this and customize other characters' [[Personal Vehicles]] for free (e.g. [[Michael De Santa|Michael]]'s [[Tailgater]] and [[Premier]], [[Trevor Philips|Trevor]]'s [[Bodhi]], [[Amanda De Santa|Amanda]]'s [[Sentinel]] and [[Tracey De Santa|Tracey]]'s [[Issi]]). To do this, players need to acquire these vehicles as Franklin; in the case of Michael and Trevor, players will need to move them and their personal vehicle close to Franklin in the game world, before [[Switch Scenes|switching characters]] and taking the car as Franklin. Amanda's and Tracey's cars, meanwhile, can be found at the [[De Santa Residence]].
