[[LEAD]]
'''Unit 2 Popular Street''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in a [[National Transfer & Storage Co.|small warehouse complex]] accessed off of [[Popular Street]] in [[La Mesa]], along with [[Unit 14 Popular Street]]. The alley between Unit 14 and the [[Maibatsu Factory]], accessed off [[Supply Street]], may also be used to enter the complex.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]142,500 and can store 10 [[Personal Vehicles]] and three [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
