'''4584 Procopio Drive''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[Procopio Drive]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], featured in the [[Independence Day Special]] for ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]155,000 and includes a 6-car garage.
