[[LEAD]]
The '''Senora Freeway''' ([[U.S. Route 1]] and [[U.S. Route 13]]) is a freeway in {{V}} and {{O}} that runs through [[Los Santos County]] and [[Blaine County]] in [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. Its southern end is at the meeting of the two freeways, the [[Los Santos Freeway (HD Universe)|Los Santos Freeway]] and the [[Palomino Freeway]] in the northeastern part of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] and travels north until reaching [[Dignity Village]], where it becomes the [[Great Ocean Highway]].
