[[LEAD]]
'''0897 Mirror Park Boulevard''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located on the side the [[Downtown Cab Co.]] Depot in [[Mirror Park]] facing [[Mirror Park Boulevard]]. It sits adjacent to [[BJ's Market]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]34,000 and can store two [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
