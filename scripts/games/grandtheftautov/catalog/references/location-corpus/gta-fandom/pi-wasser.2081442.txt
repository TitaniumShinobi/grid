'''Pißwasser''', stylized as '''PIßWASSER''' ([[BAWSAQ]]: PIS) (English: /ˈpɪsˌwɑsər/; German: /ˈpɪsˌvasɐ/), Anglicised as '''Pisswasser''' or '''Piswasser''', is a German export lager frequently advertised on radio and TV in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}. The player can buy stocks from them on the [[Bawsaq.com]] website in {{V}}.
