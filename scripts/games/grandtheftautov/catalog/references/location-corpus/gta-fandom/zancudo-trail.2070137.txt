[[LEAD]]
'''Zancudo Trail''' is a two-way unpaved road in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', running from the [[Great Ocean Highway]] in [[North Chumash]] to [[Route 68]] in [[Great Chaparral]]. A large portion of it runs alongside the [[Zancudo River]] and the [[Lago Zancudo]] wetlands.
