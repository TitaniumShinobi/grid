[[LEAD]]
'''The Lab''' is a [[Radio Stations in GTA V|radio station]] featured in {{V}} and {{O}} that plays {{WP|Hip-hop|hip hop}}. The station was initially released with the [[Personal Computer|PC]] version of ''GTA V'', but was later added to other versions as part of the [[Ill-Gotten Gains Part 2]] update.
