[[LEAD]]
'''Fruit''', also known as '''Fruit Computers''', is a technology corporation appearing in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{CW}}, {{V}}, {{O}}, and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
While Fruit does not have a strong physical presence in {{IV}}, they make up for it in physical and Internet advertising. Advertisements for the [[IFruit|iFruit 3G]], as well as the uHelmet in {{TBOGT}}, can be seen in [[Star Junction]], and Fruit serve as sponsors for the [[WTF]] musical and ''[[A History of Liberty City]]'' documentary on [[CNT]]. Players can also visit Fruit's website, [[Fruitcomputers.com]], and explore details about the iFruit 3G. A Fruit computer with a synthesized female voice serves as the host of [[The Journey]] radio station, further implying the technological capabilities of Fruit. 

As mentioned on the [[Outsourceforamerica.com|Outsource For America]] website, Fruit's CEO, Doug Hatchet, has outsourced all of Fruit's employees overseas, except himself. Doug now is the sole occupant of the Fruit Headquarters. All the company has left in it is a broken copier and an empty stapler.

There is also a seemingly abandoned Fruit warehouse in [[Port Tudor]], [[State of Alderney|Alderney]], implied to be a former distribution warehouse.
