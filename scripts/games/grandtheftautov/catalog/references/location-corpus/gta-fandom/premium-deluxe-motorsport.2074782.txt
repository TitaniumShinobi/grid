[[LEAD]]
'''Premium Deluxe Motorsport''' is a vehicle dealership in {{V}} and {{O}} . It is owned by [[Simeon Yetarian]].

[[SECTION:2:Business Buildings]]
*[[Simeon's Delivery Garage]]

[[SECTION:4:Test Drive and Purchase]]
{{Help Text|Premium Deluxe Motorsport {{HUDIcons|game=O|carshowroom|yellow}} is now open. View, test drive and purchase vehicles inside the showroom.}} 
The vehicles are available for test drive and purchase in a weekly rotation of five selected vehicles. The vehicle prices recorded here may include any discounts applied during event weeks, and as a result may differ from their standard retail price.

{{Help Text|Purchasing this vehicle will also unlock the livery.}}
Starting with the [[GTA Online: Bottom Dollar Bounties|Bottom Dollar Bounties]] update, vehicles that have hidden or locked liveries applied will now permanently unlock said livery when purchased.
