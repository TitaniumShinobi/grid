[[LEAD]]
'''Betta Pharmaceuticals''' is a pharmaceutical company that appears in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Stock Market]]
In ''GTA V'', the player can buy and sell stocks from Betta Pharmaceuticals on the [[Bawsaq.com|BAWSAQ website]]. Their main competitor on the stock market appears to be [[Bilkinton Research]]. The Betta Pharmaceuticals stock is affected by the events of [[The Hotel Assassination]], part of [[Lester Crest]]'s [[Lester's Assassinations|assassination missions]].
