[[LEAD]]
The [[Canis]] '''Kamacho''' is an off-road utility truck featured in {{O}} as part of [[GTA Online: The Doomsday Heist|The Doomsday Heist]] update, released on February 6th, 2018, during the [[Battle Week]] event, and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
*Available for purchase from [[Southern San Andreas Super Autos]] for [[Money|$]]345,000.

[[SECTION:3:The Diamond Casino & Resort]]
*[[Casino Story Missions]] {{Vague}} {{Nd}} Used by the [[Duggan Crime Family]] and [[The Professionals]].

[[SECTION:3:The Diamond Casino Heist]]
*[[The Diamond Casino Heist/Preparations|The Diamond Casino Heist Preparations]] {{Vague}} {{Nd}} May appear during certain missions involving the Duggan Crime Family and The Professionals.
*[[The Diamond Casino Heist]] Finale {{Nd}} If the heist leader choose [[Chester McCoy]] as the getaway driver, a Kamacho appears as the departure vehicle. The truck is painted in gray, with a Retro Red Stripe livery.
