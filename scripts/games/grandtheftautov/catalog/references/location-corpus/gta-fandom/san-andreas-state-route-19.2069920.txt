'''San Andreas State Route 19''' is a [[Numbered Highways in GTA V|numbered highway]] in {{V}}. The route runs through the center of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] via [[Alta Street]] and [[Davis Ave]], providing direct access to [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]].
