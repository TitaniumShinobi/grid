[[LEAD]]
'''Cassidy Trail''' is a long rural [[List of Street Names|road]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in the [[Raton Canyon]], [[Blaine County]]. It runs from [[Raton Pass]] in the west to [[Calafia Road]] in the east, near the [[Calafia Bridge]].
