[[LEAD]]
'''Ranch''' is a clothing store chain and fashion brand that appears in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{CW}}, {{V}}, and {{O}}.

A [[Ranch (3D Universe)|rendition]] of the chain is also featured in the {{3D}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
*The branch in [[Leftwood]], [[State of Alderney|Alderney]] is based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/w3LKv8LGewgd4s277|4 Washington Street}} in {{WP|Tenafly, New Jersey}}.
*The branches in [[The Exchange (GTA IV)|The Exchange]], [[Algonquin]], [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]] are based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/d3dLWM5THjRgmgWf8|174 Broadway}} in the {{WP|Financial District, Manhattan|Financial District}} of {{WP|Manhattan}}, New York.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online'']]
*The branch in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos County]] is a rendition of the {{WP|Superdry}} outlet, which has since closed, at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/K5gR2PJUEhpfthCK8|1334 Third Street Promenade}} in {{WP|Santa Monica, California}}.
