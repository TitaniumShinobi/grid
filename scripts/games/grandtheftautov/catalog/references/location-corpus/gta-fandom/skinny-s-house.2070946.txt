[[LEAD]]
'''Skinny's House''' is the residence of [[Skinny]] in {{V}} and {{O}} located on [[Fudge Lane]] in [[El Burro Heights]], [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]].

[[SECTION:3:Interior]]
The house's interior can only be accessed during the mission [[Compound Interest]], with a layout largely based on that of [[Apartments|medium-end apartments]] in ''Grand Theft Auto Online'', although it features props from various other homes. The living room is spacious, with a flatscreen TV and a bong on the table. It features an open kitchen and a small bathroom with a shower, and a circular wooden dining room table topped with empty bottles, blueprints and an opened pizza box.

Skinny's bedroom similarly reuses the medium-end interior, although the walls are decorated with posters of [[Motorcycles|motorcycles]]. The bed itself is unmade and littered with trash, and shares its model with the one found in [[Trevor's Trailer|Trevor's trailer]].
