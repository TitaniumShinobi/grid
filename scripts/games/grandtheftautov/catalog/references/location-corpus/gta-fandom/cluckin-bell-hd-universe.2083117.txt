[[LEAD]]
'''Cluckin' Bell''' is a fast food restaurant chain appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
*The [[Northwood]] location is based on {{WP|Mosque No. 7}} in Harlem, Manhattan. The outlet itself may be a reference to an infamous {{URL|https://youtu.be/su0U37w2tws|KFC/Taco Bell location in Manhattan}}, which suffered a serious rodent infestation in early 2007, resulting in the location permanently closing.
*The [[Dukes]] location is based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/AznUctY63TVLS8Fo9|136-02 Roosevelt Avenue}} in Flushing, New York.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online'']]
*The [[Rockford Plaza]] location on [[Las Lagunas Boulevard]] is based on the now-closed California Pizza Kitchen location at the {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/MjjaPAtXuJkCyauJ6|Beverly Center}} in Los Angeles, California.
*The [[Del Perro Pier]] location is based on the Taco Bell location on the {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/KN3koCjtxXYvSVpp8|Santa Monica Pier}} in Santa Monica, California.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]


[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
In {{IV}}, Cluckin' Bell has one branch on [[Huntington Street]] in [[Cerveza Heights]], [[Dukes]], one on [[Denver-Exeter Avenue]] in [[Northwood]] and another on [[Burlesque]] in [[The Triangle]] (both in [[Algonquin]]). There is also one branch on [[Babbage Drive]] in [[Berchem]], [[State of Alderney|Alderney]], which is inaccessible.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online'']]
Cluckin' Bell operates three outlets in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], none of which are accessible to the player. An unused [[Police Scanner]] audio file mentions a Cluckin' Bell restaurant in [[Mirror Park]]. However, the location is not present in the game. It is possible that this location was replaced by [[Horny's Burgers]].
