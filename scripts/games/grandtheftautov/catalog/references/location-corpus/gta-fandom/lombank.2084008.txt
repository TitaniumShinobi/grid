[[LEAD]]
'''Lombank''' is a banking company appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
<div class="mw-collapsible-content">
{{H4|''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars''}}
*The [[City Hall (GTA IV)|City Hall]] branch is based on the {{WP|Citibank}} outlet located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/qsea12bbdS4HwT59A|250 Broadway}} in the {{WP|Civic Center, Manhattan|Civic Center}} neighborhood of Manhattan, {{WP|New York City}}.
*The [[North Holland]] branch appears to be based on the {{URL|https://www.301cathedral.com/|Towers on the Park}} condominium located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/1CMrsrVvgWutqHU2A|301 West 110th Street}} in the {{WP|Harlem}} neighborhood of Manhattan, New York City.
*The [[Hove Beach]] branch is based on a {{WP|Chase Bank}} branch located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/hdN5XQfoT8TCNTw87|1002 Brighton Beach Avenue}} in the {{WP|Brighton Beach}} neighborhood of {{WP|Brooklyn}}, New York City.
{{H4|''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online''}}
*The branch office in [[Little Seoul]] is based on a Chase Bank branch located at {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/CMiYW8mJkymafxaV7|3720 Wilshire Boulevard}} in the {{WP|Koreatown, Los Angeles|Koreatown}} neighborhood of {{WP|Los Angeles}}, {{WP|California}}.
*The bank branch in [[Rockford Hills]] is a rendition of a former {{WP|OneWest Bank}} branch located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/GuuAdyfnVqtWbJKHA|9550 S Santa Monica Boulevard}} in {{WP|Beverly Hills, California|Beverly Hills}}, California.
