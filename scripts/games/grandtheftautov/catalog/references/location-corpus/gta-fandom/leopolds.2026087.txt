[[LEAD]]
'''Leopolds Department Store''' is a luxury department store chain in the [[HD Universe]], first appearing in {{IV}}.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'', ''Episodes'', and ''Chinatown Wars'']]
*The [[Easton]] location is based on {{WP|770 Broadway}}, which was originally {{WP|Wanamaker's}} department store.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online'']]
*The [[Rockford Hills]] location is based on the former Saks Fifth Avenue location at {{WP|9600 Wilshire Boulevard}} in Beverly Hills.
