[[LEAD]]
'''Department of Defense''' was a [[Casino Work]] mission featured in {{O}} as part of [[GTA Online: The Diamond Casino & Resort|The Diamond Casino & Resort]] update. It was a freeroam mission given to the player by [[Agatha Baker]]. The event was removed with [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] update, likely due to its location interfering with [[The Music Locker]] entrance.
