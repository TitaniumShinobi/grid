[[LEAD]]
'''ZiT''' is a music identification and streaming service appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
*When the player downloads {{EFLC}}, the radio stations will be updated to include the music featured in both episodes. However, if the player calls ZiT to identify a track from either episode, it will not be able to identify the song. Despite this, it will still be able to identify a song from the original game if the player is playing one of the two episodes.
**Similarly, in ''[[Grand Theft Auto IV: The Complete Edition]]'', if the player calls ZiT in ''GTA IV'' while an ''EFLC'' song is playing (e.g. [[Funkmaster Flex]] and [[Statik Selektah]]'s mixes on the ''EFLC'' version of [[The Beat 102.7]]), ZiT will not be able to correctly detect them.
