[[LEAD]]
'''Canyon Entertainment''', also known as '''Browne Canyon''', is a media company in the {{3D}} and the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
In {{VCS}}, Canyon Entertainment produced the crime drama ''[[Angel and the Knight]]'' for [[VBS]] in [[1984]].
