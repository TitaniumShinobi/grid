[[LEAD]]
'''Walker''' is a business in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:3:Walker - Port of Los Santos]]
The business' ocean storage warehouse is located on [[Plaice Place]] in the [[Port of Los Santos]]. It is a large green building with "NO PARKING ON THIS SIDE" written on its left wall, and "OCEAN STORE" written on its roof.

Two small sailboats are located in the rear dry-dock, as well as an unusable [[Tug (boat)|Tug]].
