[[LEAD]]
'''Pillbox Hill''' is the largest neighborhood in [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. Pillbox Hill is based on the real-life neighborhood of {{WP|Bunker Hill, Los Angeles}}.
