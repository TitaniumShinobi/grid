[[LEAD]]
'''0432 Davis Avenue''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in an apartment building accessed through an alley off [[Roy Lowenstein Boulevard]]. The entrance may also be accessed off [[Davis Avenue]] and [[Innocence Boulevard]]. It is directly across the alleyway from the [[Hi-Men]] Cocktail Bar, as well as across the street from [[0754 Roy Lowenstein Boulevard]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]72,500 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
