[[LEAD]]
'''Burger Shot''' is a chain of hamburger fast-food restaurants in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}. The player can buy stocks from them on the [[Lcn-exchange.com]] website in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]''.

[[SECTION:3:Location Influences]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes'']]
*The [[Golden Pier]] location is based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/fMd1ectEKAzfVFRXA|62 Chelsea Piers}} in New York City.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online'']]
*The [[Del Perro Pier]] location is based on the Pizza Hut location on the {{URL|https://goo.gl/maps/4dND2vYzuQzfReGM6|Santa Monica Pier}} in Santa Monica, California.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Grand Theft Auto Online'']]
There are two Burger Shot restaurants in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], located in [[Vespucci Canals]] and the [[Pleasure Pier]]. According to an unused [[Police Scanner]] audio file, a [[Vinewood (HD Universe)|Vinewood]] Burger Shot was intended to be in the game. However, it does not exist. It is possible that it was replaced by Up-n-Atom Burger.
