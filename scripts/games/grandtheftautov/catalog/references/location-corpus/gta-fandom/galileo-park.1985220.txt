[[LEAD]]
'''Galileo Park''' is a municipal park in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos County]], that appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Places of Interest]]
*The park's focal point is the [[Galileo Observatory]].
*The park also contains the [[Arthur's Pass Trails]], a hiking trail to the north of the Observatory.
*The iconic [[Vinewood Sign (HD Universe)|Vinewood Sign]] is located in its eastern side.
