[[LEAD]]
The '''Bank of Liberty''' is a bank chain appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
*The [[Hatton Gardens]] and [[Suffolk]] branches are based on the now-closed {{WP|Valley Bank|Valley National Bank}} branch formerly located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/WoKFLmBKh1AdwyTq5|434 Broadway}} in {{WP|Soho, Manhattan|Soho}}, {{WP|Manhattan}}, {{WP|New York City}}.
*The [[South Slopes]] branch is based on the Greenpoint Savings Bank at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/6AHeo8wCjsPYRajQ7|807 Manhattan Avenue}} in {{WP|Greenpoint, Brooklyn|Greenpoint}}, {{WP|Brooklyn}}, New York City. Curiously, the bank features a plaque on the side elevation which seems to be based on the {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/ep8yw3K1jVUYSGYU7|plaque}} in homage to {{WP|Elkan Naumburg}} located at the Naumburg Bandshell at Central Park.
*The [[East Island City]] branch is based on the now-closed {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/SpzuQtm4DR5yn6My8|HSBC branch}} formerly located at 22-15 43rd Avenue in Long Island City, New York.
