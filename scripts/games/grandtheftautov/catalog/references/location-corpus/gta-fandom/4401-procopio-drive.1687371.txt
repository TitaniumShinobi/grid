'''4401 Procopio Drive''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located on [[Procopio Drive]] in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], featured in the [[Independence Day Special]] update.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty 8]]'s website, dynasty8realestate.com for [[Money|$]]165,000 and includes a 6-car garage.
