[[LEAD]]
'''The Los Santos State Gas Company''', also known as the '''Los Santos Gas Company'''{{Ref|name=LosSantosGas|ref=[[Casino - Play to Win]]}}, is the organization in charge of supplying gas to [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], and its surrounding areas in {{V}} and {{O}}. It owns large storage tanks and silos in [[Cypress Flats]], [[Banning]] and [[Elysian Island]] in the [[Port of Los Santos]].

The gas company is owned by the [[Duggan Crime Family]], with [[Avery Duggan]] serving as its CEO until his death in [[2019]], upon which his nephew [[Mason Duggan]] succeeded him.
