[[LEAD]]
The '''Republic of Peru''', simply known as '''Peru''', is a country in South America. It is bordered by [[Ecuador]] and [[Colombia]] to the north, [[Brazil]] to the east, [[Bolivia]] to the south-east, [[Chile]] to the south, and the Pacific Ocean to the west.

It is referenced throughout the ''Grand Theft Auto'' series.
