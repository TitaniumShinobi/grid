The '''Netherlands''' is a country located in {{WP|Western Europe}}. A founding member of the {{WP|European Union}}, the country is bordered by [[Germany]] to the east, [[Belgium]] to the south, and the North Sea to the north-west.

The Netherlands are occasionally mentioned in the ''Grand Theft Auto'' series, usually through mentions of Amsterdam or the history of [[Liberty City (HD Universe)|Liberty City]].

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories'']]
*In the mission [[Overdose of Trouble]], [[Maria Latore|Maria]] mentions she has been getting some new pills from Holland.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*There is a chain of coffee shops in [[Vice City (3D Universe)|Vice City]] called [[Old Amsterdam Coffee Shop]].
*The clothing shop [[T&A Men]] is based on the Dutch international clothing brand {{WP|C&A}}.
