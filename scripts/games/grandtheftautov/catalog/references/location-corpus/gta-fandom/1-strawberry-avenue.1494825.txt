[[LEAD]]
'''1 Strawberry Avenue''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is accessed through an alley connected to [[Duluoz Avenue]] in [[Paleto Bay]], located between [[Herr Kutz Barber]] and [[Arcades|Pixel Pete's Fun Time Arcade]] (formerly [[Helmut's European Autos]]).. The Dynasty 8 listing's address is incorrect however, as [[Strawberry Avenue]] is a street located in [[South Los Santos]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]26,000 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
