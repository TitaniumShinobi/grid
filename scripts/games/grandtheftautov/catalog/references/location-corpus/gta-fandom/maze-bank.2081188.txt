The '''Maze Bank''' is a chain of banks in the {{HD}} located in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. In {{V}}, the player can buy stocks from the company in the [[Lcn-exchange.com]] website.

In {{O}}, it also runs [[Maze Bank Foreclosures]] (foreclosures.maze-bank.com), a website introduced in the [[GTA Online: Bikers|Bikers]] update.
