[[LEAD]]
'''Canada''' is a country located in {{WP|North America}}. It shares two land borders with the [[United States of America|United States]], via the {{WP|contiguous United States}} to the south and the US state of {{WP|Alaska}} to the north-west, and a separate border with {{WP|Greenland}} (a part of [[Denmark]]) in the north via {{WP|Hans Island}}.

''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' games are released in Canada on the same day as the United States.

Canada is currently home to one Rockstar studio, [[Rockstar Toronto]], which developed the PC versions of {{IV}} and {{EFLC}}. Another Rockstar studio was located in [[Rockstar Vancouver|Vancouver]] until [[2012]], when production merged with the Toronto studio.
