[[LEAD]]
'''Galileo Road''' is a two-way road in {{V}} and {{O}}, located in [[Great Chaparral]] in [[Los Santos County]] that begins at [[Mount Vinewood Drive]] and ends at [[Baytree Canyon Road]].

It is the start point of a race in [[Offroad Races]].
