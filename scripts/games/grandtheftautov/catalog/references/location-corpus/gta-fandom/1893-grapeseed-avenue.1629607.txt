'''1893 Grapeseed Avenue''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[Grapeseed Avenue]] in [[Grapeseed]], [[Blaine County]], featured in the [[Independence Day Special]] for ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]118,000 and includes a 2-car garage.
