[[LEAD]]
'''Vapid Motor Company''', commonly referred to as simply '''Vapid''', is an [[United States of America|American]] automotive manufacturer appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
{{Main|Vapid of Los Santos}}
There is a [[Vapid of Los Santos]] dealership in [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown]] [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. A [[Pillbox Hill Garage|garage]] is located in the building and can be bought by [[Trevor Philips]]. [[Mors Mutual Insurance]] use the dealership as a depot in {{O}}. Vapid also operated an aircraft division, and formerly owned an [[McKenzie Field Hangar|aircraft hangar]] in [[Grapeseed]], which can also be bought by Trevor Philips.
