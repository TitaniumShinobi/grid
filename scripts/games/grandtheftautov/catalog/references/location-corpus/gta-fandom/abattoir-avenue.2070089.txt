[[LEAD]]
'''Abattoir Avenue''' is a road in [[Cypress Flats]] and the [[Port of Los Santos]] that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. It runs from [[Chum Street]] in the northeast to [[Elysian Island]] in the south.
