[[LEAD]]
'''Downtown Vinewood''' is a neighborhood of [[Vinewood (HD Universe)|Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is bordered by [[West Vinewood]] to the west, [[Hawick]] to the south, [[Vinewood Hills]] to the north and [[East Vinewood]] to the east. The district acts as Vinewood's main commercial area, and is focused around the eastern stretches of [[Vinewood Boulevard (HD Universe)|Vinewood Boulevard]].
