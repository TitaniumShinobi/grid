[[LEAD]]
'''Rancho''' is a neighbourhood in southern [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is bordered by [[Strawberry]] to the north, [[Cypress Flats]] to the southeast, [[La Mesa]] to the northeast, and [[Davis]] to the west.
