[[LEAD]]
'''Procopio Truck Stop''' is a truck stop in {{V}} and {{O}} located along the [[Great Ocean Highway]] in [[Procopio Beach]], [[Blaine County]], [[San Andreas]].

The area takes its name from an {{WP|Procopio|infamous Californian bandit}}. It houses the first ever [[Up-n-Atom Burger]] outlet.
