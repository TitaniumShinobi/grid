'''Vice Beach''' is an area in the [[Vice City]] metropolitan area that appears in all three separate [[universes]] of the {{S}}. It is based on the city of {{WP|Miami Beach, Florida}}. It has three different renditions:

*[[Vice Beach (2D Universe)]] {{nd}} A neighborhood of [[Vice City (2D Universe)|Vice City]] in the first {{1}}.
*[[Vice City Beach]] {{nd}} An island in {{VC}} and {{VCS}} located in [[Vice City (3D Universe)|Vice City]], [[State of Florida|Florida]], also referred to as '''Vice Beach'''.
*[[Vice Beach (HD Universe)]] {{nd}} A coastal resort city in {{VI}}, located in [[Vice-Dale County]], [[Leonida]].
