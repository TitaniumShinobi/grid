[[LEAD]]
'''Cavalry Boulevard''' is a road in [[Ludendorff]], [[North Yankton]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is the only known named road in Ludendorff.

During [[Bury the Hatchet]], if playing as [[Michael De Santa]], the player can hear a flashback of Michael discussing his deal with the [[Federal Investigation Bureau|FIB]] with his wife [[Amanda De Santa|Amanda]] while driving on Cavalry Boulevard.
