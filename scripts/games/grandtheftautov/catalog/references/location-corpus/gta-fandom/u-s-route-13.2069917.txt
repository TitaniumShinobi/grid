[[LEAD]]
'''U.S. Route 13''' (or the [[Senora Freeway]]) is one of the [[Numbered Highways in GTA V]]. It begins in [[Grapeseed]], at the northern end of [[Route 1]] and ends at an intersection with [[Route 18]].
