[[LEAD]]
'''8754 Route 68''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located behind the [[Ammu-Nation (HD Universe)|Ammu-Nation]] location on [[Route 68]] in [[Great Chaparral]], near the entrance to [[Fort Zancudo]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]65,000 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
