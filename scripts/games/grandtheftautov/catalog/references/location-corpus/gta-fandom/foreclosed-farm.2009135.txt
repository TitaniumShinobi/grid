The '''Foreclosed Farm''' is a small farm in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is located at the intersection of [[Senora Road]] and [[Baytree Canyon Road]] in [[Great Chaparral]], [[Blaine County]].
