[[LEAD]]
'''Bolingbroke Penitentiary''' is the state prison of [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in {{V}} and {{O}}, lying between the jurisdiction of [[Los Santos County]] and [[Blaine County]].

[[SECTION:2:Events of ''GTA Online:'' The Diamond Casino Heist]]
During the "Prison Guard" variant of the [[Heist Prep: Vault Keycards]] mission for [[The Diamond Casino Heist]], the player must enter the prison and kill a security guard to steal the [[The Diamond Casino & Resort|Diamond Casino]] vault keycards. The player hijacks a [[Prison Bus|prison bus]] and disguises themselves as a prison guard to gain entry without raising suspicion.
