[[LEAD]]
'''Out of Towners''', abbreviated as '''O.o.T.''' and sometimes referred to as '''Out of Towners Welcome''', is a bar and restaurant chain appearing in ''[[Grand Theft Auto IV]]'', ''[[Grand Theft Auto: Chinatown Wars]]'', ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'', and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.
