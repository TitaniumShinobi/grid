[[LEAD]]
The [[Vulcar]] '''Fagaloa''' is a two-door station wagon added to ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of the [[GTA Online: Southern San Andreas Super Sport Series|Southern San Andreas Super Sport Series]] update.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
*Available for purchase from [[Southern San Andreas Super Autos]] for [[Money|$]]335,000.
**No longer available; [[:Category:Vehicles removed for purchase in GTA Online: San Andreas Mercenaries|removed]] from the website as part of the [[GTA Online: San Andreas Mercenaries|San Andreas Mercenaries]] update.
