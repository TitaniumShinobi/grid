The '''Tataviam Mountains''' is a mountain range featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is located east of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], between [[Los Santos Freeway (HD Universe)|Los Santos Freeway]] and [[Palomino Freeway]].
