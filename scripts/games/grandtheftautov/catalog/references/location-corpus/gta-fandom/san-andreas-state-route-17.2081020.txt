[[LEAD]]
'''San Andreas State Route 17''' is one of the [[Numbered Highways in GTA V]]. It is signed as a north–south urban highway that serves most of the industrial areas of [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]], ultimately leading to the [[Port of Los Santos]] using [[Buccaneer Way]]. It connects [[Del Perro]] with East Los Santos via. [[Boulevard Del Perro]] and [[Popular Street]], terminating at [[San Andreas State Route 14|State Route 14]]. The route is signed in-game on guide signs on [[Interstate 4]] and at its eastern end, [[U.S. Route 20]] and [[San Andreas State Route 18]].
