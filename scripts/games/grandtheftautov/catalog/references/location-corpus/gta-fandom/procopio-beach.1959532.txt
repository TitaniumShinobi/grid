[[LEAD]]
'''Procopio Beach''' is a beach in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in northern [[Blaine County]], east of [[Paleto Bay]]. It is located off the [[Great Ocean Highway]] and is located east of the [[Procopio Promenade]]. It includes the [[Procopio Truck Stop]].
