[[LEAD]]
The [[Benefactor]] '''Streiter''' is an all terrain station wagon featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[GTA Online: The Doomsday Heist|The Doomsday Heist]] update, released on December 23rd, 2017, during the [[Festive Surprise 2017]] event.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
*Available for purchase from [[Southern San Andreas Super Autos]] for [[Money|$]]500,000.
**No longer available; [[:Category:Vehicles removed for purchase in GTA Online: San Andreas Mercenaries|removed]] from the website as part of the [[GTA Online: San Andreas Mercenaries|San Andreas Mercenaries]] update.
