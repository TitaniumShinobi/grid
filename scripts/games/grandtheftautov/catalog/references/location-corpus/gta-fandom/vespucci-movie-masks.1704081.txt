'''Vespucci Movie Masks and Memorabilia''' is a [[Masks (clothing)|mask]] and clothing store in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located on [[Vitus Street]] in [[Vespucci Beach]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], south of the pier on the [[Vespucci Beach Sidewalk]]. 

The store sells novelty masks and graphic T-shirts. The store is unlocked after the [[Masks]] heist setup mission in ''GTA V'', but it is available from the start in ''GTA Online''.
