[[LEAD]]
The '''People's Republic of China''' (Chinese: '''中华人民共和国'''), more commonly known simply as '''China''' (Traditional Chinese: '''中國''', Simplified Chinese: '''中国'''), is a country located in east Asia. It is the third-largest country in the world and the second most populous. It is bordered by [[Mongolia]] to the north, [[Russia]] and [[North Korea]] to the north-east, [[Vietnam]], Laos, Myanmar, [[India]], Bhutan and [[Nepal]] to the south, [[Pakistan]] to the south-west, and [[Afghanistan]], Tajikistan, Kyrgyzstan and [[Kazakhstan]] to the west.
