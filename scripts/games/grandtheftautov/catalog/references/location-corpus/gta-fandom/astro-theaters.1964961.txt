'''Astro Theaters''' is a multiplex movie theater chain introduced in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. Its only known location is at the intersection of the [[Prosperity Street Promenade]] and [[Red Desert Avenue]] in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]].
