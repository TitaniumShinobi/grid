[[LEAD]]
The '''Los Santos County Sheriff's Department''' ('''LSSD''') is a county law enforcement agency in {{V}} and {{O}} that operates in [[Los Santos County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], as well as [[Blaine County]], despite the latter being an entirely different county.

[[SECTION:2:Structure]]
The LSSD seemingly follow a rank and structure system similar to the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department. Like the LASD, the majority of uniformed patrol officers in the LSSD are deputies.

[[SECTION:2:Stations]]
:''Main Article: [[Police Stations]]''

The Los Santos County Sheriff Department is large and organized, possessing three major stations throughout [[Los Santos County]] and [[Blaine County]].

*[[Sandy Shores Sheriff's Station]] {{nd}} [[Alhambra Drive]] & [[Mountain View Drive]].
*[[Paleto Bay Sheriff's Office]] {{nd}} [[Paleto Boulevard]] & [[Great Ocean Highway]].
*[[Davis Sheriff/Police Station]] {{nd}} [[Innocence Boulevard]] & [[Roy Lowenstein Boulevard]] (strangely, no LSSD deputies or vehicles spawn here as [[Los Santos Police Department (HD Universe)|LSPD]] officers and vehicles appear at the station instead.)
