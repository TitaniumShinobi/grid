The '''Land Act Dam''' is an arch concrete dam in the [[Los Santos River]], featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It holds the [[Land Act Reservoir]], the main source of water in the city of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], in the middle of the [[Tataviam Mountains]]. It is connected by pipe to what looks like a filtering plant beside the [[Los Santos Freeway]]. It is located in [[Los Santos County]], east of the city. A dirt path runs through its crest, connecting it to  [[East Vinewood]], the Los Santos Freeway and the [[Palomino Freeway]].
