[[LEAD]]
'''ProLaps''' is a sportswear and sport equipment manufacturer appearing in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{CW}}, {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
ProLaps merchandise is first mentioned in ''Grand Theft Auto IV'' via advertisements. In ''[[The Ballad of Gay Tony]]'', ProLaps is shown with an expanded product line, including fighting gloves (as seen in "[[Momma's Boy]]"), [[Parachute|parachutes]], [[Golf Club|golf clubs]] and [[Caddy|golf carts]]. [[Luis Fernando Lopez|Luis]]' [[L.C. Cage Fighters|fight club]] tee is also implied to be produced by ProLaps.
