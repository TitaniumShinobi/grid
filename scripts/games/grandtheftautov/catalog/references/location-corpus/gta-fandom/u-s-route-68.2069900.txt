[[LEAD]]
'''U.S. Route 68''' is an east-west rural [[Numbered Highways in GTA V|numbered highway]] in the state of [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] and appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. The highway passes through rural desert areas of the state and the small town of [[Harmony]]. Route 68 also acts as the border between [[Los Santos County]] and [[Blaine County]].

The [[Random Events in GTA V#Stag Do Running Man|random event]] of the [[Groom in Trouble]] can be found on this road.
