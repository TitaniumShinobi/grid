The '''City of Davis''', simply referred to as '''Davis''', is an incorporated city in southern [[Los Santos County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], appearing in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

'''Davis''' is believed to have been called '''Ganton'''.
