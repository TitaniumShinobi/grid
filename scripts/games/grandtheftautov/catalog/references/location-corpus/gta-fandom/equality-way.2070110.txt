[[LEAD]]
'''Equality Way''' is a [[Streets|street]] located in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in {{V}} and {{O}}. From the south, it begins at [[Bay City Avenue]], passes under [[Red Desert Avenue]], heading northwest until it merges with the [[Del Perro Freeway]], next to the starting point of [[Magellan Avenue]]. It is basically an access street that connects the freeway to Del Perro.

One of the 50 [[Stunt Jumps in GTA V|stunt jumps]] is performed on this road.
