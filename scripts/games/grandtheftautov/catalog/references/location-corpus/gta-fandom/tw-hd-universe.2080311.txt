'''Totally Wireless Internet Cafe''' ('''TW@'''; pronounced ''{{WP|twat}}'') is an [[Internet in GTA IV|internet]] café appearing in the {{HD}} of the {{S}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
In {{IV}}, the player can access TW@ cafés at all times and use computers to browse in-game [[Websites in GTA IV|websites]] for $1. The player can also rob between $9 and $199 from the [[Cash Register|cash register]], which will result in a one-star [[Wanted Level in GTA IV|wanted level]]. In the introductory cutscene to the [[Logging On]] mission, Roman actually says the name of the establishment, confirming that it is indeed pronounced "twat".

In {{TBOGT}}, as well as {{TLAD}} in the standalone {{EFLC}} version, the computers have a new wallpaper: a dark background with a silhouette of the Liberty City skyline and the phrase "I ♥ LC". This may be a reference to the "{{WP|I Love New York}}" logo, which the name of the mission "[[I Luv LC]]" also references.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
In {{IV}}, the [[State of Alderney|Alderney]] branch is a rendition of {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/gCRvhY7QpdFJCXPU7|415 Washington Street}} in Hoboken, New Jersey. Interestingly, 415 Washington Street appears to be an amalgam of the TW@ building and the neighboring tenement to the left with the green cladding.
