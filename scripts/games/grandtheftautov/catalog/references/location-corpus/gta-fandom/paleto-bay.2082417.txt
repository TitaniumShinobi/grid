[[LEAD]]
'''Paleto Bay''' is a small town located in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is situated northwest of the [[Paleto Forest]], west of [[Procopio Beach]] and sits on the coastline of the [[Pacific Ocean]] and the northwestern foot of [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]]. It is mainly a residential area with some shops.
