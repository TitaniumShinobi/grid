[[LEAD]]
'''Record A Studios''' is a recording studio featured in [[GTA Online: The Contract|The Contract]] update for ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is owned and operated by [[Dr. Dre]].

[[SECTION:3:Studio Tour/Short Trips]]
After the completion of [[The Contract: Dr. Dre|Dr. Dre's contract]], DJ Pooh invites the Protagonist and their associate for a tour of the studio, along with [[Franklin Clinton|Franklin]] and [[Lamar Davis|Lamar]]. During the tour, the group makes a visit to the smoking room, where the Protagonist and their associate smoke Lamar's [[LD Organics]] "Special Strain" and have an "out-of-body experience" whereby they lose consciousness and undertake the [[Short Trip - Seed Capital|first of three Short Trips]], seemingly becoming Franklin and Lamar.
