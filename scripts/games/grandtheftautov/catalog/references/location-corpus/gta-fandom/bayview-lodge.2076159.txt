[[LEAD]]
'''Bayview Lodge''' is an inaccessible lodge featured in {{V}} and {{O}}. 

It is located on [[Procopio Promenade]] in [[Paleto Forest]], [[Blaine County]]. Aside from it being a lodge, it also has a diner and general store.
