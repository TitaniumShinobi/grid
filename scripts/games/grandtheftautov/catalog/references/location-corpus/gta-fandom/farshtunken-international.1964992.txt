[[LEAD]]
'''Farshtunken International''' is a high-end jewelry and accessories business appearing in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
The store on Portola Drive in {{V}} is a replica of a {{WP|Harry Winston, Inc.}} store located at 310 North {{WP|Rodeo Drive}} that was demolished and replaced in 2017. The distinctive cylindrical structure on the roof of the rear of the building is based on the rear of the {{WP|Lladró}} building which used to be located at 408 N. Rodeo Drive. The building is currently occupied by a {{WP|Chanel}} branch, and the back structure no longer exists.
