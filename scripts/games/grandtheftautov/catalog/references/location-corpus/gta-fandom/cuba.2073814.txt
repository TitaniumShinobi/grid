[[LEAD]]
The '''Republic of Cuba''', simply known as '''Cuba''', is an island country in the [[Caribbean Sea]], situated south of [[State of Florida|Florida]], [[United States of America|USA]], east of [[Mexico]], north of [[Jamaica]] and west of [[Haiti]]. It is a single-party state governed by the Communist Party of Cuba (CPC).
