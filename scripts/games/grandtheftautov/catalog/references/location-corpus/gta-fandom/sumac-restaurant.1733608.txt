'''Sumac Restaurant''' is a restaurant in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located next to [[Pescado Rojo]] and [[Perrera Beach Motel]] on [[Bay City Avenue]] in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. 

Based on Ivy at the Shore located on {{WP|Ocean Avenue (Santa Monica)|Ocean Avenue, Santa Monica}}, the restaurant is distinguishable by its blinds, staircase and veranda.
