[[LEAD]]
'''Music Locker Radio''' (also simply referred to as '''The Music Locker''' and '''MLR''') is a [[Radio Stations in GTA Online|radio station]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' added just after [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] update, on December 22, [[2020]]. It plays House, disco and techno music.
