[[LEAD]]
'''0112 South Rockford Drive''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[South Rockford Drive]] in [[Vespucci Canals]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' and is currently the cheapest available safe house in Los Santos.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]80,000 and includes a 2-car garage.
This apartment is in a very good location, Being close to Vespucci Helipad, Ginger Street Store, Ammu-Nation and 2057 Vespucci Boulevard.
