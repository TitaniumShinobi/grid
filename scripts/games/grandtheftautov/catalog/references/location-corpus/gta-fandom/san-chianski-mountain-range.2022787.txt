[[LEAD]]
The '''San Chianski Mountain Range''' is a mountain range appearing in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located on the eastern coast of [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. It is bordered by the [[Grand Senora Desert]] to the west and the [[Pacific Ocean]] to the east.
