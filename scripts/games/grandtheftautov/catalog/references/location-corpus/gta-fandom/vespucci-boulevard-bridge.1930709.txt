'''Vespucci Boulevard Bridge''' is a road bridge in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in [[La Mesa]]. The bridge crosses the [[Los Santos River]] and connects [[Mission Row]] to La Mesa.
