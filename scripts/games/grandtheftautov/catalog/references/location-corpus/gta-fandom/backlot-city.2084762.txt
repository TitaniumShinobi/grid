[[LEAD]]
'''Backlot City''', simply referred to as '''Richards Majestic''' on the map, '''Richards Majestic Studio''' on the police dispatch, and '''Vinewood Studios''' in the [[Heads-Up Display|HUD]] in the mission [[Last Play - Go Figure]], is a neighborhood in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], in {{V}} and {{O}}. The entire neighborhood is gated and consists of two businesses: [[Richards Majestic Productions]] and [[De Santa (company)|De Santa]].
