[[LEAD]]
'''Grapeseed''' is a small rural town located in [[Blaine County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. Grapeseed is situated north of [[Sandy Shores]], southwest of [[Mount Gordo]], west of the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] and sits on the coastline of the [[Alamo Sea]] and the southeastern foot of [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]]. Its slogan is "The Industrial Heart of the Alamo Sea".
