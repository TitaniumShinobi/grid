The '''Raton Canyon Bridge''', also erroneously known as the '''Calafia Bridge''', is a train bridge in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in the [[Raton Canyon]] National Park, [[Blaine County]].
