[[LEAD]]
'''Dollar Pills''' is a pharmacy, grocery and drug store chain featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It also offers a 24-hour photo service. The player can buy stocks from them at the [[Lcn-exchange.com]] website. Most likely Dollar Pills is selling medicaments at low prices.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
*The [[Harmony]] and [[Strawberry]] branches are renditions of a former Rite Aid branch located at {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/YpCoAb7sSPt9z1KKA|3566 Rodeo Place}} in the {{WP|Crenshaw, Los Angeles|Crenshaw District}} of {{WP|Los Angeles}}, {{WP|California}}, which has since been demolished.
