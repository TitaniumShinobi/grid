[[LEAD]]
'''Textile City''' is a small commerce neighborhood in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located in [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]]. It borders [[Alta]] to the north, [[Mission Row]] to the south, [[Pillbox Hill]] to the west and [[La Mesa]] to the east.
