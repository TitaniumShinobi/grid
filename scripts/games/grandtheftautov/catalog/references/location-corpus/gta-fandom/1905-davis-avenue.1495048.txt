'''1905 Davis Avenue''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located on the northern end of the [[Mosley Auto Service]] building in [[Strawberry]], with the garage entrance facing [[Carson Avenue]].

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]75,000 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
