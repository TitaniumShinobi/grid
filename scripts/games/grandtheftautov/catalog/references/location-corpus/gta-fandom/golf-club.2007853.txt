[[LEAD]]
The '''Golf Club''' is a weapon and a [[Golf|golfing]] equipment appeared in {{VC}}, {{SA}}, {{VCS}}, {{V}} and {{O}}. It also appeared, although not as a weapon, in {{TBOGT}}.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]


[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*[[Leaf Links (3D Universe)|Leaf Links]], [[Vice City Beach]] {{nd}} Behind the golf club clubhouse.
*Entering a [[Caddy]] also gives the player a golf club if the melee weapon slot is free.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: San Andreas'']]
[[File:GolfClub-LocationsMap-GTASA.jpg|thumb|Golf Club's locations map]]
*[[Mulholland]], [[Los Santos (3D Universe)|Los Santos]] {{nd}} At the base of the broadcasting tower behind the [[Vinewood Sign (3D Universe)|Vinewood Sign]].
*[[Angel Pine]], [[Whetstone]] {{nd}} In a garage on the southwest side of Angel Pine.
*[[Avispa Country Club]], [[San Fierro (3D Universe)|San Fierro]] {{nd}} Beside the entrance to the Avispa Country Club building.
*[[Yellow Bell Golf Course]], [[Las Venturas (3D Universe)|Las Venturas]] {{nd}} On top of the overhang behind the main clubhouse.
*Angering NPC drivers, especially [[Taxi Drivers]], by ramming their car can cause them to attack the player with a golf club.
*Entering the [[Caddy]] will automatically give the player a golf club.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City Stories'']]
*Found at various areas of Leaf Links, Vice Beach:
**West side of the north part of Leaf Links Golf Club.
**Driving range at the north part of the golf course.
**Northwest of a path loop connecting the north and central parts of the golf course.
**South of the bridge connecting the golf course to [[Vice Point]].
**By a tree in the middle part of the south island of the golf course.
*Entering a [[Caddy]] also gives the player a golf club if the melee weapon slot is free.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto V'']]
*[[Los Santos Country Club|Los Santos Golf Club]], [[Richman (HD Universe)|Richman]] {{nd}} The player is granted one after playing a round of golf. It can also be gained by going around back and scaring a golfer to make them drop the weapon.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto Online'']]
*Los Santos Golf Club - Can be acquired by scaring or killing any golfer to make them drop their club.
*Can be found in player-created content, such as [[Deathmatch]]es, [[Capture]]s and [[Last Team Standing|LTS]].
*Can be found in a shack on an island in [[North Chumash]].
* Acquired for the mission [[On Course]] and kept afterward.
