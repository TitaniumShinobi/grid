[[LEAD]]
The '''Palomino Freeway''' ('''[[Route 15]]''') is a freeway located in [[Los Santos County]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:Exit 1 / [[Sustancia Road]]]]
Exit 1 takes you through the middle of the Palomino Highlands past the [[NOOSE Headquarters|S.A. State Office]] and into the [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos.]] The first signs for Exit 1 can be seen 300 Meters before the exiting then a final sign 100m before the exiting. Exit 1 has a Ramp Speed of 55mp/h (88&nbsp;km/h)

[[SECTION:3:Exit 2 / [[Senora Way]] ([[Interstate 1]] North)]]
Exit 2 takes you down Senora way past the [[Palmer-Taylor Power Station]], [[RON Alternates Wind Farm]] and [[Davis Quartz|Davis Quartz Quarry]] into Sandy Shores. Strangely there are no signs for Senora Way on the Palomino Freeway heading North. Exit 2 has a Ramp Speed of 55mp/h (88&nbsp;km/h)
