[[LEAD]]
'''Eclipse Laundry''' is a closed laundry store in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located on [[Eclipse Boulevard]], [[West Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. It currently has a [[Wolfs International Realty]] 'For Rent' sign in the window.

The store is not enterable and plays no role in the storyline.
