[[LEAD]]
'''Mount Haan Drive''' is a two-way [[List of Street Names|road]] in [[Vinewood Hills]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in {{V}} and {{O}}. The road begins at [[Marlowe Drive]] and ends at the summit of [[Mount Haan]], where the [[Vinewood Sign (HD Universe)|Vinewood Sign]] is located.
