[[LEAD]]
'''Lunar New Year Stunt Races''' are a limited-time series of [[Stunt Races]] featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of the [[GTA Online: Agents of Sabotage|Agents of Sabotage]] update, released on January 23, [[2025]], during the [[Year of the Snake Week]] and returned during the [[Itali Classic Week]] with one new race, released on February 12, [[2026]].
