[[LEAD]]
The '''State of San Andreas''', also known as the '''San Andreas Republic''', is a state located in the southwestern [[United States of America]]; the southern half of the state is featured in {{V}} and {{O}}. San Andreas is closely based on real-life {{WP|California}}. When compared to its 3D Universe [[State of San Andreas (3D Universe)|predecessor]], ''San Andreas'' is much more accurate in terms of geography and size.

[[SECTION:2:Geography]]
The entire state, like its real-life counterpart, is geographically diverse, with nearly every biome. The metropolis of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] is located at the very south of the state, separated from the [[Grand Senora Desert]] by the [[Vinewood Hills]], where the famous [[Vinewood Sign (HD Universe)|Vinewood Sign]] is located. The [[Alamo Sea]], a salty lake based on real-life Salton Sea, along with the aforementioned desert and the towns of [[Sandy Shores]] and [[Grapeseed]] is in a wide basin surrounded by the massive [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]], the highest point of the state, in the north, [[Mount Josiah]] to the west, the [[San Chianski Mountain Range]] and [[Mount Gordo]] to the east, and the Vinewood Hills to the south. Across Mount Chiliad, the town of [[Paleto Bay]] and its surrounding forest is connected to the rest of the state by the [[Great Ocean Highway]], Mount Chiliad Tunnel and [[Braddock Pass]] between Mount Chiliad and Mount Gordo. The [[Fort Zancudo]] U.S. Army Base is located at the foot of Mount Josiah, beside the [[Zancudo River]], one of two natural outlets of the Alamo Sea, the other being [[Cassidy Creek]], which cuts through [[Raton Canyon]]. Natural beaches border the west of the island, stretching from the Los Santos district of [[Vespucci]] to Paleto Bay, while the east coast has high cliffs and mountains.

The aviation infrastructure of Southern San Andreas includes [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|Los Santos International Airport]] (LSIA), [[Fort Zancudo]] (for military use), the [[Sandy Shores Airfield]] (owned by one of the protagonists Trevor Philips), and [[McKenzie Field]] in Grapeseed (used for air trafficking operations). The [[Port of Los Santos]] is a major seaport where cargo ships can be found.

As depicted in ''Grand Theft Auto Online'', every year around the {{WP|Christmas and holiday season|holiday season}}, Southern San Andreas gets covered in snow for a week or two – a phenomenon that is rare for its real-life counterpart.

[[SECTION:2:Counties]]
San Andreas has four known counties: Los Santos County, Blaine County, Red County and Flint County. However, the latter two do not appear in the game.

[[SECTION:3:Los Santos County]]
{{Main|Los Santos County}}
'''Los Santos County''' is the southernmost county in the state, and also the most developed in the south state. It consists of the cities of [[Los Santos (HD universe)|Los Santos]], [[Del Perro]], [[Rockford Hills]] and [[Davis]], along with a large variety of highlands, beaches, and interconnected [[Numbered Highway in San Andreas|highways]]. The town of [[Chumash]] is located in the western side of the county along with a large beach. It is bordered by Blaine County to the north by [[Route 68]]. Los Santos County is the urban area of Southern San Andreas.

[[SECTION:3:Blaine County]]
{{Main|Blaine County}}
'''Blaine County''' is the second-southernmost county in the state, and also the northernmost in the south state. Blaine County is mostly known for its wildlife, including a large desert, highlands, a sea, and forests. There are three towns in the county: [[Sandy Shores]], [[Grapeseed]], and [[Paleto Bay]]. One of the most visited landmarks in the county is [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]]. Blaine County is the rural area of Southern San Andreas.

[[SECTION:3:Other Mentioned Counties]]

[[SECTION:2:Towns]]
The state features four known towns:

[[SECTION:3:Sandy Shores]]
[[File:Ajmsandyshoresmainave.jpg|thumb|200px|Sandy Shores.]]
{{Main|Sandy Shores}}

'''Sandy Shores''' is the most notable due to this being the home of one of the protagonists, [[Trevor Philips]]. The town is located in the center of the [[Grand Senora Desert]], bordering the [[Alamo Sea]] to the north.

[[SECTION:3:Chumash]]
[[File:Pacific_Bluffs.jpg|thumb|200px|Chumash.]]
{{Main|Chumash}}

'''Chumash''' is a town mostly based on {{WP|Malibu, California}}. It is supposedly populated by hipsters, as stated by [[Dave Norton]]. There are many beachfront homes and some docks. It is bordered by the [[Great Ocean Highway]].

[[SECTION:3:Paleto Bay]]
[[File:PaletoBay-GTAV-Aerial.jpg|thumb|200px|Paleto Bay.]]
{{Main|Paleto Bay}}

'''Paleto Bay''' is the northernmost town in southern San Andreas. Paleto Bay is known for being a visited landmark for people who like BASE jumping from [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]]. Paleto Bay is a good rest stop for truckers on the [[Great Ocean Highway]] due to it holding various services such as a bank and a [[Filling Station|gas station]].

[[SECTION:3:Grapeseed]]
[[File:Grapeseed-GTAV-LookingEast.jpg|thumb|200px|Grapeseed.]]
{{Main|Grapeseed}}

'''Grapeseed''' is a town located at the northeast end of the [[Alamo Sea]]. The town is primarily an agricultural one and contains several farms. It is the second smallest town in the state, just ahead of Sandy Shores.

[[SECTION:2:Highway System]]
{{Main|Numbered Highways in GTA V}}
[[File:Route_68.jpg|thumb|The famous Route 68.]]
San Andreas has a complex highway system that contains many elaborate routes, interstates, and freeways. The system reaches all throughout the state and is the most developed highway system in the ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' series to date. [[San Andreas Highway Patrol]] officers can be found on the highways north of [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]].
