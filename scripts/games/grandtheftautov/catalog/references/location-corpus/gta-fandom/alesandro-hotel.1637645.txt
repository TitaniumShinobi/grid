[[LEAD]]
The '''Alesandro Hotel''' is a hotel in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is located on [[Strawberry Avenue]] in [[Textile City]], [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]]. There is a Pharmacy and a Nail Bar & Spa incorporated to the hotel. It cannot be entered and plays no role in the storyline whatsoever.
