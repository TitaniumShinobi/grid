[[LEAD]]
'''Mount Josiah''' is one of three mountains in [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', the others being [[Mount Chiliad (HD Universe)|Mount Chiliad]] and [[Mount Gordo]]. It is located on the west side of San Andreas, just north of [[Fort Zancudo]] and west of the [[Alamo Sea]].
