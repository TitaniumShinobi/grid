'''Sandy Shores Airfield''' is a private airfield located on [[Panorama Drive]], southwest of the namesake town of [[Sandy Shores]], [[Blaine County]], in the middle of the [[Grand Senora Desert]]. The airfield appears in {{V}} and {{O}}.
