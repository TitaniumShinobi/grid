'''Casa Cristina''' is an apartment complex featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is located on the corner of [[Carson Avenue]] and [[Forum Drive]] in [[Strawberry]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] next to [[The Tahitian]]. Casa Cristina is loosely based on August Terrace Apartment located at August St, {{WP|Los Angeles}}.
