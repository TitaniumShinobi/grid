'''4531 Dry Dock Street''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located on [[Dry Dock Street]], adjacent to [[Nicholl's Liquor]] and [[Kennedy Sneaker Co.]] .

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]67,500 and can store 6 [[Personal Vehicles]] and two [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycles]].
