'''El Rancho Boulevard''' is a street in [[Murrieta Heights]], [[Rancho]], [[El Burro Heights]] and [[Cypress Flats]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] and [[Palomino Highlands]] in {{V}} and {{O}}.

It begins at the intersection of [[Dutch London Street]] and [[Carson Avenue]] and ends at [[Vespucci Boulevard]].
