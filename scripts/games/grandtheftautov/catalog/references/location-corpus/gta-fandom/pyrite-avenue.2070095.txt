[[LEAD]]
'''Pyrite Avenue''' is a two-way street in [[Paleto Bay]], [[Blaine County]], that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}. The street runs from [[Paleto Boulevard]] in the north to the [[Route 1|Great Ocean Highway]] in the south.
