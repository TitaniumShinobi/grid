[[LEAD]]
'''West Vinewood''' is a middle to upper class neighbourhood in [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], featured in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is bordered by [[Rockford Hills]] to the west, [[Burton]] to the south, [[Vinewood Hills]] to the north, and [[Downtown Vinewood]] and [[Hawick]] to the east.
