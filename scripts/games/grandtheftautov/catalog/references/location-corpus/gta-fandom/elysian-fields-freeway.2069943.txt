'''Elysian Fields Freeway''' ('''U.S. Route 20'''), is a freeway located in [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. It is part of the in-game [[Numbered Highways in GTA V|highway system]].

[[SECTION:2:Route Description]]
The Elysian Fields Freeway is an east-west highway in southern Los Santos that serves as a freeway bypass of [[San Andreas State Route 22]] through the [[Port of Los Santos]]. Its carried by [[Elysian Freeway Bridge]] and is a major connector between [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]] and [[Los Santos International Airport (HD Universe)|LSIA]].

From the west, The four-lane elevated freeway continues from the [[La Puerta Freeway]] at an off-ramp into the [[Port of Los Santos|Port]]. As soon as it touches ground after the Desmond Bridge, it hooks north along the [[Murrieta Oil Field]], and crosses [[El Rancho Boulevard]] at the intersection of San Andreas state routes [[San Andreas State Route 17|17]] and [[San Andreas State Route 18|18]]. It becomes a proper freeway at this point (widening up to six or eight lanes) as it reaches the the intersection of the [[Interstate 4]]/[[U.S. Route 15]]. From there it continues as the [[Del Perro Freeway]] (Interstate 2).
