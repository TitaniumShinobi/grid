[[LEAD]]
'''Scotland''' is a constituent country that is part of the [[United Kingdom]].

Owing to the series' Scottish origins, many references are made to the country through the games. The city of {{WP|Dundee}}, the {{S}}' birthplace, is known as the gaming capital of Scotland, and is referenced multiple times throughout the series.

Scotland is currently home to two [[Rockstar Games]] studios:
*[[Rockstar North]]
*[[Rockstar Dundee]]

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*[[Love Fist (3D Universe)|Love Fist]] are a Scottish metal band.
*Billboards advertising Dundee vacations are found at the [[Escobar International Airport]].
*"{{WP|Japanese Boy}}" by Scottish singer {{WP|Aneka}} is played on [[Flash FM (3D Universe)|Flash FM]].
*DEEPFRIEDMARSBARS is a cheat code in the [[Personal Computer|PC]] release of the game that makes [[Tommy Vercetti]] appear overweight, referencing the real-life {{WP|deep-fried Mars bar}}, a controversial Scottish dish popularized in the mid-1990s and criticized for its unhealthiness.
*The [[Leaf Links (3D Universe)|Leaf Links]] name appears to be a reference to the {{WP|Leith Links}} park in {{WP|Edinburgh}}, Scotland, which is one of the earliest known places where golf was played.
*The name of [[Wave 103]] is a reference to {{WP|Wave FM|Wave 102}}, a former radio station in {{WP|Dundee}}, Scotland.
