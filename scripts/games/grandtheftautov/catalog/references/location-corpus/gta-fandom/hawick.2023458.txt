[[LEAD]]
'''Hawick''' is a neighborhood located in [[Vinewood (HD Universe)|Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]] in {{V}} and {{O}}. It is bordered by [[Rockford Plaza]], [[Burton]] and [[West Vinewood]] to the west, [[Alta]] to the south, [[East Vinewood]] to the east, and the rest of Vinewood to the north.
