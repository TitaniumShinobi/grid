'''Facade''' (Stylized as '''FACADE''', pronounced "fasad") is a technology corporation appearing in {{IV}}, {{EFLC}}, {{V}}, {{O}}, and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto IV'' and ''Episodes from Liberty City'']]
In {{IV}}, two of the company's products, "[[Wing It]]" presentation software and Babybuilder 1.0, are advertised on the in-game [[Radio Stations in GTA IV|radio]] and on billboards.
