[[LEAD]]
'''634 Boulevard Del Perro''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located behind the [[Noodle Exchange]] restaurant on [[Boulevard Del Perro]] in [[Rockford Hills]], and is accessed by an alley adjacent to the restaurant.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]33,500 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
