[[LEAD]]
'''Morningwood''' is a neighborhood in northern [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]], in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It is bordered by [[Richman (HD Universe)|Richman]] to the north, [[Backlot City]] to the south, [[Del Perro]] to the west, and [[Rockford Hills]] to the east.
