[[LEAD]]
'''2000 Great Ocean Highway''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in the parking lot of [[Hookies]] in [[North Chumash]], as part of a longer building of individual garages.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]31,500 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
