[[LEAD]]
'''Demonoil''' is an engine lubricant brand that appears in {{O}} as part of the [[GTA Online: Arena War|Arena War]] update, and due to appear in {{VI}}.

[[SECTION:3:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City – The Definitive Edition'']]
{{Version|section=y|game=VC|version=de}}
Demonoil later makes a brief appearance in {{TDE|VC}} on a model calendar in the [[Print Works]] featuring its branding, replacing the [[Pervelli Calendar]] brand from the original. As with other {{HD}} [[Grove Street Games/Errors/BrandsTable|brand appearances]] in ''The Definitive Edition'' trilogy, this appearance is considered [[Canon|non-canon]] to the {{3D}}.
