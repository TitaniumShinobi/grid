'''Ace Liquor''' (also known as '''Liquor Ace''') is a small, run-down liquor store that appears in {{V}} and {{O}}.

[[SECTION:3:The Diamond Casino & Resort]]
In [[2019]], the lab was used by a heist crew to plan a heist on [[The Diamond Casino & Resort]]. After [[Best Laid Plans|tracking down]] the heist crew members, the protagonist finds the heist planning room and kills the heist crew members.
