The '''{{WP|Caribbean Sea}}''' is an {{WP|Americas|American}} mediterranean sea of the [[Atlantic Ocean (HD Universe)|Atlantic Ocean]] that is mentioned in ''[[Grand Theft Auto: Vice City]]'' and appears in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' as part of [[GTA Online: The Cayo Perico Heist|The Cayo Perico Heist]] update.

The entire area of the Caribbean Sea, the numerous islands of the West Indies, and adjacent coasts, are collectively known as the [[Caribbean]].
