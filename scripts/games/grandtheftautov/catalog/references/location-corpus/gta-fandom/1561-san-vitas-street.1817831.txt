'''1561 San Vitas St, Apt 2''' is a [[Safehouses in GTA Online|safehouse]] located on [[San Vitus Boulevard]] in [[West Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]], featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]99,000 and includes a 2-car garage.

If the player has purchased the [[Criminal Enterprise Starter Pack]], this apartment will be available for FREE.
