[[LEAD]]
'''0754 Roy Lowenstein Boulevard''' is a [[Garages in GTA Online|purchasable garage]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:2:Property Information]]
The garage is located in an electrical substation on [[Roy Lowenstein Boulevard]] in [[Rancho]], across the street from the [[Hi-Men]] cocktail bar.

It is available for purchase through [[Dynasty8realestate.com]] for [[Money|$]]29,500 and can store 2 [[Personal Vehicles]] and one [[Pedal and Metal Cycles|Bicycle]].
