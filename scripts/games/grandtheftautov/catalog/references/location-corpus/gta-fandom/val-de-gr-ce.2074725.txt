'''Val-de-Grâce''' is a [[France|French]] high-class clothing brand featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]
*The building on Portola Drive is based on {{URL|https://maps.app.goo.gl/n3EgKyyzxKB7nSp2A|401 North Rodeo Drive}} in Beverly Hills, California. The store closely resembles how it looked when occupied by a {{WP|De Beers}} store before 2014.
