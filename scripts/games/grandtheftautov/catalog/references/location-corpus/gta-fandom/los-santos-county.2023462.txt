[[LEAD]]
'''Los Santos County''', officially the '''County of Los Santos''', is a county featured in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'', located in southern [[State of San Andreas (HD Universe)|San Andreas]]. It is bordered by [[Blaine County]] to the north by [[Route 68]].
