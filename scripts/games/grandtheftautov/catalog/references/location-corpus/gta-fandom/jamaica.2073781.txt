[[LEAD]]
'''Jamaica''' is an island nation located in the [[Caribbean Sea]]. It lies to the south of [[Cuba]] and west of Hispaniola.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto III'', ''Advance'' and'' Liberty City Stories'']]
*The [[Uptown Yardies]], led by [[King Courtney]], are a Jamaican gang.
*[[K-JAH]], a radio station in ''[[Grand Theft Auto III]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto: Liberty City Stories]]'', plays reggae music, which originated in Jamaica.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto: Vice City'']]
*[[Jeremy Robard]] says he has an office in Jamaica.
