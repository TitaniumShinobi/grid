The '''Del Perro Plaza''' is a shopping plaza in {{V}} and {{O}} located on [[Boulevard Del Perro]] and [[Prosperity Street]] in [[Del Perro]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]]. None of the shops are accessible, but there are usable [[Sprunk (HD Universe)|Sprunk]] and [[eCola (HD Universe)|eCola]] [[Vending Machines|vending machines]] inside the mall. 

One of the [[deathmatch]]es in ''GTA Online'' takes place here.
