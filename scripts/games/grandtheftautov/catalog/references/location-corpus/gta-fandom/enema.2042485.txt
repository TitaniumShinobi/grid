[[LEAD]]
'''Enema''' (also known as '''Enema Los Santos''') is a high-end fashion brand appearing in ''[[Grand Theft Auto IV]]'', ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''.

[[SECTION:3:Locations]]


[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto IV'']]
*The Enema store on [[Manzano Road]] in [[Leftwood]], [[State of Alderney|Alderney]], is seemingly a rendition of {{URL|https://www.propertyshark.com/mason/Property/1910903/3-W-Railroad-Ave-Tenafly-NJ-07670/|3 West Railroad Avenue}} in {{WP|Tenafly, New Jersey}}.

[[SECTION:4:''Grand Theft Auto V'' and ''Online]]
*The branch on Rockford Drive is modeled after a {{WP|Dolce & Gabbana}} location at 312 North {{WP|Rodeo Drive}}. Dolce & Gabbana is primarily represented in game as [[Gutter & Blood]], which occupies a store next door, also on Rockford Drive. This specific in-game Gutter & Blood store is modeled after a nearby Prada location at 343 North Rodeo Drive, with the swapped positions of these brands in relation to their real-life counterparts likely being intentional.
