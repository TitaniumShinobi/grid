'''Appropriate Associates''' is a company in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' that specializes in sending people to parties upon request.

Their in-game [[Websites in GTA V|website]] can be found at '''www.appropriateassociates.com'''. An advertisement can also be heard for them on [[Los Santos Rock Radio]].
