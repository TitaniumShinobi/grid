'''Santo Capra''' (also known as '''Santo Capro''' in ''[[The Ballad of Gay Tony]]'') is a high-end clothing company appearing in the [[HD Universe]] of the ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' series.

[[SECTION:2:Locations]]
The single store in Rockford Hills has entry points from Little Portola and Rockford Drive with identical facades.
