'''$1 Saver''' is a dollar store in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]'' located in [[Eclipse Boulevard]], [[West Vinewood]], [[Los Santos (HD Universe)|Los Santos]].

The store is not enterable and plays no role in the storyline.
