'''Japan''' (Japanese: 日本国) is an island country in {{WP|East Asia}}, located in the {{WP|Pacific Ocean}} off the northeastern coast of the Asian mainland. 

The country uses the [[CERO]] rating system to rate all ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' releases, with all games in the series so far being rated Z (18+). Japan receives ''Grand Theft Auto'' releases at a much later time, as it has taken a long time for [[Rockstar Games]] to localize them. Japanese releases of ''Grand Theft Auto'' games do not feature dialogue re-dubbed in the Japanese language and instead feature English voice acting and Japanese subtitles.
