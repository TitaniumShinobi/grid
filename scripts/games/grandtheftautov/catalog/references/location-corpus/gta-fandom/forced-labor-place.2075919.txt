[[LEAD]]
'''Forced Labor Place''' is a road in [[La Mesa]], [[East Los Santos (HD Universe)|East Los Santos]] in ''[[Grand Theft Auto V]]'' and ''[[Grand Theft Auto Online]]''. It begins at the [[Capital Boulevard]], passes through the [[Fridgit|Fridgit Ice & Storage]] and ends at [[Innocence Boulevard]] and [[Orchardville Avenue]].
