[[SECTION:2:Office Garages and Custom Auto Shop]]
{{Main|Office Garages}}
With the addition of the Import/Export update, players can now buy 1 to 3 garage expansions within the same building that they can customize and provide space to store up to 60 vehicles.

Once a garage is added, the player can also add a custom auto shop with a dedicated [[Office Mechanic]] within the building which operates as a combined [[Los Santos Customs]] and [[Benny's Original Motor Works]] facility allowing full customization of the players vehicles.
