[[LEAD]]
'''Interstate 2''', also known as '''I-2''' and most of the '''Del Perro Freeway''' is a freeway in {{V}} and {{O}} that runs through [[Downtown Los Santos (HD Universe)|Downtown Los Santos]] heading east. Its western terminus is at a merging off the [[Great Ocean Highway]] ([[U.S. Route 1]]) in Del Perro and goes east throughout the northern-central section of Los Santos before it later turns southwards and ends at the intersection of the [[Interstate 4]]/[[U.S. Route 15]], where it continues as [[U.S. Route 20]].

The highway never serves less than six lanes in total and is one of the largest highways in the ''[[Grand Theft Auto]]'' series by max lanes. The highway is eight lanes without lane merges at its greatest.
