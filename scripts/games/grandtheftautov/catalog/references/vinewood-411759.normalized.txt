Vinewood in GTA V

iframe†www.googletagmanager.com The Vinewood Sign in the first trailer for Grand Theft Auto V .
Vinewood is a district in Los Santos appearing in Grand Theft Auto V and Grand Theft Auto Online . The area is famous for being the centre of the American film industry with a number of large scale premieres, such as the film Meltdown , and contains the Vinewood Walk of Fame and the Vinewood Sign . West Vinewood , East Vinewood , Vinewood Hills , and Downtown Vinewood are the neighbourhoods that comprise of the larger Vinewood district.
So Vinewood is one large neighbourhood with a few small neighbourhoods in it.
