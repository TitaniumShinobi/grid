[East Los Santos]
East Los Santos is a geographic sector located in eastern Los Santos, mainly east of the Los Santos River, appearing in Grand Theft Auto V and Grand Theft Auto Online. The district is heavily influenced by three Hispanic street gangs: the Los Santos Vagos, the Los Varrios Aztecas and the Marabunta Grande. The district has a number of houses and businesses, which are mainly run-down, and is described as being dangerous even in the daytime for tourists.

[Neighbourhoods]
Cypress Flats, East Vinewood, El Burro Heights, La Mesa, Mirror Park, Murrieta Heights, Murrieta Oil Field
