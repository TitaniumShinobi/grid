## Superseded-statement record

The following statement families are explicitly superseded when they appear in historical roadmaps, old screenshots, tests, or source comments:

1. “Home is the permanent main product surface.”
2. “Games/workstation are main-panel tabs or equivalent permanent destinations.”
3. “History and Settings are the only global destinations.”
4. “The assistant is globally docked on Home and every workspace.”
5. “Library/profile management is a global main navigation destination.”
6. “Selecting a game navigates away from a separate workstation instead of rendering that game’s mod/plugin workspace.”
7. “Repository executors imply Production/assistant authority.”
8. “User prose can supply structured technical target selections.”
9. “AI can diagnose, repair, authorize, execute, or verify.”

These may be referenced as historical implementation facts only when clearly labeled **Dormant prototype** or **Superseded**.
